# Eight proposals

# solution: single_solution (cluster 0)
## proposal_id: P1
## solution_name: EV Charging Stations
## key_phrases: charging | electric | charging stations | ev charging | ev | stations | electric vehicles | charging infrastructure
## objective: Provide electric vehicle charging infrastructure that supports sustainable transportation and can be scheduled for efficient use.
## description: Install electric vehicle charging stations at industrial sites, building parking lots, ports, and related facilities to support EV adoption and reduce dependence on fossil fuels. The charging network may be powered by renewable energy sources and can include smart scheduling features that adjust charging times and energy use based on demand, occupancy, weather, traffic, and renewable generation.

# solution: single_solution (cluster 1)
## proposal_id: P2
## solution_name: Building Integrated Photovoltaic Systems
## key_phrases: building | bipv | building integrated | building integrated photovoltaic | integrated photovoltaic | building materials | materials | integrated
## objective: Integrate photovoltaic generation into building materials so buildings can produce electricity on site.
## description: Combine solar panels with building materials such as roof tiles, facades, windows, or cladding to create energy-generating structures for residential and commercial buildings. This approach generates on-site electricity, can support building power needs, and is described as a way to improve energy self-sufficiency, reduce reliance on additional infrastructure, and contribute to visual and structural design options.

# solution: single_solution (cluster 5)
## proposal_id: P6
## solution_name: Solar PV Tracking System
## key_phrases: tracking | solar | pv tracking | solar PV tracking | orientation | pv | solar PV | tracking optimizes
## objective: Optimize solar panel orientation and tracking to increase energy generation.
## description: Use tracking technology to adjust the orientation, positioning, and tilt of solar photovoltaic panels based on the sun’s movement, weather patterns, and seasonal changes. # solution: single_solution (cluster 6)

## key_phrases: hybrid | storage | battery | solar | energy | battery storage | power | renewable
## objective: Combine renewable generation with storage or other power sources to provide stable and reliable electricity supply.

# solution: single_solution (cluster 7)
## proposal_id: P8
## solution_name: Solar Power for Ports
## key_phrases: solar | ports | power | solar power | power ports | operations | ports harnessing solar | ports harnessing
## objective: Generate clean electricity from solar power for port facilities and operations.
## description: Install solar photovoltaic systems, including rooftop, infrastructure-mounted, and floating solar panels, to power port operations and related facilities such as lighting, cargo handling, cranes, communications, security, and charging. The solution is described as a way to reduce dependence on fossil fuels and traditional grid supply while supporting cleaner port operations and lower emissions.
