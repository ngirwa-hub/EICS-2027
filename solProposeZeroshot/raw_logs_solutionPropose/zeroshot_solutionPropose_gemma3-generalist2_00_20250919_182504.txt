Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project's goals, and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer: Enables power delivery without physical cables, useful for powering equipment in ports (cranes, automated guided vehicles) or within industrial facilities.
2. Modular DC Power Distribution Units: These units offer a scalable and flexible way to distribute DC power within buildings or data centers, allowing for easy adjustments based on changing power demands.
3. DC-Native UPS Systems: Provides uninterruptible power supply functionality directly in DC, eliminating AC conversion losses and improving efficiency in critical applications like data centers and industrial processes.
4. DC Microgrid Management Software: A software platform to optimize the operation and control of DC microgrids, balancing generation, storage, and load within ports, industry, and buildings.
5. DC-Enabled Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure: Supports faster and more efficient charging of electric vehicles directly from DC microgrids in ports and buildings, minimizing energy losses.
