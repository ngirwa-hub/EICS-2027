Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and were not included in the initial list:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, similar to AC PDUs but optimized for DC voltage levels and offering advanced monitoring capabilities.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion for lighting, increasing efficiency and simplifying integration with DC microgrids, particularly beneficial for large buildings and industrial facilities.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure: Rapid DC charging stations for electric vehicles are vital for ports and industrial areas, requiring robust and efficient DC power delivery systems.
4. DC-Native Motor Drives: Replacing traditional AC motor drives with DC-native drives reduces conversion losses and improves efficiency in industrial processes and port equipment.
5. DC-Based HVAC Systems: Utilizing DC power for heating, ventilation, and air conditioning can improve overall system efficiency and integration with DC microgrids in buildings and data centers.
