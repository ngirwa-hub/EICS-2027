Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project’s target sectors (ports, industry, data centers, and buildings), beyond the initial list:

1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These specialized PDUs manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering efficient power delivery and monitoring capabilities. They replace traditional AC PDUs, reducing conversion losses.
2. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion for lighting loads, improving energy efficiency and reducing heat generation within buildings and industrial spaces.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure for Ports: Dedicated DC fast charging stations for electric vehicles (trucks, ships, forklifts) in ports, minimizing charging time and maximizing operational efficiency.
4. DC-Coupled Microgrids for Data Centers: Integrating renewable energy sources and energy storage directly into a DC microgrid within data centers enhances resilience and reduces reliance on the main grid.
5. DC-Enabled Industrial Automation Systems: Utilizing DC power for industrial automation equipment, such as robots and PLCs, can simplify power distribution and increase overall system efficiency.
