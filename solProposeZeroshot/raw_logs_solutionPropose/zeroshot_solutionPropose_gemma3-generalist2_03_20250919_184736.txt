Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project’s focus areas (ports, industry, data centers, buildings), beyond the previously listed items:

1. DC-Based Energy Management System: A centralized system for monitoring and controlling DC power distribution, optimizing energy usage and identifying inefficiencies across a facility.
2. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure: Dedicated charging stations utilizing DC power directly for electric vehicles, bypassing AC conversion and enabling faster charging times, particularly useful in ports and industrial areas.
3. DC Power Distribution Units (DC PDUs): Specialized units designed to distribute DC power throughout a building or facility, providing reliable power delivery and circuit protection.
4. Modular DC Power Architectures: Flexible, scalable DC power systems that can be easily adapted to changing power demands in data centers and industrial settings, using standardized DC power blocks.
5. DC-Native Lighting Systems: LED lighting systems designed to operate directly on DC power, reducing energy losses and improving overall efficiency in buildings and ports.
