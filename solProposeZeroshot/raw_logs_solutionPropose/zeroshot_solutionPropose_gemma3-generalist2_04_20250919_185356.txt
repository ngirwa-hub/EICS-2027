Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and were not included in the previously reviewed list:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering intelligent load balancing and remote monitoring capabilities.
2. DC-Based Energy Management Systems (EMS): A system that optimizes DC power usage by dynamically adjusting loads and prioritizing critical functions based on real-time conditions.
3. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC LEDs directly eliminates AC-DC conversion for lighting, increasing efficiency and reducing heat generation within buildings.
4. DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Enables faster and more efficient charging of EVs by eliminating the AC-DC conversion typically required for charging stations.
5. DC-Based Microgrid Controllers: Software and hardware that manage the operation of DC microgrids, ensuring stability, optimizing energy flow, and integrating renewable sources.
