Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project's focus areas (ports, industry, data centers, and buildings), beyond the previously listed items:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, similar to AC PDUs but optimized for DC voltage levels and incorporating smart monitoring capabilities.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion in lighting circuits, improving efficiency and reducing energy waste, particularly beneficial for large buildings and data centers.
3. DC-Compatible Power Supplies: Standardized DC power supplies designed for various industrial and commercial applications, ensuring compatibility and interoperability within DC microgrids.
4. DC-Native Motor Drives: Motors designed to operate directly on DC power, removing the need for AC-DC-AC conversion and increasing efficiency in industrial processes and port equipment.
5. DC-Based Energy Management Systems (EMS): Intelligent systems that monitor and control DC power flows, optimize energy usage, and provide real-time insights for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.
