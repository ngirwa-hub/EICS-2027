Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and weren't included in the initial list:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering intelligent load balancing and remote monitoring capabilities.
2. DC-Based Microgrid Controllers: Software and hardware systems that manage the operation of DC microgrids, optimizing energy usage and ensuring reliable power supply.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles: DC chargers specifically designed for ports and industrial sites to quickly replenish electric vehicle batteries.
4. DC-Native Lighting Systems: LED lighting systems designed to operate directly from DC power, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and improving energy efficiency in buildings.
5. DC-Based Power-over-Ethernet (PoE) Systems: PoE solutions distributing DC power alongside data over Ethernet cables, simplifying power delivery to low-voltage devices in buildings and industrial settings.
