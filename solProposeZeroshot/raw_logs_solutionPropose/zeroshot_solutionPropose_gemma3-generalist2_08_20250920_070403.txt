Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the goals of the Shift2DC project and not repeating the previously listed items:

1.  DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Provides direct DC charging for electric vehicles within ports and industrial facilities, minimizing conversion losses compared to AC charging systems.
2.  DC-Based Lighting Control System: Enables efficient and responsive lighting management within buildings and data centers, directly utilizing DC power and reducing energy waste.
3.  DC-Native Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Replaces traditional AC PDUs with DC equivalents in data centers and industrial settings, optimizing power delivery and reducing conversion losses.
4.  DC-Compatible Modular UPS Systems: Offers uninterruptible power supplies specifically designed for DC microgrids, providing reliable backup power and seamless integration.
5.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) System: Facilitates wireless power delivery for low-voltage devices within buildings or industrial environments, enhancing convenience and flexibility.
