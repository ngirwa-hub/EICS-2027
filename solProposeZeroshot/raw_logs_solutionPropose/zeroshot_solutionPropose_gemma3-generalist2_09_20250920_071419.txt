Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project’s target sectors (ports, industry, data centers, buildings) and weren't previously listed:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Centralized management and distribution of DC power within a facility, offering monitoring, control, and load balancing capabilities.
2. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Directly powering LED lighting with DC voltage, reducing conversion losses and improving overall system efficiency in buildings and industrial settings.
3. DC-Based Motor Drives: Utilizing DC motors and variable frequency drives to enhance efficiency and controllability in industrial applications and port equipment.
4. DC-Enabled Charging Infrastructure: Implementing DC fast charging stations for electric vehicles, particularly crucial in ports and industrial zones.
5. Bi-directional DC Power Sharing: Allowing different DC loads and sources to exchange power dynamically, optimizing resource utilization and resilience across buildings and data centers.
