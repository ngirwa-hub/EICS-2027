Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and were not included in the initial list:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, providing voltage regulation, overload protection, and monitoring capabilities.
2. DC-based Lighting Systems: Replacing traditional AC-based lighting with DC lighting can significantly reduce conversion losses and improve energy efficiency in buildings and industrial facilities.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure: Enables rapid charging of electric vehicles (especially relevant for ports and industrial sites with vehicle fleets) directly from a DC source, avoiding AC-DC conversion at the charging station.
4. DC-Native Power Electronics for Industrial Processes: Specialized power electronics designed for DC loads in industrial applications (e.g., motors, welders) can offer higher efficiency and improved control compared to AC-based alternatives.
5. DC Microgrid Management System: A software platform that optimizes the operation of DC microgrids, balancing power generation, storage, and demand across multiple loads and sources.
