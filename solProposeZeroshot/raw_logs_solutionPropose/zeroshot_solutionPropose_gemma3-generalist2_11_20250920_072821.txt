Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and weren't previously listed:

1.  DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These PDUs manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering greater efficiency and flexibility compared to traditional AC PDUs. They can incorporate intelligent power management features.
2.  DC-Native LED Lighting Systems: Direct powering of LED lighting from a DC source eliminates AC-DC conversion, reducing energy losses and potentially extending LED lifespan.
3.  DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Integrating DC fast charging directly into DC microgrids minimizes conversion steps and can improve grid stability.
4.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) Systems: Facilitates power transmission without cables, useful for charging mobile devices, robots, or other equipment within ports, industry or data centers.
5.  DC-Compatible Modular Power Supplies: Allows for scalable and redundant power delivery within data centers and industrial settings, easily adapting to changing power demands.
