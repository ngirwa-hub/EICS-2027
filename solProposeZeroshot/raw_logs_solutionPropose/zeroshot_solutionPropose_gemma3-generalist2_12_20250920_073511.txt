Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and were not included in the previous list:

1. DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Direct DC charging eliminates AC-DC conversion at the charging station, improving efficiency and potentially reducing charging time for electric vehicles.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC power directly for lighting reduces conversion losses compared to traditional AC-based systems and can be particularly beneficial in large buildings or industrial facilities.
3. DC Power Distribution Units (DC PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a building or facility, providing a centralized point for control and monitoring.
4. DC-Native Microgrid Controllers: Specialized controllers optimized for DC microgrids enhance stability, manage energy flows, and facilitate integration of renewable sources.
5. DC-Based Power-over-Ethernet (PoE) Systems: Expanding PoE to higher power levels using DC simplifies power delivery to devices like sensors and cameras, common in ports and industrial settings.
