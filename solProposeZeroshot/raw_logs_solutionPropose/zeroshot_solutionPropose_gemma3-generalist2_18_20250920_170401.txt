Here are some additional DC solutions relevant to ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project's goals and not repeating previously listed items:

1.  DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Enables direct charging of EVs from DC power sources, reducing AC-DC conversion losses at charging stations and supporting DC microgrids.
2.  DC-Native Industrial Automation Systems: Replacing traditional AC-based automation systems with DC equivalents reduces conversion losses and allows for more efficient integration with DC microgrids in industrial settings.
3.  DC-Based Lighting Control System: Allows for more precise and efficient control of lighting in buildings and other applications, leveraging the inherent advantages of DC for dimming and power management.
4.  DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Provides a standardized and modular way to distribute DC power within buildings, data centers, and industrial facilities, simplifying installation and maintenance.
5.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer System: Facilitates wireless charging of devices and equipment within ports and buildings, offering flexibility and eliminating the need for wired connections.
