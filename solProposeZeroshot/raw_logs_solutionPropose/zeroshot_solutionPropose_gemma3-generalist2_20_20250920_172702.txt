Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project’s focus areas (ports, industry, data centers, and buildings), and weren't previously listed:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering precise control and monitoring capabilities, similar to AC PDUs but optimized for DC systems.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC power directly for LED lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion, increasing efficiency and reducing heat generation within buildings and industrial facilities.
3. DC-Native Motor Drives: Employing DC motors and drives avoids AC-DC conversion for industrial applications, providing smoother operation, improved efficiency, and reduced maintenance.
4. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure: Providing DC fast charging for electric vehicles within ports and industrial sites reduces charging times and minimizes energy losses.
5. DC Microgrid Management System: A software platform that optimizes the operation of DC microgrids, balancing energy supply and demand while ensuring stability and resilience.
