Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project’s goals and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, similar to AC PDUs, but optimized for DC voltage levels and incorporating smart features for monitoring and control.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting eliminates the need for AC-DC conversion in lighting circuits, improving efficiency and potentially enabling more integrated lighting control strategies within buildings and industrial settings.
3. DC-Compatible Motor Drives: Using DC motors and drives directly reduces conversion losses in industrial processes and potentially allows for more flexible and efficient motor control compared to traditional AC systems.
4. DC-Based Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning (HVAC): Direct DC HVAC systems reduce conversion losses and integrate more easily with DC microgrids and renewable energy sources, particularly in data centers with high cooling demands.
5. DC-Enabled Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Direct DC charging stations reduce conversion losses and can be integrated with DC microgrids to support EV charging and grid stabilization.
