Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for the Shift2DC project’s target sectors (ports, industry, data centers, buildings) and weren’t previously listed:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a facility, similar to AC PDUs, but optimized for DC voltage levels and offering features like remote monitoring and control.
2. DC-Native Load Management System: A system to dynamically adjust DC power consumption based on real-time availability and demand, optimizing energy use and preventing overload situations.
3. Modular DC Power Supplies: These are scalable, pre-engineered DC power blocks that can be combined to meet varying power demands in different applications, offering flexibility and ease of deployment.
4. DC-Based Microgrid Controller: A specialized control system that manages the operation of a DC microgrid, coordinating distributed generation, storage, and loads for optimal performance and resilience.
5. DC-Compatible Lighting Systems: Lighting fixtures designed to operate directly on DC power, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and improving overall energy efficiency within buildings.
