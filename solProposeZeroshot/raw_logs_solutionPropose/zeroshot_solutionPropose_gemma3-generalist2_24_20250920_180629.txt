Here are some additional DC solutions relevant to the Shift2DC project’s target sectors (ports, industry, data centers, buildings), not previously listed, and formatted as requested:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, similar to AC PDUs, but optimized for DC voltage levels and load management.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC lighting directly, bypassing AC-DC conversion, increases efficiency and reduces heat generation in buildings and industrial settings.
3. DC-Native Motor Drives: Replacing traditional AC motor drives with DC-native alternatives improves efficiency and responsiveness in industrial applications and port equipment.
4. DC-Coupled Energy Harvesting Systems: Integrating various energy harvesting sources (solar, wind, thermal) directly into a DC system simplifies integration and maximizes energy capture.
5. DC Microgrid Management Software: This software optimizes the operation of DC microgrids, balancing load, managing energy storage, and ensuring system stability.
