Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the goals of the Shift2DC project and not repeating solutions already listed:

1. DC-Based Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Provides direct charging for DC-powered vehicles, reducing conversion losses and enabling faster charging speeds within port and industrial settings.
2. DC Power Distribution Units (DC PDUs): Standardized units that manage and distribute DC power within buildings and data centers, offering improved efficiency and modularity compared to traditional AC systems.
3. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Eliminates AC-DC conversion for lighting, improving efficiency and reducing heat generation in buildings and industrial facilities.
4. DC-Powered Automation Systems: Utilizing DC for industrial automation equipment reduces conversion losses and allows for more efficient integration with DC microgrids.
5. Wireless DC Power Transfer Systems: Enables contactless power delivery for devices and equipment within ports and buildings, enhancing flexibility and reducing cabling needs.
