Here are some additional DC solutions relevant to the Shift2DC project’s focus areas (ports, industry, data centers, buildings) that weren’t previously listed:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a building or facility, offering features like remote monitoring and load balancing. They improve efficiency and offer greater control over DC power usage.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion for lighting loads, resulting in reduced energy losses and potentially longer LED lifespan. This is particularly relevant for data centers and industrial facilities.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure for Ports: Dedicated DC fast charging stations for electric vehicles, specifically designed for port environments with high power demands, minimizes energy conversion losses and speeds up charging times.
4. DC-Native Motor Drives for Industrial Applications: Replacing traditional AC motor drives with DC-native drives reduces conversion losses and can improve motor efficiency, particularly in industrial processes.
5. DC Microgrid Management Software: Software platforms for monitoring, controlling, and optimizing DC microgrid operation, enabling better resource allocation and enhanced resilience across various applications.
