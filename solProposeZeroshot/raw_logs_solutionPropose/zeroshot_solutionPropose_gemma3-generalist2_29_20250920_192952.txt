Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and weren't included in the initial list:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering precise control and monitoring capabilities for various loads.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC lighting directly eliminates AC-DC conversion losses associated with traditional lighting, improving efficiency in buildings and industrial settings.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure: Providing high-power DC charging stations for electric vehicles, particularly crucial for ports and industrial areas with heavy vehicle traffic.
4. DC-Native Motor Drives: Replacing traditional AC motor drives with DC motor drives can reduce conversion losses and improve the overall efficiency of industrial equipment.
5. DC-Coupled Microgrids: Integrating renewable energy sources directly into a DC microgrid, bypassing AC conversion, improves efficiency and enhances resilience for ports, data centers, and buildings.
