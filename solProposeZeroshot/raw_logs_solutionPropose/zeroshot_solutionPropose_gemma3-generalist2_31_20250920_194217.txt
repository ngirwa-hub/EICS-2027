Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project’s focus areas, beyond the previously listed items:

1.  DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These intelligently manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering features like remote monitoring, load balancing, and fault isolation.
2.  DC-Based Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC lighting directly, without AC-DC conversion, can improve efficiency and reduce energy losses in buildings and industrial settings.
3.  DC-Compatible Motor Drives: These drives allow for direct operation of electric motors on DC power, improving efficiency in industrial applications and port equipment.
4.  DC Harmonic Filters: Minimizing harmonic distortion on DC networks is crucial for maintaining power quality and protecting equipment in data centers and industrial environments.
5.  DC-Native Building Energy Management Systems (BEMS): A BEMS designed specifically for DC networks allows for optimized energy usage and integration of renewable sources within buildings.
