Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the goals of the Shift2DC project and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Direct DC charging eliminates AC-DC conversion at the charging station, increasing efficiency and potentially reducing charging costs. This is particularly relevant for ports and industrial facilities with significant EV fleets.
2. DC-Based Lighting Control Systems: Implementing DC lighting systems with integrated control allows for more efficient operation, smart dimming capabilities, and potential integration with building management systems.
3. DC-Native Power Electronics for Industrial Processes: Developing specialized DC power electronics tailored to the specific needs of industrial processes (e.g., welding, electroplating) can improve efficiency and reduce harmonic distortion.
4. DC-Coupled Energy Management System (EMS): An EMS specifically designed for DC microgrids can optimize energy flow, balance load, and manage distributed generation sources within a building or industrial setting.
5. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) for Robotics: Utilizing DC-based WPT can simplify power delivery to robotic systems within ports, data centers, or factories, reducing cabling and increasing operational flexibility.
