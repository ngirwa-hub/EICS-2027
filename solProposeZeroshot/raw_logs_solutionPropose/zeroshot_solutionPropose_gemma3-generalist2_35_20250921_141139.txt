Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the goals of the Shift2DC project and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC-Powered Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure: Provides dedicated DC power for EV charging stations, reducing conversion losses and enabling faster charging speeds within port facilities and industrial parks.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Utilizes DC power directly for lighting, bypassing AC-DC conversion and improving energy efficiency, particularly beneficial for large buildings and data centers.
3. DC-Native Power Management Integrated Circuits (PMICs): Develops specialized chips that efficiently manage and distribute DC power within industrial equipment and building systems, minimizing losses and optimizing performance.
4. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer Systems: Enables contactless power delivery for devices and equipment within ports, data centers, and buildings, increasing flexibility and reducing cabling.
5. Distributed DC-Based Energy Management Systems: Provides real-time monitoring and control of DC power distribution within buildings and industrial facilities, enabling optimization and fault detection.
