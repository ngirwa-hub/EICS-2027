Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project’s goals and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer System: Enables contactless power delivery within facilities, useful for powering equipment in ports or industrial settings where wiring is difficult. This reduces cabling complexity and improves safety.
2. DC-Native Power Management Integrated Circuit (PMIC): A specialized chip designed to efficiently manage DC power distribution within buildings and data centers, optimizing energy usage and reducing losses.
3. DC Microgrid Energy Trading Platform: Facilitates peer-to-peer energy exchange within a DC microgrid, allowing different facilities (e.g., buildings within a port) to buy and sell excess DC power.
4. DC-Enabled Modular Building Blocks: Prefabricated building components designed for DC power distribution, simplifying construction and enabling flexible power configurations in buildings and industrial facilities.
5. DC-Optimized Lighting and Appliance Standards: Encourages the development of lighting and appliances designed to operate efficiently on DC power, reducing the need for AC-DC conversion and maximizing energy savings.
