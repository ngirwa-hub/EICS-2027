Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project’s goals and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Enables direct charging of DC-powered vehicles, reducing AC-DC conversion losses and simplifying port and building electrical systems.
2. Distributed DC Power Management System: Allows for localized control and optimization of DC power distribution within industrial facilities or large buildings, improving efficiency and reducing energy waste.
3. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Utilizes DC power directly for lighting, bypassing AC conversion and potentially increasing lighting system efficiency and reducing harmonic distortion.
4. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) System: Provides a method for wirelessly transmitting DC power to devices within a port or industrial setting, eliminating the need for physical connections.
5. DC-Compatible Modular UPS: A modular uninterruptible power supply designed to operate natively in a DC environment, enhancing data center resilience and power quality.
