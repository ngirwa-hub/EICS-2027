Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project’s focus sectors (ports, industry, data centers, and buildings), and were not included in the previously reviewed list:

1.  DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These PDUs directly distribute DC power within a facility, reducing conversion losses and simplifying power management compared to traditional AC PDUs.
2.  DC-Native Lighting Systems: Directly powering LED lighting with DC eliminates AC-DC conversion at the fixture level, improving efficiency and reducing heat generation.
3.  DC-Enabled Charging Infrastructure: Providing DC fast charging for electric vehicles or industrial equipment directly from DC sources minimizes conversion losses and increases charging speed.
4.  DC Microgrid Management System: A software platform to monitor, control, and optimize the performance of DC microgrids, including load balancing and energy storage integration.
5.  DC-Compatible Power Metering: Specialized meters designed for accurate and reliable measurement of DC power, crucial for monitoring energy consumption and optimizing DC system performance.
