Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project's goals and not repeating previously listed solutions:

1.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer System: Enables power distribution without cables, particularly useful in ports for automated guided vehicles (AGVs) and in data centers for server power.
2.  DC-Native Power Management Integrated Circuits (PMICs): Specifically designed chips for DC systems, optimizing efficiency and control for building and industrial applications.
3.  DC Microgrid Energy Management System (EMS): A software platform for real-time monitoring, control, and optimization of DC microgrids in various sectors, balancing load and generation.
4.  DC-Enabled Predictive Maintenance Platform: Uses data from DC measurement devices to anticipate equipment failures and schedule maintenance proactively, improving reliability in industrial and port environments.
5.  DC-Based Lighting and HVAC Control System: Directly powers lighting and HVAC systems from a DC source, avoiding AC-DC conversions and potentially increasing energy efficiency in buildings.
