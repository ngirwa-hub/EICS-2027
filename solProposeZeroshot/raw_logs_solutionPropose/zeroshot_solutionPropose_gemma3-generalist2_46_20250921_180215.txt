Here are some additional DC solutions, tailored for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and not included in the initial list:

1.  DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a facility, providing monitoring and control capabilities similar to AC PDUs. They enhance efficiency and allow for dynamic power allocation.
2.  DC-Based Lighting Systems: Replacing traditional AC lighting with DC lighting eliminates conversion losses and improves overall system efficiency, particularly beneficial in large facilities.
3.  DC Motor Drives: Utilizing DC motors directly powered by DC sources avoids AC-DC conversion for industrial applications, increasing efficiency and reducing equipment size.
4.  DC Fast Charging Infrastructure: Expanding DC fast charging stations for electric vehicles at ports and industrial sites provides a quicker and more efficient charging process compared to AC charging.
5.  DC-Native Building Management Systems (BMS): Integrating DC power distribution directly into building management systems allows for more granular control and optimization of energy usage.
