Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project’s target sectors (ports, industry, data centers, and buildings), beyond the previously listed items:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering centralized control and monitoring capabilities. They are essential for efficient DC power management in buildings and data centers.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion, improving efficiency and reducing heat generation, particularly beneficial in industrial settings and large buildings.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure: Enables rapid charging of electric vehicles (EVs) within ports and industrial facilities, improving operational efficiency and reducing charging times.
4. DC-Native Industrial Control Systems: Replacing traditional AC-based control systems with DC-native systems reduces conversion losses and simplifies automation in industrial environments.
5. DC Microgrid Management Software: This software optimizes the operation of DC microgrids, balancing energy supply and demand and integrating renewable energy sources for enhanced resilience and efficiency across all target sectors.
