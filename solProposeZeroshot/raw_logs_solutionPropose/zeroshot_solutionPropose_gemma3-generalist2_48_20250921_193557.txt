Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project’s goals, and not previously listed:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering centralized control and monitoring capabilities.
2. DC-Based Energy Management System (EMS): An intelligent system that optimizes DC power usage, balances loads, and integrates renewable energy sources for improved efficiency.
3. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Directly powering LED lighting with DC eliminates AC-DC conversion losses, reducing energy consumption and improving system reliability.
4. DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Provides efficient and direct DC charging for electric vehicles, reducing conversion losses and supporting DC microgrid integration.
5. Modular DC Power Architectures: Enables scalable and adaptable DC power systems that can be easily expanded or reconfigured to meet changing needs in various applications.
