Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project’s goals and not repeating solutions from the initial list:

1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These PDUs would directly distribute DC power within facilities, reducing conversion losses and simplifying power management compared to traditional AC PDUs.
2. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure for Ports: DC fast chargers are essential for electric vehicle adoption in ports and can be directly integrated into DC microgrids, increasing efficiency.
3. DC-Native Industrial Automation Systems: Shifting industrial control systems to natively use DC power can eliminate unnecessary AC-DC conversions and improve overall system efficiency.
4. Modular DC Power Architectures for Data Centers: Utilizing modular, scalable DC power systems allows for greater flexibility and redundancy in data center power infrastructure.
5. DC Lighting Control Systems for Buildings: Direct DC lighting systems remove the need for AC-DC conversion in lighting circuits, improving energy efficiency and enabling advanced lighting control features.
