Here are some additional DC solutions, beyond the initial list, that would be valuable for the Shift2DC project’s focus areas of ports, industry, data centers, and buildings:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a facility, similar to AC PDUs, providing monitoring, switching, and surge protection. They’re crucial for organized and reliable DC power delivery.
2. DC Fault Current Limiting Devices: These devices automatically reduce fault currents in a DC system, improving safety and minimizing damage during short circuits, particularly important in high-voltage DC environments.
3. DC-Based Microgrid Management System: A software platform that optimizes the operation of DC microgrids, coordinating energy sources, storage, and loads to maximize efficiency and resilience.
4. DC-Compatible Lighting Systems: LED lighting solutions designed for direct DC operation, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and improving overall system efficiency in buildings and industrial settings.
5. Modular DC Power Architectures: Pre-engineered, scalable DC power building blocks that allow for flexible and adaptable power solutions in data centers and industrial facilities, simplifying deployment and future expansion.
