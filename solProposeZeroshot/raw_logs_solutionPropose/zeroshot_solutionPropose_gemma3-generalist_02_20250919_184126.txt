Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and were not included in the initial list:

1. DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Direct DC charging eliminates AC-DC conversion at the charging station, increasing efficiency and potentially reducing charging time for EVs.
2. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC lighting directly from a DC power source reduces conversion losses compared to traditional AC lighting systems, lowering energy consumption.
3. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These PDUs would natively distribute DC power within facilities, reducing losses and simplifying integration with DC loads.
4. DC-Compatible Modular UPS Systems: These systems would provide uninterruptible DC power, crucial for sensitive equipment in data centers and industrial processes, improving reliability.
5. DC-Based Microgrid Control System: A centralized control system optimized for DC microgrids, managing power flow, energy storage, and load balancing for enhanced operational efficiency.
