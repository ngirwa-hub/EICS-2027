Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and were not included in the previous list:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering intelligent load balancing and remote monitoring capabilities.
2. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Utilizing LEDs directly powered by DC eliminates AC-DC conversion for lighting, increasing efficiency and reducing heat generation.
3. DC-Based Motor Drives: Replacing traditional AC motor drives with DC alternatives offers improved efficiency and control, particularly beneficial in industrial applications.
4. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure for Ports: Dedicated DC fast charging stations for electric vehicles and port equipment, minimizing charging times and maximizing operational efficiency.
5. DC Microgrid Management Software: A centralized software platform to monitor, control, and optimize the performance of DC microgrids across various applications.
