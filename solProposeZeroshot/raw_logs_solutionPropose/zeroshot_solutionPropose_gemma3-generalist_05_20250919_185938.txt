Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project’s aims and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a facility, providing intelligent monitoring and control capabilities similar to AC PDUs. They are crucial for efficient power delivery in DC microgrids.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC lighting directly reduces AC-DC conversion losses and can improve efficiency and lifespan compared to traditional AC lighting solutions.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure: Providing DC fast charging stations for electric vehicles and other equipment directly from DC microgrids simplifies power delivery and reduces conversion losses.
4. DC-Native Motor Drives: Replacing traditional AC motor drives with DC-native alternatives in industrial processes minimizes conversion losses and can offer improved motor control.
5. DC Power Quality Monitoring System: This system provides real-time data on DC voltage, current, and harmonics, ensuring power quality and enabling predictive maintenance.
