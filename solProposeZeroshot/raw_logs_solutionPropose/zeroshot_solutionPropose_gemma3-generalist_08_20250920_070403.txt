Here are some additional DC solutions relevant to the Shift2DC project’s focus areas (ports, industry, data centers, buildings) that weren’t previously listed:

1. DC-Based Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Direct DC charging eliminates AC-DC conversion losses at charging stations, increasing efficiency and potentially reducing charging times.
2. DC Power Distribution Units (DC PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a facility, similar to AC PDUs but optimized for DC systems, enabling better control and monitoring.
3. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC lighting fixtures removes the need for AC-DC conversion in buildings, boosting energy efficiency and reducing heat generation.
4. DC-Powered Robotics and Automation: Supplying robotic systems directly with DC power streamlines operations within industrial settings and data centers, minimizing energy conversion steps.
5. DC-Based Microgrid Control System: A centralized control system specifically designed for DC microgrids, enabling optimized power flow, load balancing, and seamless integration of renewable energy sources.
