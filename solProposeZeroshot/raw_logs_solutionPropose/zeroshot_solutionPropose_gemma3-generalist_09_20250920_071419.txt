Here are some additional DC solutions relevant to the Shift2DC project's focus areas (ports, industry, data centers, buildings) that weren's previously listed:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering centralized control and monitoring capabilities for improved efficiency and safety.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC lighting directly, bypassing AC conversion, reduces energy losses and can improve the lifespan of LEDs commonly used in modern lighting.
3. DC-Native Power Supplies: Power supplies designed to operate directly from a DC source, minimizing conversion steps and enhancing overall system efficiency in devices and equipment.
4. DC-Based Motor Drives: Replacing traditional AC motor drives with DC alternatives can improve motor control precision and reduce energy consumption in industrial applications and building systems.
5. DC Microgrid Management Software: Software platforms for monitoring, controlling, and optimizing the performance of DC microgrids, enabling dynamic load balancing and efficient energy management.
