Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project’s goals and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a facility, similar to AC PDUs but optimized for DC voltage levels and offering enhanced monitoring and control capabilities.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion for lighting loads, increasing efficiency and potentially enabling more flexible and responsive lighting control.
3. DC-Native Motor Drives: Utilizing DC motors and drives directly in industrial processes avoids AC-DC conversion losses common with traditional motor systems, improving overall energy efficiency.
4. DC-Coupled Microgrids for Buildings: Integrating renewable energy sources, energy storage, and building loads within a DC microgrid architecture improves resilience and optimizes energy usage.
5. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer Systems: Enables charging of electric vehicles and other devices wirelessly using DC power, reducing cabling and improving convenience within port and industrial settings.
