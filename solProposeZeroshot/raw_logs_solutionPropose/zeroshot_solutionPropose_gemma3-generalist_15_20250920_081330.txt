Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project’s focus sectors (ports, industry, data centers, buildings), beyond the previously listed items:

1.  DC-Powered Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure: Direct DC charging stations eliminate AC-DC conversion losses, increasing efficiency and reducing infrastructure costs, particularly beneficial for ports and industrial facilities.
2.  DC-Native LED Lighting Systems: Replacing traditional AC-driven LED drivers with DC power directly reduces energy losses and offers finer control over lighting intensity.
3.  DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within buildings and data centers, providing enhanced monitoring and control capabilities.
4.  DC-Based Microgrid Controller: This centralized system optimizes the operation of DC microgrids by coordinating generation, storage, and loads, improving resilience and efficiency.
5.  DC-Compatible Power Quality Monitoring System: A system designed to specifically analyze and improve power quality within DC microgrids, addressing issues like voltage sags and harmonics.
