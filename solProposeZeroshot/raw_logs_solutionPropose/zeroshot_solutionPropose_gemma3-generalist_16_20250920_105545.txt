Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project’s goals and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a facility, providing voltage regulation, overload protection, and monitoring capabilities. They are essential for efficient DC power management.
2. DC-Based Energy Management System (EMS): A software platform to monitor, control, and optimize DC power consumption and generation across a building or industrial site, improving energy efficiency.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure for Ports: Dedicated DC charging stations specifically designed for electric vehicle fleets and heavy-duty vehicles commonly found in port environments.
4. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Lighting solutions that operate directly on DC power, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and potentially improving efficiency and lifespan.
5. Modular DC Power Architectures: Scalable and adaptable DC power systems built from standardized modules, allowing for easy expansion and reconfiguration as needs change within a data center or industrial setting.
