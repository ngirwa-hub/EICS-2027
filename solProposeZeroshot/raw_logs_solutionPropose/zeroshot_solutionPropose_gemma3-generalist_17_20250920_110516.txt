Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building upon the Shift2DC project's goals and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a facility, providing monitoring, switching, and surge protection for connected DC loads. They are essential for organized and efficient DC power management.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC lighting fixtures eliminates AC-DC conversion losses commonly found in traditional lighting, improving overall energy efficiency in buildings and industrial settings.
3. DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Direct DC charging eliminates AC-DC conversion steps for EVs, increasing charging efficiency and potentially reducing infrastructure costs, especially relevant for ports and industrial facilities.
4. DC-Native Motor Drives: Replacing traditional AC motor drives with DC-native drives in industrial applications minimizes conversion losses and can provide improved motor control and efficiency.
5. DC-Based Microgrid Control System: A centralized system to manage and optimize the operation of DC microgrids, coordinating power generation, storage, and load management for increased resilience and efficiency.
