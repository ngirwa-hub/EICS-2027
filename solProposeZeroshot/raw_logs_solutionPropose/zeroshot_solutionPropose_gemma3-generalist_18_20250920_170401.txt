Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project’s focus areas (ports, industry, data centers, buildings) and weren’t previously listed:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a facility, providing monitoring, control, and surge protection. They are essential for organized and reliable DC power delivery.
2. DC-Based Microgrid Management System: A software platform that optimizes the operation of DC microgrids, balancing generation, storage, and load, while prioritizing renewable energy sources. This enhances efficiency and resilience.
3. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Lighting systems designed to operate directly on DC power, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses associated with traditional LED lighting, reducing energy consumption.
4. DC-Enabled Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure: Specialized charging stations that deliver DC power directly to electric vehicles, minimizing conversion losses and accelerating charging times.
5. DC-Based HVAC Systems: Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems that utilize DC power, often paired with DC-powered heat pumps, for improved efficiency and integration with DC microgrids.
