Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project’s goals and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering intelligent load balancing and remote monitoring capabilities. They are crucial for organized and efficient DC power management.
2. DC-Based Microgrid Controller: A centralized controller that optimizes the operation of a DC microgrid, managing energy flow, prioritizing loads, and integrating renewable energy sources. This enhances resilience and efficiency.
3. DC-Native UPS Systems: Uninterruptible Power Supplies designed to operate natively in DC, avoiding AC-DC-AC conversions and providing cleaner, more efficient backup power.
4. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure for Ports: Specialized DC chargers designed for electric vehicle fleets within port environments, reducing charging times and supporting electrification efforts.
5. DC-Enabled Lighting Systems: Integrated lighting solutions utilizing DC power directly, minimizing conversion losses and improving energy efficiency in buildings and industrial settings.
