Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, beyond those already listed for the Shift2DC project:

1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These PDUs would distribute DC power within a facility, offering improved efficiency compared to traditional AC PDUs and reducing conversion losses.
2. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Directly powering LED lighting with DC eliminates the need for AC-DC conversion in lighting circuits, leading to energy savings and simplified infrastructure.
3. DC-Enabled Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Utilizing DC fast charging directly from DC sources, such as solar panels or battery storage, improves charging efficiency and reduces grid strain.
4. DC-Based Motor Drives for Industrial Equipment: Replacing traditional AC motor drives with DC-based alternatives can increase efficiency and provide more precise motor control, particularly beneficial for variable speed applications.
5. DC-Coupled Microgrids with Virtual Power Plants (VPPs): Integrating multiple DC microgrids into a larger network via a VPP allows for optimized power sharing, load balancing, and enhanced resilience across various facilities.
