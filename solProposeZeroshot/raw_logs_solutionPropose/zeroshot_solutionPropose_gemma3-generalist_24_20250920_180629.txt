Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and were not included in the earlier list:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, providing monitoring and control capabilities similar to AC PDUs. They improve efficiency and simplify DC power management.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion in lighting circuits, reducing energy losses and potentially extending LED lifespan. This is particularly beneficial in large buildings and industrial facilities.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure for Ports: Dedicated DC fast charging systems for electric vehicles (EVs) within ports can reduce charging times and improve the efficiency of electric vehicle operations.
4. DC-Native Motor Drives for Industrial Applications: Utilizing DC motors and variable frequency drives directly connected to DC power eliminates AC-DC conversion, increasing efficiency and reducing harmonic distortion in industrial processes.
5. DC-Coupled Microgrids for Data Centers: Integrating DC power sources (like solar PV) directly into data center microgrids through DC-DC converters reduces conversion losses and enhances resilience.
