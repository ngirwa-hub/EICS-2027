Here are some additional DC solutions relevant to ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project's goals, and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC-Based Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Enables direct charging of DC-powered vehicles, reducing AC-DC conversion losses and simplifying port and industrial site charging systems.
2. DC-Native Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Replaces traditional AC PDUs in data centers and buildings, offering increased efficiency and enabling direct integration of DC power sources.
3. DC-Coupled LED Lighting Systems: Eliminates AC-DC conversion for LED lighting, improving efficiency and reducing heat generation within buildings and industrial facilities.
4. DC Microgrid Management Software: Provides a centralized platform for monitoring, controlling, and optimizing DC microgrids in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.
5. DC-Based Power Quality Monitoring System: Continuously assesses the quality of DC power, ensuring reliable operation of connected equipment and enabling proactive maintenance.
