Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project’s focus sectors (ports, industry, data centers, and buildings), and weren's previously listed:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering precise voltage regulation and load balancing for improved efficiency.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion, reducing energy losses and potentially offering longer lamp lifespans in DC-powered buildings.
3. DC-Native Motor Drives: Replacing traditional AC motor drives with DC drives in industrial applications can offer improved efficiency and control, particularly for variable-speed applications.
4. DC-Coupled Energy Management Systems (EMS): An EMS specifically designed for DC grids optimizes energy usage by monitoring and controlling DC power flows across different loads and sources.
5. Wireless DC Power Transfer Systems: Allows for contactless power delivery to devices within a DC microgrid, useful for ports (e.g., charging electric vehicles) and buildings (e.g., powering sensors).
