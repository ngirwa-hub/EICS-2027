Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and weren't included in the previous list:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These intelligently manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering remote monitoring and control capabilities.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion, improving efficiency and reducing energy waste in buildings and industrial settings.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure: Supports high-power charging for electric vehicles within ports and industrial complexes, minimizing charging time.
4. DC-Native Power Electronics for Motors: Utilizing DC power directly for motors in industrial applications and data centers reduces conversion losses and enhances efficiency.
5. Dynamic DC Power Allocation: A system that intelligently adjusts DC power distribution based on real-time demand across different loads within a building or industrial site.
