Here are some additional DC solutions relevant to ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project's aims, and not repeating solutions already listed:

1. DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Provides direct DC power to EVs, reducing conversion losses and potentially enabling faster charging speeds within port and industrial settings.
2. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Eliminates AC-DC conversion for lighting, improving efficiency and reducing heat generation in buildings and data centers.
3. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Provides a more efficient and flexible way to distribute power within industrial facilities and data centers, minimizing conversion losses.
4. DC-Compatible Power Management Integrated Circuits (PMICs): These specialized chips efficiently manage and regulate DC power distribution in buildings and data centers, optimizing energy use.
5. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) Systems: Enables contactless power delivery within ports and industrial environments, useful for powering automated guided vehicles (AGVs) and other mobile equipment.
