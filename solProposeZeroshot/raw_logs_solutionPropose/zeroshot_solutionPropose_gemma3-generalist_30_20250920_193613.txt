Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project's focus areas, beyond the previously listed items:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, similar to AC PDUs but optimized for DC voltage levels and incorporating smart monitoring capabilities.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Replacing traditional AC lighting with DC lighting can eliminate conversion losses and improve energy efficiency in buildings and industrial settings.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure: Providing DC fast charging stations specifically designed for DC microgrids in ports and industrial areas streamlines electrification efforts.
4. DC-Native Motor Drives: Utilizing DC motors directly, instead of AC motors with inverters, increases efficiency and reduces complexity in industrial applications and data centers.
5. DC-Based HVAC Systems: Implementing DC-powered heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems reduces energy waste and improves overall system efficiency in buildings and data centers.
