Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and were not previously listed:

1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These PDUs directly distribute DC power within a facility, reducing AC-DC conversion losses common in traditional systems. They offer improved efficiency and simplified power management.
2. DC-Native Load Balancing System: A system that intelligently distributes DC power across different loads within a facility, preventing overloading and optimizing energy usage based on real-time demand.
3. DC-Compatible Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC lighting fixtures, particularly LED lighting, eliminates the need for AC-DC conversion at the point of use, improving overall system efficiency and reducing heat generation.
4. DC-Based Motor Drives: Replacing traditional AC motor drives with DC motor drives, particularly for industrial applications and port equipment, can offer higher efficiency and better control, alongside simplified power electronics.
5. DC-Coupled Microgrids with Virtual Power Plants: Integrating multiple DC microgrids within a larger area, and treating them as a single, manageable power source, optimizes resource utilization and enhances resilience.
