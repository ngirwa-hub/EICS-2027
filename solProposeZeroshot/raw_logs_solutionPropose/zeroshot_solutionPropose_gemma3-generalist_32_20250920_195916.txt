Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project’s goals and not repeating previously listed items:

1.  DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Direct DC charging stations eliminate AC-DC conversion losses, improving efficiency and reducing infrastructure costs within ports and industrial facilities.
2.  DC-Native Power Management Integrated Circuits (PMICs): These specialized chips optimize DC power distribution and management within data centers and buildings, improving overall system efficiency and reducing heat generation.
3.  DC-Based Lighting Control Systems: Direct DC lighting systems remove AC-DC conversion, resulting in lower energy consumption and simplified control within buildings and industrial settings.
4.  DC-Coupled Microgrid Controller: A centralized control system specifically designed for DC microgrids, enabling intelligent load balancing, energy storage management, and seamless integration of renewable energy sources across multiple sectors.
5.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) Systems: Enables contactless power delivery for applications like automated guided vehicles (AGVs) in ports and industry, and for powering IoT devices in buildings, all running on DC power.
