Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project goals and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These specialized PDUs distribute DC power within a facility, offering more efficient power delivery and reduced conversion losses compared to traditional AC PDUs.
2. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC lighting fixtures directly eliminates the need for AC-to-DC conversion, leading to energy savings and simpler system designs within buildings and industrial facilities.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure for Ports: Implementing DC fast chargers directly connected to DC microgrids within ports minimizes conversion losses and improves charging efficiency for electric vehicles and port equipment.
4. DC-Coupled Energy Management System (EMS): A centralized EMS that manages DC power flows, optimizes energy usage, and integrates renewable energy sources within a DC microgrid, improving overall system efficiency.
5. Modular DC Power Architectures for Data Centers: Employing modular, scalable DC power blocks allows for greater flexibility and redundancy in data center power systems, reducing downtime and improving resilience.
