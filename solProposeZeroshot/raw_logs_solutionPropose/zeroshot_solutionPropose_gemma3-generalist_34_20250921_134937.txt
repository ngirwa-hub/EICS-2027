Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project's goals and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a facility, similar to AC PDUs but optimized for DC voltage levels and load management.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion in lighting circuits, improving efficiency and reducing heat generation within buildings and industrial settings.
3. DC-Native Motor Drives: Utilizing DC motors and drives directly in industrial processes avoids AC-DC conversion, increasing efficiency and reducing overall system complexity.
4. DC-Coupled Solar Inverters: These inverters convert DC solar power directly to DC voltage for use in DC microgrids or other DC loads, reducing conversion losses.
5. DC-Based Microgrid Controllers: Sophisticated controllers that manage the operation of DC microgrids, optimizing energy flow, balancing loads, and integrating renewable energy sources.
