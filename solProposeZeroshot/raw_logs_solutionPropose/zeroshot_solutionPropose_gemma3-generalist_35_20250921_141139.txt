Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, beyond the initial list, formatted as requested:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, similar to AC PDUs, but optimized for DC voltage levels and offering enhanced monitoring capabilities.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion in lighting circuits, improving efficiency and potentially extending the lifespan of LED lighting components.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure: Providing DC fast charging specifically designed for ports and industry, enabling quicker electric vehicle charging and reducing grid impact.
4. DC-Native Building Management Systems (BMS): A BMS designed to natively control and optimize DC power usage within buildings, allowing for finer-grained control and energy savings.
5. DC-Coupled Microgrids for Ports: Integrating multiple DC power sources (solar, storage, etc.) within a port environment to create a resilient and efficient energy system.
