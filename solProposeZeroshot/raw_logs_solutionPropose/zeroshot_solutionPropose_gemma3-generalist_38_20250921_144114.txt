Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and weren't included in the initial list:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, providing monitoring, control, and overload protection, similar to AC PDUs.
2. DC-based Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion, improving efficiency and potentially reducing component costs within buildings and industrial facilities.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure: Specialized DC charging stations for electric vehicles, critical for ports and industrial areas with high vehicle traffic.
4. DC-Native Motor Drives: Motors designed to run directly on DC power, avoiding AC-DC conversion and improving efficiency in industrial applications and data centers.
5. DC-based HVAC Systems: Utilizing DC motors and components in heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems to improve energy efficiency and reduce operating costs.
