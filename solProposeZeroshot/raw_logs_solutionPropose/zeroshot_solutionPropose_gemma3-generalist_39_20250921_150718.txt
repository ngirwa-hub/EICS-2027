Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project’s goals and not repeating previously listed items:

1.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer System: Enables power distribution without cables, beneficial for mobile equipment in ports and industrial settings, and for powering devices in data centers and buildings.
2.  DC-Native Load Balancing System: Optimizes power distribution across DC loads, minimizing losses and improving efficiency within industrial facilities, ports, and data centers.
3.  DC Microgrid Energy Management System: Provides centralized control and optimization of DC microgrids in buildings and industrial environments, balancing supply and demand.
4.  DC Power Quality Monitoring and Correction: Detects and mitigates power quality issues (voltage sags, swells) specific to DC systems, crucial for sensitive data center equipment.
5.  Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Provides scalable and flexible power delivery for buildings and data centers, allowing for easy expansion and customization.
