Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project's focus sectors (ports, industry, data centers, buildings), beyond the previously listed options:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, providing monitoring, switching, and overload protection, crucial for efficient DC power management.
2. DC-Based Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC lighting directly reduces conversion losses compared to AC-to-DC conversion for lighting, improving energy efficiency in buildings and industrial settings.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure: Dedicated DC fast charging stations for electric vehicles, particularly important for ports and industrial areas with high vehicle traffic, minimizes AC-DC conversion losses.
4. DC-Native Motor Drives: Replacing traditional AC motor drives with DC-native motor drives in industrial processes offers higher efficiency and improved control capabilities.
5. DC-Based Microgrid Controllers: Sophisticated controllers that intelligently manage DC microgrids, optimizing energy flow, balancing loads, and integrating renewable sources across various sectors.
