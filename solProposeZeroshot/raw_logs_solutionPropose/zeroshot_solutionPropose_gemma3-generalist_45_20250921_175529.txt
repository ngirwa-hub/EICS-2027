Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and were not included in the previous list:

1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, similar to AC PDUs, but optimized for DC voltage levels and offering enhanced monitoring capabilities.
2. DC-Based Microgrid Management System: A software platform to control and optimize the operation of DC microgrids, coordinating energy generation, storage, and consumption across various loads.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure for Ports: Specialized DC chargers designed for electric vehicle fleets and heavy-duty vehicles commonly found in port environments.
4. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Lighting fixtures designed to operate directly on DC power, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and improving energy efficiency in buildings.
5. DC-Compatible Power Metering Systems: Advanced meters capable of accurately measuring DC power consumption, enabling better energy management and billing in DC environments.
