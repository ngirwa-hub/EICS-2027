Here are some additional DC solutions that could be valuable for the Shift2DC project’s target sectors (ports, industry, data centers, and buildings), and were not included in the previous list:

1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): DC PDUs manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering precise control and improved energy efficiency compared to traditional AC systems.
2. Wireless DC Power Transfer Systems: Enables power delivery without physical cables, ideal for mobile devices and equipment within ports or industrial settings.
3. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Eliminates AC-DC conversion for lighting, reducing energy losses and improving the overall efficiency of buildings and data centers.
4. DC-Compatible Power Conditioners: Improves power quality by filtering noise and voltage fluctuations in DC systems, vital for sensitive equipment in data centers and industrial processes.
5. DC Microgrid Management Software: A software platform to monitor, control, and optimize the performance of DC microgrids across multiple sectors, improving stability and reducing operational costs.
