Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project's goals, and not included in the previously reviewed list:

1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These PDUs would distribute DC power within a facility, offering efficiency gains by minimizing AC-DC conversions needed for connected devices. They simplify power management and reduce cabling complexity.
2. DC-Native Load Balancing System: This system optimizes power distribution within a DC microgrid by intelligently managing the load across different DC sources and loads, preventing overloading and maximizing efficiency.
3. Wireless DC Power Transfer System: Enables power delivery without physical cables, useful for mobile equipment in ports or industrial settings and for powering IoT devices in buildings.
4. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure for Ports: Provides rapid charging for electric vehicles and other DC-powered equipment commonly found in port environments, increasing operational efficiency.
5. Modular DC Power Architectures for Data Centers: Allows for scalable and flexible power delivery in data centers, enabling efficient use of space and resources while accommodating growing power demands.
