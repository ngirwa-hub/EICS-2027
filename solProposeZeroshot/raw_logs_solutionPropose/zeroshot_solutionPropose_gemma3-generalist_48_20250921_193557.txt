Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building on the Shift2DC project's goals and not repeating solutions already listed:

1. DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Provides direct DC power to EVs, bypassing AC-DC conversion at the charging station and improving efficiency. This is particularly relevant for ports and industrial facilities with high EV usage.
2. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC LED lighting throughout buildings and industrial facilities reduces conversion losses compared to traditional AC lighting, leading to energy savings.
3. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Simplifies DC power distribution within data centers and industrial settings, minimizing voltage drops and improving overall system efficiency.
4. DC Microgrid Management System: Software platform for optimizing the operation of DC microgrids, including load balancing, energy storage management, and fault detection.
5. Modular DC Power Supplies: Scalable and easily adaptable DC power supplies that can be combined to meet varying power demands in data centers and buildings.
