Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, building upon the Shift2DC project’s goals and not repeating previously listed items:

1. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer System: Enables contactless power delivery within facilities, useful for charging electric vehicles in ports or powering equipment in industrial settings.
2. DC Microgrid Management Software: Provides centralized control and optimization of DC microgrids, enhancing efficiency and reliability across buildings, data centers, and industrial facilities.
3. DC Power Quality Monitoring System: Continuously assesses and reports on DC power quality metrics, ensuring stable and reliable operation of sensitive equipment in data centers and industrial processes.
4. DC-Native Load Balancing System: Dynamically distributes power demand across different DC sources and loads, maximizing efficiency and preventing overload in buildings and industrial plants.
5. Standardized DC Equipment Certification Program: Establishes a clear set of performance and safety standards for DC equipment, fostering wider adoption and interoperability across all four sectors.
