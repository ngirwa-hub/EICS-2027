Okay, considering the Shift2DC project’s focus on ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and excluding the previously listed solutions, here are five additional DC solutions that I believe would be valuable:

1.  DC Power Distribution Units (DC PDUs): These devices manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering voltage regulation, load balancing, and remote monitoring capabilities similar to AC PDUs.
2.  DC-Based Microgrid Management System: A software platform for optimizing DC microgrid operation, including energy dispatch, demand response, and integration of distributed generation resources.
3.  DC Fault Current Limiter: A device specifically designed to limit DC fault currents to safe levels, protecting equipment and improving system resilience, particularly important in large DC grids.
4.  DC Energy Harvesting Interface: Integrates small-scale renewable energy sources (solar, thermal) directly into DC grids, providing a decentralized and efficient power source.
5.  DC-Native Lighting Control System:  Utilizes DC power directly for lighting systems, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and enabling more efficient and responsive lighting control strategies.
