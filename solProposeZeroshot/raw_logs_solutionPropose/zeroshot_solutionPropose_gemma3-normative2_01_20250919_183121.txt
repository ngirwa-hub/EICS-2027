Okay, considering the Shift2DC project's focus and the need for additional DC solutions beyond the initial 11, here are five suggestions, tailored to the target sectors and formatted as requested. I've strived to propose solutions that address specific challenges within each sector and complement the existing list.

1.  DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent PDUs will manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering monitoring, load balancing, and remote control capabilities.
2.  DC-Native Lighting Systems: LED lighting directly powered by DC eliminates AC-DC conversion at the fixture level, increasing efficiency and reducing heat generation in buildings and industrial settings.
3.  DC-Based Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure: Direct DC charging infrastructure for ports and industrial areas minimizes conversion losses compared to traditional AC charging, optimizing renewable energy utilization.
4.  DC-Coupled Microgrid Controllers: Dedicated controllers for DC microgrids in data centers and ports will optimize energy flow, manage distributed generation, and provide grid stability through advanced algorithms.
5.  DC-Compatible Energy Management Systems (EMS): An EMS specifically designed for DC systems to monitor, control, and optimize energy usage across buildings and industrial processes, enabling peak shaving and demand response.
