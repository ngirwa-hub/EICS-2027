Okay, considering the Shift2DC project’s focus and the need for additional DC solutions beyond the original 11, here are five solutions, formatted as requested, tailored to the target sectors (ports, industry, data centers, and buildings) and not already listed.

1.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) for Ports: Enables charging of electric vehicles, automated guided vehicles (AGVs), and port equipment without physical cables, enhancing operational efficiency and reducing infrastructure complexity.
2.  DC Microgrid Management System (MGMS): A sophisticated software platform for real-time monitoring, control, and optimization of DC microgrids within industrial facilities, improving energy usage and resilience.
3.  DC-Native Server Architecture: Designs data center servers and hardware optimized for direct DC power delivery, reducing conversion losses and potentially improving energy efficiency beyond what's achievable with AC-to-DC conversion.
4.  DC Lighting Control System with Occupancy Sensing: Integrates DC lighting fixtures with advanced occupancy sensors and dimming capabilities, maximizing energy savings within buildings and improving occupant comfort.
5.  DC-Powered Electric Vehicle Charging Hubs: Centralized charging stations in ports, industry, and building complexes that leverage DC infrastructure for fast, efficient charging of electric vehicles and equipment, minimizing energy conversion losses.
