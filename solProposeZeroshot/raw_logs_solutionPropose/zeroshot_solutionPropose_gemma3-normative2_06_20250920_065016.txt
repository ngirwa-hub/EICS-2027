Okay, considering the Shift2DC project’s goals and the four target sectors (ports, industry, data centers, and buildings), and excluding the previously listed solutions, here are five additional DC solutions that I believe would be valuable additions.

1.  DC Power Distribution Units (DPDUs): Intelligent DPDUs manage and distribute DC power within facilities, offering granular monitoring and control capabilities for enhanced energy efficiency and fault isolation.
2.  DC-based Microgrid Management System: A software platform that optimizes energy flow within a DC microgrid, prioritizing renewable sources and managing load balancing across multiple DC loads.
3.  DC-Native Energy Management System (EMS): An EMS specifically designed for DC environments, providing advanced analytics, predictive maintenance, and integration with building automation systems.
4.  DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Direct DC charging stations eliminate AC-DC conversion, improving charging efficiency for electric vehicles in ports and industrial settings.
5.  DC-Enabled Modular Data Center Power Architecture: A standardized, modular DC power architecture for data centers, allowing for scalable, resilient, and highly efficient power delivery systems.
