Okay, acknowledging the Shift2DC project goals and the previously listed solutions, here are five additional DC solution proposals, formatted as requested, and tailored to the target sectors of ports, industry, data centers, and buildings:

2.  Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDU): Offers flexible and scalable DC power distribution within buildings and industrial settings, allowing for easy expansion and reconfiguration based on evolving load demands.
3.  DC Fault Current Limiting Resistors: Provides a cost-effective mechanism for limiting fault currents in DC microgrids, complementing circuit breakers and enhancing system safety, applicable across all target sectors.
4.  DC Power Quality Monitoring and Analysis Software: Offers real-time insights into DC power quality parameters (voltage, ripple, harmonics) enabling proactive maintenance and improving the reliability of DC systems in ports and buildings.
5.  Bi-directional DC Charging Infrastructure: Supports efficient charging and discharging of electric vehicles (EVs) in ports and buildings using DC power, enabling vehicle-to-grid (V2G) capabilities and grid stabilization services.
