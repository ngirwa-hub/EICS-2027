Okay, considering the Shift2DC project’s focus and the earlier list, here are five additional DC solutions that could be valuable for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, formatted as requested.

1.  DC-Native Load Balancing System: Intelligent algorithms and controls that dynamically distribute DC power across loads within a DC microgrid, optimizing energy usage and preventing overload.
2.  DC Power Quality Monitoring and Correction: A suite of devices and software to proactively identify and mitigate common DC power quality issues like voltage sags, swells, and harmonic distortion.
3.  Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDU): Standardized, scalable DC PDUs with integrated monitoring and control features, simplifying deployment and management in industrial and data center environments.
4.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) System: Enables wireless power delivery for charging electric vehicles in ports, powering IoT devices in buildings, and supplying electricity to moving equipment in industrial settings.
5.  DC Fault Current Limiting Resistors: Devices placed strategically within DC circuits to reduce fault currents, enhancing safety and minimizing equipment damage in the event of a short circuit.
