Okay, acknowledging the Shift2DC project's focus and the previous list, here are five additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and which were not previously mentioned. I'm focusing on areas that address wider system integration and operational efficiencies.

1.  DC-Based Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) Integration: Enables electric vehicles within ports and industrial sites to feed power back into the DC microgrid, enhancing resilience and optimizing energy use.
2.  DC Power Distribution Units (DC PDUs): Standardized DC PDUs manage and distribute power within buildings and data centers, providing overload protection, metering, and remote control capabilities.
3.  DC-Native Energy Management System (EMS): An EMS specifically designed for DC microgrids, offering real-time monitoring, control, and optimization of energy flows, anticipating and responding to demand fluctuations.
4.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) System: Facilitates contactless power delivery within industrial settings and ports for mobile equipment, reducing cabling needs and increasing operational flexibility.
5.  DC Microgrid Cybersecurity Platform: A dedicated cybersecurity solution to protect DC microgrids from cyber threats, ensuring the integrity and reliability of DC power distribution networks.
