Okay, considering the Shift2DC project’s focus and the previously listed solutions, here are five additional DC solutions that could be particularly valuable for the targeted sectors (ports, industry, data centers, buildings) and weren't on the initial list.

1.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) System: Enables contactless energy delivery within facilities, useful for powering automated guided vehicles (AGVs) in ports and industry, and flexible powering of devices in data centers and buildings.
2.  DC Microgrid Energy Management System (EMS): A software platform for optimized power flow, load balancing, and grid stability within DC microgrids, crucial for complex industrial facilities and large buildings.
3.  DC-Native Power Quality Monitoring & Harmonic Mitigation: Active filtering specifically designed for DC circuits to minimize harmonic distortion and improve power quality, essential for sensitive equipment in data centers and industrial processes.
4.  DC-Based Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Standardized DC fast-charging stations designed for integration with port and industrial DC microgrids, accelerating electrification and reducing grid strain.
5.  DC-Powered Lighting and HVAC Control System: An integrated system for controlling lighting and HVAC systems directly from a DC power source, reducing AC-DC conversion losses and improving energy efficiency in buildings.
