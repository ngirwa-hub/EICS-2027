Okay, here are five additional DC solutions, tailored to the Shift2DC project's focus on ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and *not* repeating solutions from the previous list. I've included brief explanations and aimed for solutions that address specific challenges within those sectors.

1.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer Systems: Enables charging of electric vehicles in ports and industrial facilities without physical cables, promoting safety and efficiency.
2.  Adaptive DC Power Management Software: Optimizes DC power distribution within buildings and data centers, dynamically adjusting power allocation based on real-time demand and renewable energy availability.
3.  DC-Native Modular Power Supplies: Provides standardized, scalable DC power building blocks for industry and data centers, simplifying system design and increasing flexibility.
4.  DC Microgrid Energy Transaction Platform: Facilitates peer-to-peer energy trading and management within port and industrial microgrids, encouraging renewable energy adoption and resilience.
5.  DC Fault Current Limiting Devices: Provides improved safety and reliability within DC distribution networks by reducing fault current magnitudes beyond the capabilities of conventional protection methods.
