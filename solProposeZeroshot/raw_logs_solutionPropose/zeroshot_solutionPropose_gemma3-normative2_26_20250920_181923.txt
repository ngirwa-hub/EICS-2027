Okay, considering the Shift2DC project's focus on ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and excluding the previously listed solutions, here are five additional DC solutions that I believe would be valuable:

1.  DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent PDUs for DC microgrids, enabling granular power management, remote monitoring, and efficient load balancing within facilities.
2.  DC-Based Microgrid Control Software: A centralized software platform for managing and optimizing DC microgrids, incorporating predictive algorithms and grid services capabilities.
3.  DC Fault Current Limiting Devices: Specialized devices designed to quickly and safely limit fault currents in DC circuits, enhancing system resilience and safety.
4.  DC Energy Harvesting Integration: Systems for integrating energy harvesting technologies (e.g., solar, waste heat) directly into DC power networks, maximizing renewable energy utilization.
5.  DC-Native Power Electronics for HVAC: Direct current powered heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems which offer improved energy efficiency compared to conventional AC systems.
