Okay, considering the Shift2DC project's focus and the initial list, here are five additional DC solutions that would be valuable across the targeted sectors (ports, industry, data centers, buildings), formatted as requested and not overlapping with the prior list.

1.  DC Power Distribution Units (DC PDUs): These units manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering intelligent load balancing, remote monitoring, and surge protection capabilities.
2.  DC-Native Energy Management System (DC-EMS): This system optimizes DC power usage across a facility, incorporating real-time data analysis, predictive maintenance, and integration with renewable energy sources.
3.  DC-Based Microgrid Controller: A central controller managing multiple DC microgrids within a larger facility, ensuring coordinated operation, resilience, and efficient energy sharing.
4.  DC-Coupled Lighting System: Utilizing DC LEDs directly, removing AC-DC conversion within lighting fixtures and offering enhanced control and improved efficiency for building illumination.
5.  Modular DC Power Blocks: Standardized, pre-engineered DC power blocks that can be easily combined and scaled to meet evolving power requirements in industrial or data center environments.
