Okay, acknowledging the Shift2DC project’s goals and the earlier list, here are five additional DC solutions, formatted as requested, and relevant to the target sectors (ports, industry, data centers, buildings).

1.  DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent DC PDUs manage power delivery within DC microgrids, providing monitoring, control, and load balancing capabilities. These are crucial for optimizing energy use and identifying inefficiencies.
2.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer Systems: Allows for contactless energy transmission within facilities, suitable for powering mobile equipment in ports or industrial settings, and flexible lighting in buildings.
3.  Bidirectional DC Charging Infrastructure: Supports both charging electric vehicles and discharging energy back to the grid or building, enhancing resilience and providing grid services.
4.  DC-Native Energy Management Systems (EMS): EMS specifically designed for DC microgrids, enabling granular control, predictive analytics, and automated responses to fluctuating loads and renewable energy production.
5.  DC-Optimized Lighting Control Systems: These systems are designed for direct integration with DC lighting, reducing conversion losses and enabling advanced dimming and color control features within buildings.
