Considering the Shift2DC project’s focus and the previously listed solutions, here are five additional DC solutions that I believe would be valuable for the targeted sectors (ports, industry, data centers, buildings), and were not included in the earlier list. I'm focusing on areas where DC’s inherent advantages can be maximized and where gaps exist.

1.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) System: Enables contactless energy transfer within facilities, useful for powering mobile equipment in ports and industrial settings, and for flexible DC distribution in data centers and buildings.
2.  DC Microgrid Management System: An intelligent control platform that optimizes energy flow, load balancing, and fault response within DC microgrids across all sectors, incorporating predictive analytics.
3.  DC-Native Energy Management System (EMS): A system designed specifically for DC energy environments, enabling granular control and monitoring of DC loads and generation sources for enhanced efficiency.
4.  DC-Optimized Power Delivery Units (PDUs): These are intelligent PDUs for buildings and data centers, capable of providing metered DC power directly to equipment, minimizing AC-DC conversion losses at the point of use.
5.  DC-Based Predictive Maintenance System: Leverages DC system data (voltage, current, temperature) for early detection of component failures and proactive maintenance scheduling in industrial and port environments.
