Okay, here are five additional DC solutions, formatted as requested, and suitable for consideration within the Shift2DC project, targeting ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and *not* repeating solutions from the original list. I'm focusing on areas where existing solutions might need complementary functionality or address specific challenges within these sectors.

1.  DC Power Distribution Units (DC PDUs): Intelligent DC PDUs will provide regulated DC voltage distribution, overcurrent protection, and remote monitoring capabilities within DC microgrids, enhancing reliability and manageability.
2.  DC-Based Energy Management System (DC-EMS): This system optimizes energy consumption and distribution within DC-powered facilities, utilizing real-time data to minimize waste and improve overall efficiency.
3.  DC-Native Load Balancing Algorithm: A software solution to dynamically allocate power across different DC loads to prevent overload and ensure equitable distribution, particularly crucial for complex industrial facilities.
4.  DC-Compatible Lighting Control System: Enables granular control and optimization of DC lighting systems (LEDs, etc.) within buildings and ports, maximizing energy savings and improving user comfort.
5.  DC Fault Location, Isolation, and Restoration (FLIR) System: A critical safety feature for large DC grids, automatically identifies, isolates, and restores power after a fault, minimizing downtime and improving resilience.
