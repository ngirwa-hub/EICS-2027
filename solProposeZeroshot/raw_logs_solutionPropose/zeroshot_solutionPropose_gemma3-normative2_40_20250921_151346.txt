Okay, considering the Shift2DC project's goals and the sectors of ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, here are five additional DC solutions not included in the earlier list, formatted as requested:

1.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer System: Enables contactless power delivery within a DC microgrid, beneficial for mobile equipment in ports and industrial settings, and for powering sensors/devices in buildings.
2.  DC Fault Current Limiter: Proactively limits fault currents in DC grids, preventing damage to equipment and enhancing safety compared to relying solely on circuit breakers.
3.  DC Energy Management System (EMS): A sophisticated software platform for real-time monitoring, control, and optimization of DC microgrids, particularly valuable for complex environments like data centers and industrial facilities.
4.  Bi-directional DC Power Sharing Algorithm: A control algorithm that dynamically balances power distribution amongst multiple DC sources (e.g., solar, batteries) to improve overall efficiency and resilience.
5.  DC-Native Lighting Control System: A lighting system designed from the ground up to operate directly on DC power, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and enabling more advanced control strategies within buildings.
