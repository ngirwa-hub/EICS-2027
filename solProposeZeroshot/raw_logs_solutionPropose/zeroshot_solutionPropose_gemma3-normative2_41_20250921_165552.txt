Okay, considering the Shift2DC project's goals and the sectors of focus (ports, industry, data centers, and buildings), and avoiding the previously listed solutions, here are five additional DC solutions that I believe would be valuable:

1.  DC Power Distribution Units (DC PDUs): Intelligent DC PDUs would manage and distribute power within DC microgrids, providing load balancing, remote monitoring, and fault isolation capabilities.
2.  DC-Native HVAC Systems: Utilizing DC motors and compressors in heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems to eliminate AC-DC-AC conversion losses, significantly improving energy efficiency in buildings and data centers.
3.  DC-Powered Industrial Robotics Charging Infrastructure: A specialized DC charging system optimized for the increasing number of DC-powered robots in industrial settings, minimizing downtime and maximizing operational efficiency.
4.  DC-Based Shore Power for Ports: A DC shore power system allows docked vessels to draw power directly from the port’s DC microgrid, reducing AC conversion losses and supporting electrification of port operations.
5.  DC-Enabled LED Lighting Control Systems: A sophisticated lighting control system leveraging DC power distribution to optimize lighting levels, reduce energy consumption, and enable advanced sensor integration within buildings.
