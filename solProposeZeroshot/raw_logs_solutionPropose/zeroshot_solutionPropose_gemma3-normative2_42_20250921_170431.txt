Okay, considering the Shift2DC project's goals and the four target sectors (ports, industry, data centers, buildings), and avoiding the previously listed solutions, here are five additional DC solutions I believe would be valuable.

1.  DC Power Distribution Units (DC PDUs): Intelligent DC PDUs manage and distribute DC power within facilities, providing monitoring, control, and fault isolation capabilities for enhanced safety and efficiency.
2.  DC-Native Lighting Systems: Direct DC lighting fixtures eliminate AC-DC conversion at the point of use, improving energy efficiency and reducing heat generation in buildings and industrial spaces.
3.  DC-Based Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: DC fast charging systems for ports and industrial facilities, simplifying charging processes and reducing infrastructure complexity.
4.  DC-Optimized Motor Drives: Specifically designed DC motor drives for industrial equipment and port machinery, offering improved efficiency and precise control compared to traditional AC motor setups.
5.  DC Microgrid Energy Management System (EMS): A sophisticated EMS designed to optimally manage DC microgrids, incorporating real-time data analysis and control algorithms for peak performance and resilience.
