2.  DC-Based Microgrid Management System: A software platform that intelligently manages and optimizes DC microgrid operation, prioritizing renewable energy and enabling load balancing across different sectors (industry, buildings).
3.  DC-Native Power Electronics for Industrial Processes: Specialized DC power converters tailored for specific industrial processes (e.g., electrolysis, metal refining) to maximize efficiency and reduce environmental impact.
4.  DC-Enabled Lighting Control System: Allows for seamless integration of LED lighting directly into DC microgrids, enabling advanced control features and energy harvesting capabilities within buildings.
5.  Bi-Directional DC Power Transfer System: Facilitates the sharing of DC power between buildings, ports, or industrial facilities, improving resilience and reducing overall energy costs.
