Okay, considering the Shift2DC project’s goals and the sectors of ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, here are five additional DC solutions, formatted as requested and not overlapping with the previously listed items.

1.  DC-Native Load Management System: Optimizes energy consumption within DC microgrids by prioritizing DC loads and dynamically adjusting power distribution based on real-time demand and renewable energy availability.
2.  DC-Based Microgrid Energy Trading Platform: Facilitates peer-to-peer energy exchange within DC microgrids, enabling businesses and buildings to buy and sell excess DC power and promoting local energy resilience.
3.  DC Fault Current Limiter: A device designed to reduce the magnitude of fault currents in DC systems, improving safety and allowing for reduced cable sizing and overall cost savings.
4.  DC Power Quality Monitoring & Diagnostics: A system providing continuous monitoring of DC power quality parameters (voltage, ripple, harmonics) and providing diagnostic information to identify and address potential issues proactively.
5.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) System: Enables contactless power transfer within buildings and ports, reducing cabling complexity and improving flexibility, particularly useful for charging electric vehicles or powering mobile equipment.
