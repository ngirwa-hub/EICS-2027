2.  Bi-directional DC Charging Infrastructure: Facilitates vehicle-to-grid (V2G) and building-to-vehicle power flow, enabling energy sharing and grid stabilization.

5.  DC Harmonic Mitigation Filters: Specialized filters to reduce harmonic distortion in DC circuits, essential for maintaining power quality and equipment longevity.
