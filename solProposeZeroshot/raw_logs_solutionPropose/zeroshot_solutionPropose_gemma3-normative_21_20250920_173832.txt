Okay, considering the Shift2DC project's focus and the provided constraints, here are five additional DC solutions that could be beneficial for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and were not on the earlier list. I'm focusing on aspects that address practical implementation challenges and broader system integration.

1.  DC Power Distribution Units (DPDUs): Intelligent DC distribution units that manage power delivery, monitor voltage and current, and provide remote control capabilities within DC microgrids.
2.  DC-Based Energy Management Systems (DC-EMS): Software platforms designed specifically for optimizing energy usage and managing load balancing in DC powered facilities, enabling real-time adjustments based on demand and renewable generation.
3.  DC Harmonic Mitigation Filters: Specialized filters designed to reduce harmonic distortion in DC circuits, ensuring power quality and preventing interference with sensitive equipment, particularly important for industrial applications.
4.  DC-Native Load Banks: Devices enabling controlled discharge of DC power for testing and commissioning of DC power systems, crucial for ensuring reliability and performance in ports and data centers.
5.  Bi-Directional DC Contactors: Contactors allowing for controlled charging and discharging of energy storage systems (batteries, supercapacitors) within a DC microgrid, enabling greater flexibility and grid services capability in buildings and ports.
