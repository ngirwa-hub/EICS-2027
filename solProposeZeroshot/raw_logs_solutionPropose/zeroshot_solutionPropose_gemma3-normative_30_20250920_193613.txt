Okay, considering the Shift2DC project's goals and the sectors it targets, here are five additional DC solutions not included in the previous list, formatted as requested:

2.  DC-Native Energy Management Systems (DC-EMS): A DC-EMS optimizes energy use by directly monitoring and controlling DC loads and generation, avoiding AC conversion losses and improving overall efficiency in buildings and industry.
3.  DC-Based Microgrid Controllers: Specialized controllers would manage the complex interactions within DC microgrids, ensuring stability, optimizing resource allocation, and facilitating grid services in ports and data centers.
4.  DC-Optimized Lighting Systems: Integrated lighting systems designed specifically for DC operation minimize conversion losses and enhance the controllability and integration of lighting within DC powered buildings.
5.  DC-Compatible Motor Drives: Motor drives designed for direct DC operation, particularly for industrial applications, improve efficiency and reduce the size and cost of industrial equipment.
