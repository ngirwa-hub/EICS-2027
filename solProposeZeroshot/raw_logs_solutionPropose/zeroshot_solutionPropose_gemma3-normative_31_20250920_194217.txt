Okay, acknowledging the Shift2DC project’s focus and the previous list, here are five additional DC solutions, formatted as requested, and specifically tailored to enhance the adoption of DC technologies in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, while avoiding repetition of items already proposed.

1.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer System: Facilitates contactless energy delivery within industrial facilities or ports, reducing cabling and improving safety, particularly useful for automated guided vehicles (AGVs) and robotic systems.
2.  Dynamic DC Load Balancing Algorithm:  Optimizes DC power distribution across multiple loads within a DC microgrid, ensuring efficient energy usage and minimizing voltage droop, especially beneficial in data centers with variable power demands.
3.  DC Grid Cybersecurity Framework:  Establishes protocols and tools to safeguard DC microgrids from cyber threats, including access control, data encryption, and intrusion detection systems, crucial for protecting critical infrastructure in ports and industry.
4.  DC-Native Building Energy Management System:  A control system specifically designed for DC buildings, optimizing energy consumption based on real-time occupancy, weather conditions, and equipment load, enhancing overall building efficiency.
5.  Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDU): Standardized, scalable DC power distribution units allowing for easy expansion and reconfiguration of DC power networks in data centers and industrial settings, improving flexibility and maintainability.
