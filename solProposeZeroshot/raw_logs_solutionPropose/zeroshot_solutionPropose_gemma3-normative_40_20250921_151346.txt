1.  DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure: Standardized, high-power DC charging stations directly connected to DC microgrids, reducing AC conversion losses and supporting bidirectional power flow. *Especially relevant for ports (ship charging) and buildings (fleet vehicle depots).*

2.  DC-Native Lighting Control Systems: Intelligent lighting systems designed to operate directly on DC power, minimizing energy losses and enabling more sophisticated control strategies like dynamic brightness adjustment based on occupancy and daylight harvesting. *Applies to all sectors, but particularly beneficial for buildings and data centers where lighting consumes significant power.*

3.  DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These PDUs would eliminate AC-DC conversions at the point of load within industrial facilities and data centers, improving efficiency and simplifying power management. *Critical for industry and data centers with high power density.*

4.  DC-Coupled Microgrid Management Software: A software platform to optimize the operation of DC microgrids, enabling real-time monitoring, control, and automated load balancing across multiple DC sources and loads. *Essential for coordinated operation in all four sectors.*

5.  DC-Compatible Modular UPS Systems:  Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) systems designed to deliver DC power directly, avoiding the double conversion common in AC-based UPS systems, significantly improving efficiency and reducing heat generation. *Crucial for data centers and industrial processes requiring high reliability.*
