Okay, considering the Shift2DC project's focus and avoiding the previously listed solutions, here are five additional DC solutions I believe would be valuable for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.

1.  DC Power Distribution Units (DC PDUs): Intelligent DC PDUs would manage and distribute power within DC microgrids, providing monitoring, control, and surge protection for connected devices. They're essential for organized and safe DC power delivery.
2.  DC Fault Current Limiting Devices: These devices would actively manage and limit DC fault currents, enhancing safety and protecting equipment in DC systems, especially crucial in high-power industrial and port environments.
3.  DC-Native Lighting Systems: Direct DC-powered LED lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion losses in buildings and reduces energy consumption, contributing to improved energy efficiency and lower operational costs.
4.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) Infrastructure: Enables wireless charging of electric vehicles, forklifts (in ports and industry), and other equipment, simplifying operations and reducing cable clutter.
5.  DC-Enabled Microgrid Management System (MGMS): An advanced software platform for monitoring, controlling, and optimizing DC microgrid operation across multiple sectors, including predictive maintenance and dynamic load balancing.
