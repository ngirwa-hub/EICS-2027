1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): These intelligent PDUs manage and distribute DC power within facilities, offering granular control, monitoring, and power quality improvements compared to traditional AC PDUs.
2. High-Efficiency DC Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC LEDs directly powered from a DC grid eliminates AC-DC conversion losses, improving overall energy efficiency in buildings and industrial settings.
3. DC-Native Motor Drives: Replacing AC motor drives with DC-native motors and drives, particularly for industrial applications, reduces conversion losses and improves system efficiency, especially for variable-speed applications.
4. DC-Coupled Solar Inverters with Battery Integration: Combining DC solar inverters directly coupled with DC battery storage allows for efficient energy storage and management within ports and data centers, minimizing AC conversion steps.
5. Dynamic Voltage Sag Mitigation via DC Microgrids: DC microgrids can be designed with energy storage and fast-acting DC-DC converters to rapidly compensate for voltage sags, crucial for sensitive equipment in data centers and industrial processes.
