1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent PDUs manage and distribute DC power within facilities, offering precise voltage regulation and load balancing capabilities. These are critical for optimizing energy use and reducing losses in DC systems.
2. Dynamic Voltage Sag Mitigation System: Utilizes DC-DC converters and energy storage to rapidly compensate for voltage sags and interruptions, ensuring process reliability in industrial and data center environments.
3. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Direct powering of LED lighting fixtures from a DC source eliminates AC-DC conversion, boosting overall system efficiency and reducing heat generation in buildings.
4. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) for Ports: Enables contactless charging of electric vehicles and equipment within port facilities, improving operational flexibility and eliminating cable clutter.
5. Modular DC Power Architectures: Offers scalable and adaptable DC power solutions by utilizing standardized DC power modules, facilitating easier integration and future expansion in data centers and industrial settings.
