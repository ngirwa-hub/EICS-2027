1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): DC PDUs would offer a more efficient and compact alternative to traditional AC PDUs in DC microgrids, minimizing conversion losses and improving power quality.
2. Modular DC Power Architectures: Standardized, modular DC power building blocks allow for scalable and flexible power distribution systems, easily adapted to changing load requirements in industrial or data center environments.
3. DC-Native UPS Systems: These uninterruptible power supplies would directly supply DC power, eliminating the double conversion inherent in AC-based UPS systems, particularly crucial for data center reliability.
4. DC-Compatible Lighting Systems: Moving beyond simple LED drivers, this encompasses integrated DC lighting systems that can be directly powered by a DC grid, reducing losses and improving overall efficiency in buildings.
5. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) Systems: Facilitating wireless power delivery within ports and industrial settings, enabling charging of electric vehicles and powering equipment without physical connections.
