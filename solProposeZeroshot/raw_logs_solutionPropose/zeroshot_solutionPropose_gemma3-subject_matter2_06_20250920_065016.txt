1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent PDUs manage and distribute DC power within buildings or industrial facilities, offering granular control and real-time monitoring of energy usage.
2. DC Microgrid Energy Management System (EMS): A centralized EMS optimizes energy flow within a DC microgrid, coordinating renewable generation, storage, and load management for increased efficiency and resilience.
3. DC-Native Load Banks: Devices used to test and verify the performance of DC power systems and generators, mimicking real-world load conditions without AC conversion.
4. Standardized DC Power Interface: A universal interface specification for DC power equipment, promoting interoperability and reducing integration costs across different manufacturers and applications.
5. DC-Optimized Lighting Systems: Lighting systems designed to operate directly from DC power sources, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and improving overall energy efficiency in buildings.
