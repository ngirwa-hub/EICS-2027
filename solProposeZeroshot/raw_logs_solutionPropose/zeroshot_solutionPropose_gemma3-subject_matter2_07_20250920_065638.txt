1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent DC PDUs manage power distribution within DC microgrids, providing monitoring, control, and load balancing capabilities. These units can incorporate surge protection and remote diagnostics.
2. Dynamic DC Voltage Sag Mitigation: A system utilizing supercapacitors or fast-responding DC-DC converters to actively compensate for voltage sags in DC grids, crucial for sensitive equipment in data centers and industrial processes.
3. DC-Native Modular UPS: A modular uninterruptible power supply (UPS) designed specifically for DC systems, offering improved efficiency and scalability compared to traditional AC-based UPS solutions, particularly beneficial for data centers.
4. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) for Ports: Utilizing WPT technology to transmit power wirelessly to equipment within port facilities, such as automated guided vehicles (AGVs) or charging stations, reducing cabling and improving flexibility.
5. DC-Compatible Energy Management System (EMS): An EMS specifically designed to optimize energy usage within DC microgrids, integrating with renewable energy sources, storage, and loads for enhanced efficiency and cost savings across all sectors.
