1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent DC PDUs manage power distribution within DC microgrids, providing monitoring, control, and fault isolation capabilities. They are critical for scalability and reliability in large DC systems.
2. DC-Native Energy Management Systems (EMS): A DC-native EMS optimizes energy consumption and generation within DC environments, directly managing DC loads and renewable sources without AC conversion, maximizing efficiency.
3. Dynamic Voltage Sag Mitigation via DC Storage: Utilizes DC energy storage to rapidly inject power during voltage sags, enhancing the reliability of DC-powered equipment, especially important for sensitive industrial processes.
4. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) for Mobile Equipment: Enables charging of mobile equipment like Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs) and robots within ports and industrial settings, eliminating cable clutter and improving efficiency.
5. DC-Coupled Microgrid Controller with Predictive Analytics: A sophisticated controller utilizing machine learning to forecast energy demand and optimize DC microgrid operation, improving stability and reducing energy waste across all four application sectors.
