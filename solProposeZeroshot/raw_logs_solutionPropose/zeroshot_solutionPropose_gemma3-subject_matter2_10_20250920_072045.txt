1. DC-Based Power Router: Dynamically distributes DC power between multiple sources (solar, storage, grid) to optimize efficiency and reliability within a DC microgrid.
2. DC-Native Load Balancing System: Utilizes real-time DC voltage and current measurements to intelligently manage and balance the load across different branches of a DC grid, preventing overloads and voltage drops.
3. Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Standardized, scalable PDUs with integrated DC power management and monitoring capabilities for simplified deployment and maintenance in industrial and data center environments.
4. DC Fault Current Limiter: Reduces fault currents in DC grids, minimizing equipment damage and improving system resilience, especially crucial for port and industrial applications with potentially high fault scenarios.
5. Wireless DC Power Transfer System: Enables contactless power delivery for mobile equipment and devices within ports and buildings, enhancing flexibility and reducing cabling complexity.
