1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent PDUs that manage and distribute DC power within facilities, offering real-time monitoring, load balancing, and remote control capabilities.
2. Bidirectional DC Charging Infrastructure: DC fast charging stations capable of both charging electric vehicles and providing grid stabilization services through vehicle-to-grid (V2G) functionality.
3. DC-Native Energy Management Systems (EMS): EMS platforms specifically designed for DC microgrids, offering advanced control algorithms and predictive analytics to optimize energy usage and improve system resilience.
4. DC-Optimized LED Lighting Systems: Lighting systems that operate directly from DC power, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and enabling dimming and control features at the source.
5. Modular DC Power Blocks: Standardized, prefabricated DC power blocks that can be easily combined and scaled to meet diverse power requirements in industrial and data center environments.
