1. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) System: Facilitates power transmission without physical cables, ideal for powering equipment in ports and industrial settings with challenging layouts or hazardous environments. This utilizes resonant inductive coupling to transfer power efficiently.
2. Dynamic DC Voltage Sag Mitigation: A system employing supercapacitors or fast-responding DC-DC converters to instantly compensate for voltage sags in DC grids, critical for sensitive equipment in data centers and industrial processes.
3. DC-Native Power Electronics for Lighting: Directly powers LED lighting systems using DC voltage, bypassing inefficient AC-DC conversion and reducing energy consumption in buildings and ports.
4. Distributed DC-Based Reactive Power Compensation: Utilizes DC-DC converters to provide reactive power support locally within DC grids, improving power quality and reducing losses in industrial facilities and data centers.
5. DC-Based Microgrid Controller with Predictive Capabilities: An advanced control system that anticipates load fluctuations and optimizes power flow within DC microgrids, particularly beneficial for ports and buildings with variable energy demands.
