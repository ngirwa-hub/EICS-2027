1. DC-Native Load Management System: A software platform optimizing DC power distribution based on real-time load profiles and renewable energy availability, reducing waste and improving efficiency.
2. Dynamic DC Voltage Regulation: A system utilizing advanced control algorithms and power electronics to maintain stable DC voltage levels despite fluctuating renewable generation and load variations.
3. DC Power Quality Monitoring & Diagnostics: A suite of sensors and analytics tools for continuous monitoring of DC power quality parameters (voltage, current, harmonics) to proactively identify and address issues.
4. Standardized DC Equipment Interoperability Protocol: A communication protocol ensuring seamless data exchange and control between diverse DC equipment from different manufacturers.
5. Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Scalable and configurable PDUs allowing for flexible allocation of DC power based on evolving load demands in industrial or data center environments.
