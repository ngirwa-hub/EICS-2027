1. DC-Based Energy Management System: A centralized control system optimizing energy flow and load balancing within DC microgrids, leveraging real-time data and predictive algorithms for enhanced efficiency.
2. Wireless DC Power Transfer System: Enables contactless power delivery to devices within a DC environment, beneficial for ports (cranes, AGVs) and buildings (IoT devices) by reducing cabling complexity and improving safety.
3. DC Fault Current Limiter: A device to restrict the magnitude of fault currents in DC grids, protecting equipment and improving system resilience, particularly important in densely populated industrial settings.
4. DC Power Quality Monitoring & Correction: Hardware and software to actively monitor and correct voltage sags, swells, and harmonic distortions in DC systems, ensuring stable operation of sensitive equipment in data centers and buildings.
5. Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDU): Standardized, scalable PDU’s designed for DC grids, allowing for easy expansion and reconfiguration of power distribution in industrial and data center environments.
