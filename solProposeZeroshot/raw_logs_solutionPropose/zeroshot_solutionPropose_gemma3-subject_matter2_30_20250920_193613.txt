1. DC-Based Energy Management System: A centralized control system optimizing DC power distribution, load balancing, and renewable energy integration within DC microgrids, leveraging real-time data for efficiency.
2. DC-Native Power Electronics for Variable Speed Drives: Replacing traditional AC variable frequency drives with DC-DC converters directly coupled to DC motors, increasing efficiency and reducing harmonic distortion in industrial processes.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicle Fleets: Deploying high-power DC fast charging stations specifically designed for port and industrial electric vehicle fleets, minimizing charging time and grid impact.
4. DC-Coupled LED Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC power directly for LED lighting in buildings and ports, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and enabling advanced lighting control strategies.
5. Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDU): Providing scalable and adaptable DC power distribution within data centers and industrial facilities, allowing for flexible load management and redundancy.
