1. DC-Based Microgrid Management System: A software platform utilizing advanced algorithms to optimize energy flow, manage distributed generation, and ensure stable operation within DC microgrids across various sectors.
2. High-Efficiency DC Power Distribution Units (PDU): These PDUs minimize losses during DC power distribution within data centers and industrial facilities by employing optimized topologies and low-loss components.
3. DC-Native Load Banks: Load banks designed specifically for DC systems allow for comprehensive testing and validation of DC power generation and distribution equipment, ensuring reliable operation.
4. DC-Compatible Energy Metering Infrastructure: Advanced metering systems capable of accurately measuring DC power flow and quality, essential for billing, performance monitoring, and grid management in DC environments.
5. Modular DC Building Energy Management System: A scalable and customizable system for controlling and optimizing DC power usage in buildings, integrating renewable sources and storage.
