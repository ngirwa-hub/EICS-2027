1. DC-Based Energy Management System: A centralized system utilizing DC power for monitoring, control, and optimization of energy consumption across the targeted sectors, enabling real-time adjustments for efficiency.
2. Bidirectional DC Power Transfer System: Allows for flexible energy sharing between different DC microgrids within a port or industrial complex, maximizing renewable energy utilization and reducing reliance on external power sources.
3. DC-Native Load Balancing Algorithm: A sophisticated algorithm specifically designed to manage power distribution within a DC grid, prioritizing critical loads and optimizing overall system efficiency in data centers or buildings.
4. Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Standardized, scalable PDUs offering flexible voltage and current configurations, simplifying DC power distribution in industrial facilities and adaptable to changing load requirements.
5. DC-Optimized Lighting and HVAC Systems: Direct integration of lighting and HVAC systems with DC power eliminates AC-DC conversion losses, significantly improving energy efficiency in buildings and data centers.
