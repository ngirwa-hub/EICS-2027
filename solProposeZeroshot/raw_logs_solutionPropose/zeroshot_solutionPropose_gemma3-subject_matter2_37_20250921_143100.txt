1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent PDUs designed for DC microgrids, providing voltage regulation, power monitoring, and load balancing within DC systems.
2. DC-Native Energy Management Systems (EMS): EMS platforms specifically designed to optimize energy usage and manage distributed renewable generation within DC environments, offering advanced control and forecasting capabilities.

5. Bidirectional DC Power Transfer Systems: Enables power sharing between buildings or industrial facilities within a DC microgrid, allowing for optimized resource utilization and increased resilience.
