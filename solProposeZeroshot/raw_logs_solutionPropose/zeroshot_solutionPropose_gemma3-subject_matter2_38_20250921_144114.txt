1. DC-Based Microgrid Controller: A centralized or distributed control system specifically designed for DC microgrids, optimizing energy flow, managing distributed generation, and ensuring grid stability through real-time adjustments.
2. DC Power Quality Monitoring System: Continuous monitoring of DC voltage, current, harmonics, and transient events to identify and mitigate power quality issues within DC systems, improving reliability and equipment longevity.
3. Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDU): Standardized, scalable DC PDUs offering flexible power delivery options, remote monitoring, and intelligent load management within buildings and industrial facilities.
4. DC-Native Energy Management System (EMS): An EMS specifically designed for DC environments, enabling sophisticated energy optimization strategies, demand response programs, and integration with renewable energy sources.
5. DC-Optimized Lighting Systems: LED lighting fixtures and control systems designed to operate directly from DC power sources, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and enhancing energy efficiency in buildings and ports.
