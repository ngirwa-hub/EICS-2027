1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): DC PDUs offer a streamlined power distribution system within DC microgrids, reducing conversion losses and simplifying management compared to traditional AC PDUs. They incorporate intelligent monitoring and control features for enhanced efficiency.
2. Dynamic DC Voltage Sag Mitigation: A system employing fast-acting DC voltage sags mitigation, using capacitors and controllable DC sources, to ensure stable DC voltage during transient loads, particularly critical for sensitive equipment in data centers and industrial processes.
3. DC-Native Load Banks: Load banks designed specifically for testing and commissioning DC power systems, allowing for realistic simulations of load conditions without the need for AC-DC conversion and associated losses.
4. Bidirectional DC Power Transfer System: A system that enables efficient power sharing and energy transfer between different DC microgrids or sources, optimizing resource utilization and enhancing resilience in port and industrial settings.
5. DC-Based Microgrid Energy Management System (EMS): An EMS tailored for DC microgrids that intelligently manages energy generation, storage, and consumption, optimizing for efficiency, reliability, and cost-effectiveness across all application sectors.
