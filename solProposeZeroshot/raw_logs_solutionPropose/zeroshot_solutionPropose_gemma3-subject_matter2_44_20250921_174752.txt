1. DC-Based Microgrid Management System: A software platform for real-time monitoring, control, and optimization of DC microgrids, incorporating predictive analytics for renewable energy integration and load balancing.
2. Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Standardized, scalable DC PDUs with integrated metering and remote control capabilities to simplify installation and management within industrial and data center environments.
3. DC Fast Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles (Ports & Industry): High-power DC charging stations specifically designed for port operations and industrial facilities, minimizing charging times and supporting heavy-duty vehicle electrification.
4. DC-Native Lighting Control Systems (Buildings): Lighting control systems designed to operate directly on DC power, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and enabling advanced dimming and color control features.
5. Dynamic Voltage Sag Mitigation via DC Energy Storage (Data Centers & Industry): A system utilizing DC energy storage to rapidly compensate for voltage sags, improving the reliability of sensitive equipment in data centers and industrial processes.
