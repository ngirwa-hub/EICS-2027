1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): DC PDUs manage and distribute DC power within facilities, offering enhanced efficiency and reduced cabling compared to traditional AC PDUs. They incorporate intelligent monitoring and control capabilities.
2. Dynamic Voltage Sag Mitigation via DC Storage: DC-based energy storage systems can rapidly inject power to counteract voltage sags, crucial for sensitive industrial equipment and data center loads. This avoids process disruptions and improves power quality.
3. DC-Native Load Balancing Algorithms: Software solutions optimizing power distribution across DC microgrids, prioritizing critical loads and minimizing losses, particularly beneficial in data centers and industrial settings with diverse power demands.
4. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) for Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs): Implementing WPT eliminates the need for physical contacts in ports and industrial environments, improving AGV efficiency and reducing maintenance.
5. DC-Coupled Photovoltaic (PV) Systems with Integrated Battery Storage: Direct coupling of PV arrays to DC storage eliminates AC conversion losses, maximizing self-consumption and grid independence, ideal for buildings and industrial facilities.
