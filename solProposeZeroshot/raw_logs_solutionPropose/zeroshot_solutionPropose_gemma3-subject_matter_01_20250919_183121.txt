1. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT) System: Enables contactless power delivery within buildings and ports, reducing cabling needs and improving safety in hazardous environments. This utilizes resonant inductive coupling for efficient power transmission.
2. Distributed DC-Powered Lighting Control System: A low-voltage DC system for lighting management in buildings and data centers, reducing energy consumption and enabling granular control through intelligent dimming and scheduling.
3. DC-Native Power Electronics for Industrial Motors: Replacing traditional AC motor drives with DC-native inverters to directly interface with DC microgrids, increasing efficiency and enabling bidirectional power flow.
4. DC-Coupled Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) Orchestration Platform: A software platform for managing and optimizing the operation of multiple DC-coupled BESS units across a port or industrial facility, maximizing grid services and resilience.
5. DC-Based Microgrid Islanding and Black Start Capability: A system that allows a DC microgrid to automatically isolate from the main grid and continue operating during grid disturbances, crucial for port and data center reliability.
