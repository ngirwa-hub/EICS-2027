1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent DC PDUs manage power distribution within DC microgrids, providing monitoring, control, and load balancing capabilities.
2. Bidirectional DC Charging Infrastructure: Enables electric vehicles (particularly relevant for ports and industry) to both draw power from and supply power back to the DC grid, supporting Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) functionality.
3. DC-Native Lighting Systems: Replacing traditional AC-powered lighting with DC-native LED lighting reduces conversion losses and improves overall energy efficiency in buildings and data centers.
4. DC-Based Thermal Management Systems: Utilizing DC power for cooling and heating systems in data centers and industrial facilities increases efficiency and reduces reliance on AC conversion.
5. Dynamic Voltage Sag Compensation (DVSC) for DC Microgrids: Provides rapid voltage support during disturbances in DC microgrids, improving power quality and preventing equipment downtime in critical applications like data centers and ports.
