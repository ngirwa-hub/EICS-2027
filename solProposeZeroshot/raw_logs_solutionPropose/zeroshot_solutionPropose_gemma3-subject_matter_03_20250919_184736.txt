1. DC-Based Power Router: Dynamically allocates DC power from various renewable sources (solar, wind) and storage to different loads within a port, industry, data center, or building, optimizing efficiency and resilience.
2. DC Fault Current Limiter: Reduces the magnitude of fault currents in DC systems, mitigating damage to equipment and improving safety, particularly important in large industrial and port applications.
3. DC System Harmonic Filter: Addresses harmonic distortion introduced by non-linear DC loads (e.g., LED lighting, variable speed drives) to maintain power quality and prevent equipment malfunction in buildings and data centers.
4. DC Microgrid Controller with Predictive Analytics: Utilizes machine learning algorithms to forecast load and renewable generation, enabling proactive management of DC microgrids for enhanced reliability and reduced operational costs across all sectors.
5. Standardized DC Equipment Interoperability Protocol: Defines a communication protocol allowing diverse DC equipment from different manufacturers to seamlessly integrate and operate within a DC system, crucial for scalability and adoption in all four sectors.
