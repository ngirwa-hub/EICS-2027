1. DC-Based Microgrid Controller: A centralized or distributed control system specifically designed to manage DC microgrids, optimizing energy flow, load balancing, and renewable integration with real-time data analysis.
2. Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Standardized, scalable PDUs for distributing DC power within buildings and industrial facilities, facilitating flexible system expansion and simplifying maintenance.
3. DC-Native Energy Management System (EMS): An EMS designed from the ground up for DC systems, capable of advanced forecasting, demand response, and grid services leveraging DC characteristics for improved efficiency.
4. High-Efficiency DC Lighting Systems: LED lighting solutions directly powered by DC, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and reducing overall energy consumption in buildings and ports.
5. DC-Coupled Renewable Energy Forecasting: A forecasting system tailored for DC renewable sources (e.g., solar PV arrays) providing precise power output predictions to optimize DC grid operation and stability.
