1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent PDUs manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering load balancing, remote monitoring, and surge protection.
2. DC-Native Energy Management Systems (EMS): An EMS specifically designed for DC grids optimizes energy usage, predicts load profiles, and integrates renewable energy sources efficiently.
3. Dynamic Voltage Sag Mitigation: A system using DC-DC converters to rapidly compensate for voltage sags, crucial for sensitive industrial equipment and data center loads.
4. DC-Based Microgrid Control System: Centralized control software manages distributed DC generation, storage, and loads within a port or industrial park, optimizing energy flow and resilience.
5. High-Efficiency DC Lighting Systems: LED lighting directly powered by DC eliminates AC-DC conversion losses, improving energy efficiency in buildings and reducing heat generation.
