1. DC-Native Load Management System: Optimizes DC power distribution within facilities by dynamically adjusting power delivery to loads based on real-time demand and available generation, minimizing losses and improving efficiency.
2. DC-Based Microgrid Controller: A sophisticated control system managing multiple DC sources (solar, wind, storage) and loads within a microgrid, enabling autonomous operation and grid services.
3. Standardized DC Power Delivery Units (PDU): Modular, pre-engineered DC power delivery units offering scalable power solutions for buildings and data centers, promoting ease of deployment and interoperability.
4. DC-Optimized Lighting Systems: LED lighting directly powered by DC sources, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and potentially enabling more advanced lighting control strategies.
5. High-Efficiency DC Power Factor Correction: A device that actively corrects power factor in DC systems, reducing harmonic distortion and improving overall system efficiency, particularly important for industrial applications.
