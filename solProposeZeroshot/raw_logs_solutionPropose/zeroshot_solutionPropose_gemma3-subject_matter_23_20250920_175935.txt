1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent PDUs manage DC power distribution within facilities, offering dynamic load balancing and remote monitoring capabilities. These PDUs can incorporate energy metering and power quality correction functionalities.
2. High-Efficiency DC Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC LEDs directly powered from a DC grid eliminates AC-DC conversion losses inherent in traditional lighting, improving overall energy efficiency in buildings and industrial settings.
3. DC-Native Power Electronics for Industrial Processes: Replacing traditional AC-powered equipment with DC-native alternatives in industrial processes, such as motor drives and welding, reduces conversion losses and improves system efficiency.
4. DC-Based Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS): DC-UPS systems bypass the AC-DC-AC conversion steps, providing faster response times and improved efficiency for critical loads in data centers and ports.
5. Dynamic DC Voltage Sag Mitigation: A system utilizing supercapacitors or batteries to rapidly compensate for voltage sags in DC grids, ensuring stable operation for sensitive equipment in data centers and industrial processes.
