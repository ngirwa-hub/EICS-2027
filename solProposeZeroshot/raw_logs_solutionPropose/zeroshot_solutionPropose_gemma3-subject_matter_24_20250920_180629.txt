1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent DC PDUs manage power distribution within DC microgrids, providing monitoring, control, and load balancing capabilities.
2. DC-Native Energy Management Systems (EMS): A specialized EMS designed for DC grids, optimizing energy usage, integrating renewable sources, and providing real-time grid monitoring and control.
3. DC-Based Microgrid Controller: Software and hardware that coordinates distributed DC resources, manages power flow, and ensures stable operation of DC microgrids within ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.
4. DC Fault Current Limiting Devices: Devices that automatically limit fault currents in DC grids, reducing stress on equipment and improving safety, crucial for high-voltage DC systems in ports and industry.
5. Bidirectional DC Charging Infrastructure: Enables efficient charging and discharging of electric vehicles or energy storage systems directly within DC microgrids, suitable for ports, industry, and buildings.
