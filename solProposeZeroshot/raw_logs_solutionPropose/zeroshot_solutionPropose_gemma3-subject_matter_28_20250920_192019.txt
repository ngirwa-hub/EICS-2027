1. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer (WPT): Enables power delivery without physical cables, useful for charging electric vehicles in ports or powering equipment in industrial settings where cabling is difficult. Efficiency needs careful optimization to minimize losses.
2. Dynamic DC Line Rating (DCLR) System: Utilizes real-time data (temperature, weather) to optimize the current carrying capacity of DC transmission lines, maximizing throughput and reducing congestion in port and industrial applications.
3. DC Fault Current Limiter (FCL): Provides a controlled reduction of fault currents in DC grids, protecting equipment and improving system reliability, particularly relevant in large industrial and data center environments.
4. Bidirectional DC Power Sharing Controller: Facilitates seamless power exchange between multiple DC sources and loads, optimizing energy utilization and enhancing resilience in buildings and data centers with diverse renewable generation.
5. DC Microgrid Energy Management System (EMS): An advanced control system for DC microgrids that optimizes energy flow, balances supply and demand, and integrates renewable energy sources, critical for efficient operation in all four sectors.
