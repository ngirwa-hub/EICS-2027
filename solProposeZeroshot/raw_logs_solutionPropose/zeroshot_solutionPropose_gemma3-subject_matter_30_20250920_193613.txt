1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): DC PDUs offer a more efficient and compact alternative to traditional AC PDUs within DC microgrids, reducing conversion losses and space requirements. They incorporate intelligent power management and monitoring capabilities.
2. Dynamic Voltage Sag Compensation (DVSC) for DC Microgrids: DVSC devices rapidly inject DC power to mitigate voltage sags caused by fluctuating loads or renewable generation, ensuring stable operation of sensitive equipment in ports, data centers, and industry.
3. DC-Native Energy Management Systems (EMS): A DC-native EMS directly monitors and controls DC power flows, optimizing energy usage and integrating renewable sources without the inefficiencies of AC-DC-AC conversions, beneficial for buildings and data centers.
4. Modular DC Power Architectures: Utilizing standardized DC power modules allows for scalable and adaptable power systems, simplifying expansion and maintenance in industrial settings and ports with varying power demands.
5. Bidirectional DC Charging Infrastructure: Enables efficient charging and discharging of electric vehicles (EVs) within port facilities and industrial sites, allowing for vehicle-to-grid (V2G) capabilities and peak shaving.
