1. DC-Based Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent PDUs manage and distribute DC power within facilities, offering granular control and monitoring capabilities for efficient energy usage.
2. Dynamic Voltage Sag Mitigation System: A DC-based system employing supercapacitors or batteries to rapidly compensate for voltage sags, crucial for sensitive equipment in data centers and industrial processes.
3. DC-Native Load Balancing Algorithm: Software that optimizes power allocation across DC loads, minimizing cable losses and maximizing the utilization of available DC power within a building or port.
4. Modular DC Power Architectures: Pre-engineered, scalable DC power modules that simplify system design and deployment, particularly beneficial for data centers and industrial automation.
5. DC-Coupled Microgrid Controller: A sophisticated controller that manages the operation of a DC microgrid, coordinating renewable energy sources, storage, and loads for optimal performance in port or industrial settings.
