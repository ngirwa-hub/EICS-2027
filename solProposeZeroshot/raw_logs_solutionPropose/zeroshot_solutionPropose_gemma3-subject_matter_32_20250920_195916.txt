1. DC-Based Microgrid Controller: A centralized or distributed control system specifically designed for managing DC microgrids, optimizing energy flow and ensuring stability through advanced algorithms.
2. High-Efficiency DC Power Distribution Units (PDU): PDUs designed to distribute DC power within facilities with minimal losses, incorporating intelligent load balancing and remote monitoring capabilities.
3. DC-Native Energy Management System (EMS): An EMS tailored for DC environments, providing real-time data analysis, predictive maintenance, and automated responses to optimize energy consumption and reduce operational costs.
4. DC-Optimized Lighting Systems: LED lighting systems directly powered by DC sources, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and enhancing energy efficiency in buildings and industrial facilities.
5. Dynamic DC Load Shedding System: A system that automatically prioritizes and sheds non-critical DC loads during periods of high demand or grid instability, ensuring essential services remain operational.
