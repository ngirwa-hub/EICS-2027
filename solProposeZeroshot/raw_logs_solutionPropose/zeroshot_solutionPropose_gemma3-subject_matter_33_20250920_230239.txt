1. DC-Native Load Management System: Dynamically adjusts DC power delivery to loads based on real-time availability and demand, optimizing energy usage and reducing peak loads within a DC grid.
2. Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Standardized, scalable DC PDUs allow for flexible and adaptable power distribution within buildings and industrial facilities, simplifying installation and maintenance.
3. DC-Based Microgrid Controller: A sophisticated controller manages multiple distributed DC generation sources (solar, wind, fuel cells) and storage, optimizing power flow and ensuring grid stability in port and industrial settings.
4. High-Efficiency DC Lighting Systems: Utilizing DC LED lighting eliminates AC-DC conversion losses common in traditional systems, significantly reducing energy consumption in buildings and data centers.
5. DC-Coupled Solar Inverter with Reactive Power Control: Enables direct connection of solar PV to DC grids while providing dynamic reactive power compensation to improve power quality and grid stability.
