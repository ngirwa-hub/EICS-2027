1. DC-Based Microgrid Controller: A centralized or distributed control system optimized for DC microgrids, managing power flow, voltage regulation, and energy storage dispatch based on real-time conditions and demand response.
2. High-Efficiency DC Power Distribution Units (PDU): PDUs specifically designed for DC systems that minimize losses through optimized busbar design, low-resistance connections, and intelligent load balancing.
3. DC-Native Energy Management Systems (EMS): EMS tailored for DC environments, providing granular control and monitoring of DC power consumption, generation, and storage, facilitating energy efficiency and predictive maintenance.
4. DC-Compatible Lighting and Appliance Standards: Development of standardized DC lighting and appliance designs to reduce the need for AC-DC conversion and improve overall system efficiency in buildings.
5. Dynamic Voltage Sag Mitigation via DC Storage: Utilizing DC-coupled energy storage to rapidly compensate for voltage sags, critical for sensitive industrial processes and data center operations, enhancing reliability.
