Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and were not included in the earlier list:

1.  DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer System: Enables contactless power delivery within facilities, useful for automated guided vehicles (AGVs) in ports and industry, or powering sensors and devices in buildings.
2.  DC Fault Current Limiter: Provides a dynamic method to reduce fault currents in DC systems, improving safety and protecting equipment, particularly important in large industrial and port facilities.
3.  DC Microgrid Energy Management System (EMS): A software platform for optimizing energy flow, load balancing, and integrating renewable sources within DC microgrids, vital for data centers and buildings.
4.  DC Power Distribution Units (DC PDUs): Provide regulated DC voltage distribution and monitoring capabilities within buildings and industrial facilities, ensuring consistent power delivery.
5.  Bidirectional DC Charging Infrastructure: Supports efficient charging and discharging of electric vehicles (ports and industry) and energy storage systems within DC microgrids, enabling grid stabilization and peak shaving.
