Here are some additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, and were not included in the earlier list:

1. DC-Based Wireless Power Transfer System: Facilitates power delivery without physical cables, beneficial for automated guided vehicles (AGVs) in ports and industrial settings, and for powering devices in data centers and buildings.
2. DC-Native Load Banks: Provides a means to test and characterize DC power supplies and inverters, crucial for quality assurance and maintenance in industrial and port environments with large DC loads.
3. Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Enables scalable and flexible DC power distribution within buildings and data centers, adapting to changing load requirements and improving efficiency.
4. DC Microgrid Management System (MGMS): A software platform for real-time monitoring, control, and optimization of DC microgrids across various sectors, including ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.
5. DC-Optimized Lighting Systems: Utilizes DC lighting technologies (e.g., LED lighting) directly from the DC distribution network, eliminating AC-DC conversion losses and increasing overall system efficiency in buildings and ports.
