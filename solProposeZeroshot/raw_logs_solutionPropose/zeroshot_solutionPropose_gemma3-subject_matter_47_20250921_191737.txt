1. DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Intelligent PDUs manage and distribute DC power within a facility, offering features like remote monitoring, load balancing, and surge protection, crucial for reliable operation in ports, industry, and data centers.
2. High-Efficiency DC Lighting Systems: Replacing traditional AC lighting with DC-powered LEDs significantly reduces conversion losses and improves overall energy efficiency within buildings and industrial facilities.
3. DC-Native Motor Drives: Utilizing DC motors and drives directly powered by DC microgrids eliminates AC-DC-AC conversion, increasing efficiency and reducing harmonic distortion in industrial processes and port equipment.
4. Bidirectional DC Power Transfer Systems: Enables dynamic power sharing and flexibility between different DC microgrids or energy sources within ports, industry, and data centers, optimizing resource utilization.
5. DC Harmonic Mitigation Filters: Specialized filters to suppress harmonic currents generated by non-linear DC loads, ensuring power quality and preventing equipment damage in industrial and data center environments.
