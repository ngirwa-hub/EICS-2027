2. LED Lighting Systems: Energy-efficient DC lighting solutions for industrial settings and buildings, providing improved light quality and reduced energy consumption.
3. Electric Vehicle Charging Stations: A direct current fast charging system that enables efficient and rapid charging of electric vehicles at ports, reducing wait times and emissions.

5. High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Transmission: A technology that enables efficient long-distance transmission of renewable energy from remote locations, such as offshore wind farms or remote solar installations, to ports or other end-users.
