2. DC Microgrids for Ports: Establishing localized DC microgrids to manage energy distribution and supply within port facilities, reducing reliance on the main grid and minimizing energy losses.

3. Offshore Wind Integration: Incorporating offshore wind power into DC grids for data centers and buildings in coastal regions, providing renewable energy while mitigating transmission loss risks.

4. Smart Grid Control: Deploying advanced control systems that enable real-time monitoring, demand response, and energy management across the four sectors, optimizing energy usage and reducing overall costs.

5. Virtual Power Plant (VPP): Developing VPPs that combine renewable energy sources with DC storage for more efficient electricity production, balancing grid loads, and improving energy efficiency in data centers and buildings.
