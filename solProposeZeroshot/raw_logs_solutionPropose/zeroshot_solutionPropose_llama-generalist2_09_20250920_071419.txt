1. Solar Microgrids: A system that integrates solar panels and energy storage directly into a microgrid, providing reliable and sustainable power supply for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.
2. Wind Microgrids: A system that integrates wind turbines and energy storage directly into a microgrid, providing reliable and sustainable power supply for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.
3. Energy Management System (EMS): An intelligent control system that optimizes the use of DC technologies in port, industrial, data center, and building applications by monitoring energy consumption, controlling devices, and scheduling tasks.
4. Flexible DC Grids: A modular and adaptable grid architecture that allows for the integration of different DC technologies (such as solar panels, wind turbines, and energy storage systems) in a flexible way to suit specific applications in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.
5. High-efficiency DC Transformers: A device that efficiently steps down high-voltage DC power from renewable sources (such as solar panels or wind turbines) to lower voltage levels suitable for port, industrial, data center, and building applications, while minimizing energy losses.
