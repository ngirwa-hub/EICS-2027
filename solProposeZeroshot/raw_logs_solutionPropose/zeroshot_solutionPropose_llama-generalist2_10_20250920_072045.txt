2. Energy-Efficient DC Lighting System: A lighting system that uses direct current instead of alternating current, reducing energy consumption and costs in buildings.
   Explanation: This solution can reduce energy usage and costs in buildings while also promoting the use of DC technology.

3. High-Power DC Fast Chargers: A fast charging system for electric vehicles that uses direct current to provide faster and more efficient charging, particularly useful in ports and industry.
   Explanation: This solution can improve the efficiency and speed of electric vehicle charging, making it a viable option for transportation and logistics industries.

4. DC-Based HVAC System: A heating, ventilation, and air conditioning system that uses direct current technology to provide efficient and sustainable temperature control in buildings.
   Explanation: This solution can reduce energy consumption and costs in buildings while also promoting the use of DC technology for environmental benefits.

5. Off-grid DC Microgrid: A standalone power system that provides reliable and sustainable power to remote locations, such as ports or industry sites, using direct current technologies.
   Explanation: This solution can provide a reliable source of energy in areas where traditional grid infrastructure is unavailable or insufficient, promoting the use of DC technology for remote power generation.
