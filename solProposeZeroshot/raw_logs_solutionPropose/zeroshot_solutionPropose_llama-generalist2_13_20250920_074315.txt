1. Solar Power System Integration: Combining solar power systems with DC microgrids in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings for energy efficiency and cost reduction.
2. Wind Turbine Integration: Incorporating wind turbines into DC microgrids to generate renewable energy in ports, industrial applications, data centers, and building areas with high wind potential.
3. Electric Vehicle Charging Stations: Introducing fast-charging DC stations for electric vehicles at ports, parking lots, and garages to facilitate clean transportation and reduce emissions.
4. Building Automation System: Adopting a DC-based building automation system that optimizes energy use in buildings by monitoring and controlling lighting, HVAC, and other systems, leading to increased efficiency and reduced energy consumption.
5. Floating Solar Panels: Deploying floating solar panels at ports and industrial areas near water bodies for efficient energy generation while reducing land usage and visual pollution.
