1. Smart Ports DC Grid: A system that integrates DC grids with smart ports to optimize energy use and reduce greenhouse gas emissions in port operations.
2. Intelligent DC Industry Applications: A set of applications for the industrial sector using DC technologies, such as electric vehicles, robotics, and automation systems, to increase efficiency and reduce environmental impact.
3. Efficient Data Center DC System: A system that optimizes energy consumption in data centers by using high-efficiency DC power supplies, efficient cooling systems, and modular design for better scalability.
4. BuildingDC Solutions: A set of solutions for the building sector that focuses on reducing energy consumption through DC lighting systems, smart HVAC systems, and grid-connected battery storage for backup power.
5. DC Microgrids in Transportation: A system that integrates DC microgrids with electric vehicles, charging stations, and public transportation to reduce dependence on fossil fuels and promote sustainable mobility.
