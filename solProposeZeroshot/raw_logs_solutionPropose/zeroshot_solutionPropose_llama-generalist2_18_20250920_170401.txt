1. Solution Title: Smart Port Infrastructure
Explanation: A system that uses advanced DC technologies to optimize the energy consumption and efficiency in port infrastructures, such as warehouses, storage facilities, and transportation systems. This solution addresses the specific needs of ports by implementing efficient lighting systems, automated control mechanisms, and intelligent power distribution networks.

2. Solution Title: Modular DC Data Center
Explanation: A flexible and modular data center design that utilizes DC power systems for efficient cooling, power distribution, and energy management. This solution enables easy scaling of the data center capacity, reduces energy consumption and costs, and enhances resilience against power outages.

3. Solution Title: Building-Integrated DC System
Explanation: A system that integrates direct current technologies into building management systems, enabling efficient heating, cooling, lighting, and power distribution. This solution promotes energy savings, reduces energy waste, and ensures a comfortable indoor environment.

4. Solution Title: DC-Based Industrial Automation
Explanation: A set of advanced DC solutions for industrial automation, including smart sensors, actuators, and control systems. This solution optimizes the production process by reducing energy consumption and enhancing operational efficiency, ultimately leading to cost savings and improved competitiveness.

5. Solution Title: Smart DC Grid Connection
Explanation: A network of interconnected DC grids that enables efficient power distribution and management between various sectors, such as ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. This solution supports the integration of renewable energy sources, reduces transmission losses, and promotes a more sustainable energy system.
