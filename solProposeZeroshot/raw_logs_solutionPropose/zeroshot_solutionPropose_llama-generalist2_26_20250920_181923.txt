1. Solar Hybrid System: A combination of solar panels and conventional power sources such as gas or diesel generators, which can be controlled based on demand. This solution offers reliable power supply with reduced dependence on non-renewable sources in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.

2. Energy-efficient Lighting System: An LED lighting system that consumes less energy compared to traditional lighting systems. This solution can significantly reduce electricity consumption in buildings.

3. EV Charging Infrastructure: A network of charging stations for electric vehicles (EVs) powered by renewable energy sources such as solar or wind. This solution promotes the use of EVs and reduces dependence on fossil fuels in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.

4. Microgrid Control System: An advanced control system that optimizes energy management across multiple DC microgrids in a decentralized power system. This solution can improve grid resilience, reduce costs, and enhance overall efficiency for all sectors.

5. Building Management System (BMS): A digital tool to monitor and manage energy consumption in buildings, providing real-time data analysis to optimize energy use. This solution can help reduce energy waste and lower operational costs in buildings.
