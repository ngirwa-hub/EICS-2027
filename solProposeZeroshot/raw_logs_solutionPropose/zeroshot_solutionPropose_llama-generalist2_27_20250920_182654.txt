   - Title: DC Microgrids for Ports
   - Explanation: Ports require efficient and reliable power systems to support their operations. DC microgrids offer the potential for lower energy losses during conversion, leading to cost savings and improved grid stability.

   - Title: High-Efficiency DC Motors for Industry
   - Explanation: DC motors can offer higher efficiency and lower energy consumption compared to traditional AC induction motors, resulting in cost savings and improved sustainability for industrial processes.

3. DC Air Conditioning Systems for Data Centers: An innovative cooling system that utilizes DC technology to reduce energy consumption and increase efficiency in data center operations.
   - Title: DC Air Conditioning Systems for Data Centers
   - Explanation: DC-based cooling systems can offer improved efficiency and cost savings compared to traditional AC-based systems, allowing data centers to operate more sustainably while maintaining performance levels.

4. Building Integrated Renewable Energy Storage (BIRES): A system that integrates renewable energy storage technologies directly into building structures, increasing the efficiency of energy storage and utilization for buildings.
   - Title: Building Integrated Renewable Energy Storage (BIRES)
   - Explanation: BIRES systems can offer improved integration of renewable energy sources and energy storage technologies within building structures, reducing energy consumption and costs while improving overall sustainability.
