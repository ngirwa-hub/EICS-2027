1. Flexible DC Grid: A modular and scalable energy system that allows for easy integration of renewable energy sources, such as solar and wind, into the DC grid in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.
2. Advanced DC Motor Control: An intelligent control system for DC motors used in industrial applications, which optimizes energy consumption, reduces downtime, and enhances efficiency.
3. DC Smart Grids for Data Centers: A decentralized and sustainable power distribution system designed specifically for data centers, reducing energy losses and providing greater flexibility in managing backup power supplies.
4. Integrated DC Cooling System: An innovative cooling solution that utilizes DC technology to improve thermal management in buildings, leading to increased efficiency and reduced energy consumption.
5. DC Microgrids for Urban Areas: A network of DC microgrids connected within a city or region, allowing for the efficient distribution of renewable energy sources and enhancing grid resilience in the event of power outages or disruptions.
