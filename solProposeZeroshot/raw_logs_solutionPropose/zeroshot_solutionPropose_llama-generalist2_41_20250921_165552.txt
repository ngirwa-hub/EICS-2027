1. Solar Power for Ports: Harnessing solar power to reduce dependence on fossil fuels in port operations.
   Brief explanation: Using photovoltaic panels and storage systems to generate clean energy for powering ports, reducing their carbon footprint and operating costs.

2. DC Microgrids for Industry: A system that enables localized energy production, distribution, and consumption within industrial facilities.
   Brief explanation: Implementing DC microgrids in industries can optimize energy usage, improve resilience, and reduce reliance on the main grid.

3. Data Center Cooling using DCs: Employing direct cooling systems to enhance data center efficiency.
   Brief explanation: Using direct current for cooling data centers enables more efficient heat removal, reducing energy consumption and lowering operating costs.

4. Energy Efficient Lighting for Buildings: Implementing LED lighting and DC power supply for greater efficiency.
    Brief explanation: Replacing traditional incandescent bulbs with LED lights that operate on low-voltage direct current can reduce energy consumption, lower maintenance costs, and provide better light quality.

5. DC-based HVAC Systems for Buildings: A system that uses DC power to control heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems.
    Brief explanation: Incorporating direct current in HVAC systems allows for more precise temperature control, increased energy efficiency, and reduced environmental impact.
