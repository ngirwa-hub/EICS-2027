1. Solar Powered Water Pumps: Harnessing solar energy for water pumping in agricultural lands and rural areas.
   - Provides sustainable irrigation without dependency on fossil fuels.

2. Energy-Efficient Refrigeration System: A solution that reduces refrigerant consumption and emissions by using DC technology to control temperature and humidity in cold storage units.
   - Lowers energy consumption, reduces greenhouse gas emissions, and lowers operational costs for businesses.

3. Efficient Lighting Systems: Using DC technology to design LED lighting systems that reduce energy consumption and maintenance costs in various sectors such as data centers, buildings, and ports.
   - Reduces power demand, prolongs bulb life, and lowers operating costs.

4. Direct Current (DC) Bus Bars: A solution for efficient power distribution in industrial settings by using DC bus bars to replace traditional AC systems.
   - Lowers energy losses during power transmission, increases system efficiency, and reduces maintenance requirements.

5. Modular Data Center Architecture: Leveraging DC technology to create flexible and scalable data centers that can adapt to changing demands for storage and processing capabilities.
   - Enables businesses to quickly expand or contract their data center capacities without significant infrastructure modifications.

Note: These are just examples of additional DC solutions relevant to the Shift2DC project's objectives in different sectors. The actual proposals may vary depending on the specific needs and requirements of each sector.
