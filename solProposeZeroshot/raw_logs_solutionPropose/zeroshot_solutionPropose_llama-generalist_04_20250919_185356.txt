1. Solar-Powered Ports: Harness solar power for port operations, such as cargo handling and communication systems. It reduces dependency on non-renewable energy sources while also providing a sustainable solution.

2. DC Grid Integration in Industries: Incorporate DC grids into industrial processes to optimize energy efficiency and reduce transmission losses. This technology enables real-time data monitoring, dynamic load management, and better power quality.

3. DC-Based Cooling Systems for Data Centers: Use direct current cooling systems in data centers to improve efficiency and reduce environmental impact. DC cooling reduces energy consumption and eliminates the need for refrigerants, thus minimizing carbon footprint.

4. Building Management System with DC Power Supply: Implement a building management system that uses DC power supply for efficient control of lighting, air conditioning, and other services. This solution provides energy savings, increased reliability, and reduced maintenance costs.

5. Smart Grid-Compatible DC Solutions: Develop smart grid-compatible DC solutions to integrate with renewable energy sources and support grid stability. It enables real-time monitoring, efficient load management, and reduces transmission losses, leading to a more sustainable energy system.
