1. Solar PV Integration: A system that integrates solar photovoltaic technology directly into DC microgrids, providing clean energy and reducing the need for fossil fuels.
2. Energy Management System (EMS): A software solution that optimizes energy consumption by integrating renewable energy sources with energy storage systems and DC loads in industrial and commercial settings.
3. DC Microgrid Control: An intelligent control system that manages DC microgrids, enabling efficient distribution of power to various loads within buildings or industrial sites.
4. DC-DC Converter Efficiency: A technology that improves the efficiency of DC-DC converters in data centers and other applications, reducing energy losses and increasing overall performance.
5. Hybrid Power System: A system that combines multiple renewable energy sources (such as wind, solar, and hydro) to provide reliable and sustainable power for ports, industry, buildings, and data centers.
