1. Solar Power Integration for Ports: A system that integrates solar panels with port operations to reduce energy costs and carbon footprint.
Explanation: Ports can benefit from harnessing the power of the sun through photovoltaic panels installed on their roofs or adjacent lands, which could generate clean energy while reducing dependence on fossil fuels.

2. Energy-Efficient Lighting for Industry: LED lighting systems that reduce energy consumption and maintenance costs.
Explanation: Industrial settings can benefit from using energy-efficient LED lighting systems, as they consume less energy, last longer, and require less maintenance compared to traditional fluorescent bulbs or high-intensity discharge lamps.

3. DC Air Conditioning for Data Centers: A system that utilizes DC powered air conditioning units in data centers to improve energy efficiency.
Explanation: Data centers can benefit from using DC-powered air conditioning units, as they consume less energy than traditional AC-powered systems and offer better control over temperature regulation.

4. Energy-Efficient Building Design for Buildings: Architectural designs that optimize energy consumption and reduce environmental impact.
Explanation: Building design can play a crucial role in reducing energy consumption and minimizing the environmental footprint of buildings, such as through passive solar design or utilization of natural ventilation techniques.

5. DC Microgrids for Smart Cities: A system that integrates DC microgrids with smart city infrastructure to optimize energy usage and reduce carbon emissions.
Explanation: Smart cities can benefit from integrating DC microgrids into their energy systems, as they allow for greater control over local energy consumption patterns and facilitate the use of renewable energy sources like solar and wind power.
