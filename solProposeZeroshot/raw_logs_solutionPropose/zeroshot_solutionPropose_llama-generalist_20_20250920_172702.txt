3. Data Center Cooling System: Designing efficient cooling systems for data centers using DC technology.
    * Brief Explanation: Developing advanced cooling systems using direct current (DC) technology can improve energy efficiency, reduce cooling costs and minimize environmental impact.

    * Brief Explanation: Deploying DC fast-charging stations can accelerate the adoption of electric vehicles, reduce charging times and increase convenience for drivers.
