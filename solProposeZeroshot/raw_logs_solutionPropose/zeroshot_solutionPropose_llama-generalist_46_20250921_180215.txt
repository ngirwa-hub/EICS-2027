    - This solution involves installing high-power charging stations at ports to support the growing demand for electric vehicles and reduce reliance on fossil fuels.
2. Industry: DC Grid Integration for Smart Manufacturing
    - DC grid integration can enable efficient power distribution in smart manufacturing facilities, reducing energy consumption and costs.
3. Data Centers: Modular DC Infrastructure for Sustainable Computing
    - Modular DC infrastructure can be deployed in data centers to reduce energy consumption and improve efficiency, making it more sustainable to operate large computing facilities.
4. Buildings: DC-Based Lighting System with Occupancy Sensors
    - A DC-based lighting system with occupancy sensors can optimize energy usage in buildings by automatically turning lights on or off based on human presence, improving sustainability and reducing energy waste.
5. Ports: Solar-Powered DC Water Pumps for Irrigation
    - This solution involves installing solar-powered DC water pumps at ports to provide irrigation services for nearby agricultural lands, promoting sustainable farming practices while reducing dependence on non-renewable resources.
