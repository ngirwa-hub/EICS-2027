3. Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure: An efficient DC-charging network for electric vehicles, promoting the use of sustainable transportation and reducing reliance on fossil fuels in industry.
4. Intelligent Power Management System: A system that optimizes energy consumption in buildings by monitoring and controlling lighting, heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems using DC technology.
