1. Solar PV Tracking System: A system that uses sensors and algorithms to track the movement of sunlight across solar panels in real-time, optimizing energy production for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.
2. Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure: DC Fast Charging stations for electric vehicles at port areas, industrial zones, data centers, and residential buildings to support the transition towards sustainable transportation.
3. Microgrid Control System: A centralized control system that manages the interconnection of distributed energy resources (including DC-based systems) and grid-balancing mechanisms in different sectors, ensuring efficient energy management for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.
4. LED Lighting System: An intelligent lighting system that incorporates DC power electronics and sensors to optimize energy consumption based on environmental conditions, improving efficiency and sustainability in buildings and industrial areas.
5. Grid-Connected Battery Storage: A battery storage system that connects directly to the grid via DC technology, enabling flexible energy management and grid services for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.
