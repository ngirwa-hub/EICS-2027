1. Ship-to-Shore Power Supply: A DC power system that feeds electricity directly from a shore-based generator to vessels at docking stations, reducing greenhouse gas emissions and air pollution in ports.
2. Industrial DC Efficiency Improvement: DC-powered motors and drives for industrial equipment, such as conveyor belts, cranes, and sorting machines that can significantly reduce energy consumption and maintenance costs compared to AC solutions.
3. Data Center DC Energy Efficiency: Directly using DC power in data centers instead of AC-DC conversion with lossy transformers, reducing electricity bills, carbon footprint, and operating costs while improving reliability.
4. LED Lighting for Buildings: Replacing traditional incandescent or fluorescent lighting systems with efficient DC LED lamps, which consume less energy, last longer, have better color quality, and require minimal maintenance than AC-based alternatives.
5. Energy Management System (EMS) for DC Applications: An integrated system to monitor, control, and optimize the performance of DC powered devices and infrastructure in buildings, ports, industry, and data centers, improving energy efficiency and operational effectiveness.
