Here are three additional proposed DC solutions for the Shift2DC project:

1. Efficient Lighting System: A DC-based lighting system that utilizes LED bulbs and controls with DC power, providing energy savings and reduced maintenance costs.

2. Grid-Connected Solar PV Inverter: An inverter that directly converts solar photovoltaic (PV) DC output into AC for grid connection, reducing losses during AC conversion and improving system efficiency.

3. Hybrid Power System: A combination of renewable energy sources such as wind turbines, solar panels, and battery storage systems to provide a reliable and sustainable power supply for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.

All solutions are in the required format.
