1. Solar PV on Port Buildings: Installing solar photovoltaic panels on port buildings for renewable energy generation and reducing the dependency on traditional power sources.
Explanation: By installing solar panels on port buildings, energy consumption can be reduced, contributing to sustainability goals while also providing a reliable source of clean energy.

2. Battery Energy Storage System in Industries: Implementing battery storage systems for energy storage and management in industrial processes.
Explanation: This solution allows industries to store excess energy generated by renewable sources like solar or wind power and use it during peak demand hours, reducing the dependency on fossil fuels.

3. DC Powered Data Centers: Implementing direct current systems for data centers to reduce energy consumption and increase efficiency.
Explanation: By using DC power in data centers, power losses can be minimized during conversion from AC to DC power, resulting in reduced energy consumption and lower carbon footprint.

4. DC Lighting System in Buildings: Introducing DC lighting systems that use less energy and have longer lifespans compared to traditional incandescent bulbs.
Explanation: This solution reduces the overall energy consumption of buildings by replacing conventional bulbs with energy-efficient LED lights, leading to a sustainable future.

5. Smart Grid Integration in Ports: Implementing smart grid technology that can monitor and manage energy supply, distribution, and demand efficiently.
Explanation: By integrating smart grid solutions in ports, real-time monitoring of the energy system can be done, enabling better management of resources, reducing waste, and improving efficiency.
