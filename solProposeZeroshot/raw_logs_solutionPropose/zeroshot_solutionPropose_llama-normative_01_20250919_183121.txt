Here are five additional DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings:

1. Smart Grid Interface: A system that enables real-time communication between the DC grid and the AC grid, facilitating efficient energy exchange and reducing losses.
2. DC Microgrids in Ports: Implementing DC microgrids in ports to improve energy efficiency and reliability for port operations.
3. High Power DC Conversion: Developing high-power DC-DC converters that can efficiently convert high voltage direct current to lower voltage levels, ideal for powering electric vehicles in industrial settings.
4. Energy Storage with DC Charging Stations: Installing DC fast charging stations for electric vehicles and integrating them with energy storage solutions to improve renewable energy adoption in industry.
5. DC Powered Lighting Systems: Developing efficient and sustainable DC lighting systems that reduce energy consumption and minimize environmental impact in buildings.
