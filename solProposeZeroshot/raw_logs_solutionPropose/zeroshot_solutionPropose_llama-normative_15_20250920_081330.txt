1. Renewable Energy Microgrids: A system that utilizes microgrids with renewable energy sources (solar and wind) to power ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. This solution integrates DC systems to maximize efficiency and minimize environmental impact.

2. Efficient DC Distribution: A system that optimizes the distribution of electricity in buildings through the use of efficient DC distribution systems. This reduces energy waste and increases sustainability.

3. Smart DC Lighting: A system that utilizes smart lighting technology with DC power supplies to reduce energy consumption and increase efficiency in data centers, ports, industry, and buildings.

4. Energy-Efficient Data Centers: A system that optimizes the design of data centers to maximize energy efficiency and minimize environmental impact using DC technologies. This includes energy-efficient cooling systems, power management, and efficient use of renewable energy sources.

5. Advanced DC Grid Integration: A system that integrates multiple DC grids into a single grid, allowing for efficient sharing and distribution of energy in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. This solution increases flexibility and resilience while minimizing environmental impact.
