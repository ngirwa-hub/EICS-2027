Here are five additional proposed DC solutions that could be important for ports, industry, data centers, and buildings:

1. High-Power Direct Current (HPDC) Transmission: A solution that enables the transmission of high power levels over long distances using DC cables, enabling more efficient and sustainable energy transport to remote locations in these sectors.
2. Eco-Friendly Data Center Cooling System: An innovative cooling system that uses direct current (DC) technology for data centers, providing higher efficiency and reduced environmental impact compared to traditional AC-based cooling systems.
3. Smart Grid Integration: A solution that facilitates the integration of DC microgrids with existing grid infrastructure, allowing for energy management, increased reliability, and better utilization of renewable sources in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.
4. Advanced Battery Management System (BMS): An intelligent DC-based system that optimizes battery performance, monitoring, and control in electric vehicle fleets, energy storage systems, and hybrid power solutions for data centers, buildings, and industrial applications.
5. DC Microgrids for Ports and Industry: A solution that enables the creation of smaller, decentralized microgrids in ports and industry locations using DC technologies, providing increased resilience, flexibility, and lower energy costs compared to centralized grid systems.
