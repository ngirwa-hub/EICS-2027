1. Solar PV Inverter: An inverter that converts DC power generated by solar panels into AC power for use in buildings and data centers.
2. Electric Vehicle Charging Stations: DC fast-charging stations that allow electric vehicles to recharge quickly and efficiently, improving their range and reducing charging times.
3. EV Battery Energy Storage: A system that utilizes battery energy storage to provide backup power during grid outages in data centers and other critical facilities.
4. DC Fault Current Limiters: Devices that limit excessive currents in DC power systems, reducing the risk of equipment damage and ensuring reliable operation.
5. Smart Grids: DC-based smart grids that allow for real-time monitoring and control of energy consumption and distribution in buildings, industry, ports, and data centers.
