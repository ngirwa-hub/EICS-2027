1. Flexible Grid Management: A system that enables dynamic adjustments between AC and DC grid management in response to varying energy demands in different sectors. This enables efficient power distribution to various applications.
2. Microgrid Controller: An intelligent control system that optimizes energy usage within a localized area such as a port, industrial zone or building. It balances demand and supply while ensuring reliable operation of DC systems.
3. DC System Integration for Ships: A solution that converts shipboard DC power to AC for compatibility with onshore grids. This reduces energy losses during conversion, improving efficiency and environmental sustainability.
4. Energy Management System (EMS): An integrated system that monitors, controls, and optimizes energy consumption in buildings, data centers, and other applications. It enables real-time control of DC systems to enhance performance and reduce energy waste.
5. Renewable Energy Integration: A solution that facilitates the integration of renewable energy sources (such as solar or wind) into DC grids for improved efficiency and environmental benefits. It maximizes utilization of clean energy while minimizing grid dependency on non-renewable sources.
