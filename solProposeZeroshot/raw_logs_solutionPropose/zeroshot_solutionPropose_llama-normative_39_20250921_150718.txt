Explanation: Implementing a DC microgrid can enable more efficient energy management in industrial settings, allowing for a higher degree of control over energy usage while reducing reliance on non-renewable energy sources.

5. Smart DC Grid for Data Centers: By implementing direct current grids within data centers, energy consumption and efficiency can be optimized, offering improved environmental performance and lower operational costs.
