1. Efficient Port DC Power Supply: A system that supplies power directly to port equipment using DC technology for efficient and reliable energy supply.

Explanation: In ports, the use of DC power can improve energy efficiency by reducing losses during conversion from AC to DC power. This solution would ensure a consistent power supply to various equipment in the port infrastructure.

2. Industry 4.0 DC Power Supply System: An intelligent power system that integrates DC power supply with Industry 4.0 technology, providing real-time monitoring and control of energy usage.

Explanation: Industry 4.0 technology can enable efficient and optimized use of energy in industry. By combining DC power supply systems with these technologies, it is possible to monitor and control energy usage more effectively, reducing waste and increasing efficiency.

3. Data Center DC Infrastructure: An infrastructure that utilizes DC power for data centers, providing high-efficiency power distribution and improved resiliency.

Explanation: DC power is becoming increasingly popular in data centers due to its efficiency and ability to reduce energy losses during conversion. This solution would provide a more efficient and reliable power supply system for data centers, reducing the need for traditional AC power systems.

4. Energy-Efficient Building Lighting System: A lighting system that utilizes DC technology for energy-efficient and cost-effective illumination in buildings.

Explanation: Traditional AC lighting systems can result in significant energy losses during conversion to DC. By using DC lighting technology, it is possible to reduce these losses and provide more efficient and cost-effective illumination in buildings.

5. Sustainable DC HVAC System: A heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) system that utilizes DC power for energy-efficient and sustainable cooling and heating.

Explanation: Traditional HVAC systems can result in significant energy losses during conversion to DC. By using DC technology for HVAC systems, it is possible to reduce these losses and provide more efficient and sustainable cooling and heating in buildings. This solution would have a positive impact on the environment by reducing energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions.
