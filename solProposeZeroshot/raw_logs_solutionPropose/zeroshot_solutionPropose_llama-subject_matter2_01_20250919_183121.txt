1. Ports: High-Power DC Transmission: A system that enables efficient and high-powered transfer of energy from renewable sources to ports via DC transmission lines.
Explanation: Direct current (DC) transmission can increase efficiency and reduce losses when transmitting electricity over long distances. By utilizing high-voltage DC transmission, the Shift2DC project could help ports efficiently integrate renewable energy sources such as offshore wind and solar power with their existing infrastructure.

2. Industry: DC Grid for Smart Factories: A system that enables energy efficient and low carbon manufacturing processes in smart factories by using a direct current (DC) grid.
Explanation: DC grids can help reduce energy losses, improve power quality, and enable more efficient use of renewable energy sources in industrial settings. By adopting DC grids for smart factories, industries could significantly decrease their carbon footprint while improving productivity.

3. Data Centers: Modular DC Microgrids: A system that enables the deployment of modular microgrids to data centers, enabling them to operate with high levels of energy efficiency and renewable energy integration.
Explanation: Modular DC microgrids can help reduce energy consumption, improve power quality, and integrate more renewable energy sources in data centers. This solution would enable data centers to optimize their energy usage while maintaining high levels of reliability and performance.

4. Buildings: DC-Powered Elevators: A system that replaces traditional AC elevator systems with direct current (DC) powered elevators, reducing energy consumption and emissions in buildings.
Explanation: DC-powered elevators can significantly reduce energy consumption and emissions compared to traditional AC elevators. This solution would not only contribute to the sustainability goals of buildings but also improve their efficiency and overall performance.
