Solution 1: Smart Grid Integration for Ports 
A system that integrates smart grid technology with port infrastructure, enabling efficient management and optimization of energy consumption and supply for various operations.

Solution 2: Hybrid Energy Systems for Industry 
Implementation of hybrid energy systems combining renewable sources (e.g., solar, wind) with energy storage and DC transmission networks to increase efficiency and resilience in industrial applications.

Solution 3: High-Efficiency Data Center Cooling 
Adoption of high-efficiency direct-to-grid air conditioning systems that utilize DC technology for data centers, reducing energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions.

Solution 4: Microgrids for Resilient Building Systems 
Design and implementation of microgrids in buildings to ensure localized power supply, facilitate energy storage, and enable demand response in case of grid failure or fluctuations.

Solution 5: DC Lighting Systems for Efficient Illumination 
Adoption of high-efficiency DC lighting systems that reduce energy consumption and maintenance costs, compared to traditional AC lighting systems. These systems can also enable wireless control and dimming functionality.
