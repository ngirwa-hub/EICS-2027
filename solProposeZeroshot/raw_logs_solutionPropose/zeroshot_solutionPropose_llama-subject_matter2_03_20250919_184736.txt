1. Solar PV Inverter: A system that optimizes the performance and efficiency of solar photovoltaic (PV) inverters for use in renewable energy systems. It ensures maximum power output by adapting to varying environmental conditions and maximizing energy yield.
2. Energy Management System (EMS): An intelligent control system designed to manage, monitor, and optimize the operation of DC power systems across various sectors, such as ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. It enables real-time monitoring, predictive maintenance, and energy efficiency improvements.
3. Hybrid Power System: A combination of different renewable energy sources (e.g., solar PV, wind turbines) that work together to provide reliable power supply to DC loads in various sectors. This solution optimizes the use of multiple energy sources, reduces dependence on fossil fuels and grid electricity, and ensures resilience and reliability.
4. Microgrid Control System: A system that enables the integration of multiple distributed energy resources (DERs) into a localized microgrid network for efficient and coordinated management of power supply and demand in different sectors. This solution optimizes the use of renewable energy sources, reduces transmission losses, and improves grid stability.
5. DC-DC Converter Topology: A system that selects and configures appropriate topologies (e.g., series, parallel) for DC-DC converters to achieve optimal power conversion efficiency, load sharing, and fault tolerance in various applications across sectors. This solution ensures the compatibility of DC systems with different loads and energy storage devices.
