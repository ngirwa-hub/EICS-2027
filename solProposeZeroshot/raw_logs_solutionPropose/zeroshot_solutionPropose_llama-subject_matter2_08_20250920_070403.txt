Brief Explanation: Implementing DC microgrids in industrial settings enables energy-efficient manufacturing by reducing energy losses during power conversion and optimizing load management.

3. Data Centers: DC-based Cooling Systems for Energy Savings
Brief Explanation: Utilizing DC cooling systems in data centers can improve energy efficiency, reduce thermal heat loads, and minimize the use of traditional air conditioning.

4. Buildings: Smart Lighting Control System with DC Power Supply
Brief Explanation: Implementing a smart lighting control system using DC power supply enables precise control of indoor lighting, reducing energy consumption and enhancing user experience.

5. Ports, Industry, Data Centers, Buildings: Flexible AC Transmission Systems (FACTS)
Brief Explanation: Deploying FACTS devices in DC power systems can optimize power flow control, improve grid stability, and reduce energy losses across various sectors.
