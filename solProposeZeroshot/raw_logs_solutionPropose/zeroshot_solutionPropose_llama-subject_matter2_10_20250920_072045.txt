Solution Title: Smart Grid DC Power System
Explanation: This solution addresses the challenges of interoperability between AC and DC grids while improving the efficiency of energy transmission. By using a smart grid approach, DC power systems can seamlessly interface with existing grid infrastructure, allowing for bidirectional power flow and increased efficiency in energy transmission.
