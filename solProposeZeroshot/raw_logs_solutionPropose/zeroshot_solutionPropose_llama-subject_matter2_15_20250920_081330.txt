1. Ports: HVDC Transmission for Efficient Power Transfer: High voltage direct current (HVDC) transmission technology can efficiently transfer power from renewable energy sources located in remote areas to ports and industrial zones with high power demands.

2. Industry: DC Microgrids for Flexible Energy Management: Implementing DC microgrids in industries allows for efficient management of energy consumption and production, enabling flexible integration of renewable energy sources and reducing reliance on the traditional AC grid.

3. Data Centers: DC PV Inverter Technology for High Efficiency: Direct current photovoltaic (DC PV) inverters offer high conversion efficiencies compared to traditional AC PV inverters, resulting in increased energy output and reduced energy losses.

4. Buildings: DC Lighting Systems with IoT Integration: Smart lighting systems integrated with the internet of things (IoT) can optimize energy consumption and provide real-time control, enabling efficient management of lighting systems in buildings.
