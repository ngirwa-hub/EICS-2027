2. Wind-Driven DC Transmission Networks: Establishing wind-driven DC transmission networks for efficient energy transportation between remote renewable energy sources and demand centers, such as data centers and buildings.
3. Efficient DC Air Conditioning Systems: Developing highly efficient DC air conditioning systems for buildings, using advanced compressors and semiconductor-based technologies to reduce energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions.
4. Intelligent DC Building Management Systems: Deploying smart DC building management systems that optimize energy consumption through real-time monitoring, predictive analytics, and automated control strategies for HVAC, lighting, and other building services.
5. Modular DC Power Systems for Industries: Designing modular DC power systems tailored to specific industrial applications, such as food processing, textile manufacturing, and pharmaceutical production, to enhance energy efficiency and reduce waste generation.
