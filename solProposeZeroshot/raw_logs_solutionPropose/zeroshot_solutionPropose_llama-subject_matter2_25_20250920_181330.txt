High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Transmission for Renewable Integration: A technology that enables the long-distance transportation of renewable energy from remote areas to urban centers by converting AC power to DC and transmitting it through high-voltage cables, minimizing energy losses.

2. DC Grid Deployment in Buildings: The use of DC grids within buildings, allowing for efficient distribution of power among electrical devices, reducing energy wastage, and potentially eliminating the need for conventional AC outlets.

3. DC Air-Conditioning Systems: An alternative cooling system that uses direct current to operate air-conditioning units, offering improved energy efficiency and reduced electricity consumption.

4. DC Lighting System: A lighting solution that utilizes direct current for illumination purposes, promising better energy savings and higher efficiency compared to traditional AC systems.

5. DC Fault Current Detection and Protection: An innovative system that monitors and detects fault currents in high-voltage DC transmission lines and provides effective protection mechanisms, ensuring the stability of the DC grid.
