1. Solar PV with Battery Energy Storage: A combination of solar photovoltaic panels and battery energy storage systems for ports to reduce dependence on fossil fuels and provide backup power during grid outages.
Explanation: Harnessing the abundant solar energy at ports can significantly decrease their reliance on non-renewable energy sources while also providing a reliable backup power source. 

2. Microgrids for Industry: A decentralized energy system that connects multiple distributed energy resources such as wind, solar, and fuel cells to provide stable power supply to industrial facilities.
Explanation: Industries require a consistent power supply to maintain operations, and microgrids can provide this by connecting multiple sources of renewable energy. 

3. DC Power Distribution for Data Centers: A system that utilizes direct current power distribution to minimize energy losses during transmission in data centers.
Explanation: Data centers require high-efficiency power systems, and using direct current reduces energy losses during transmission. 

4. DC Lighting Systems for Buildings: An LED lighting system that utilizes direct current for efficient power distribution in buildings.
Explanation: LED lights consume less power than traditional incandescent bulbs, but require a stable direct current supply to operate efficiently. 

5. EV Charging Infrastructure: A network of DC fast chargers for electric vehicles at ports, industrial areas, and commercial buildings.
Explanation: Providing convenient and accessible EV charging infrastructure can encourage the adoption of electric transportation and reduce reliance on fossil fuels.
