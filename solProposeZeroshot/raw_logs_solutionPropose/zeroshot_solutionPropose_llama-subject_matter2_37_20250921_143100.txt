2. Energy Storage Systems for Industry: Utilizing energy storage systems to integrate renewable energy sources such as wind or solar with DC power grids. This enables industries to reduce dependency on conventional grid electricity, improving resilience and reducing costs.

3. High-Efficiency DC Compressors for Data Centers: Advanced direct current compressors that improve the efficiency of data centers by reducing energy consumption and minimizing waste heat. This technology results in lower operating costs and improved sustainability for data centers.

4. LED Lighting Systems for Buildings: Implementing LED lighting systems that operate on DC power, offering significant energy savings compared to traditional incandescent bulbs. These systems can also be easily controlled and integrated with smart building technology.
