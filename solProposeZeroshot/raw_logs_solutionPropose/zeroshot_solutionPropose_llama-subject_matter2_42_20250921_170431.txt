Explanation: DC motors are more efficient than their AC counterparts, but they require specific voltage levels for optimal operation. This solution would optimize the usage of these motors by ensuring the right voltage levels are maintained throughout the industrial process.

Explanation: Traditional AC-based cooling systems consume a significant amount of energy, leading to high operational costs. This solution would use DC power for more efficient and energy-efficient cooling systems in data centers.

Explanation: DC power can be utilized in building management systems, such as lighting and air conditioning control, to improve energy efficiency while reducing the need for regular maintenance.
