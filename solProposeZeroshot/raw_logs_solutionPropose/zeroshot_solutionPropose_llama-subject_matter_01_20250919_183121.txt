1. Solar PV Inverter: A device that converts direct current from solar panels to alternating current for use in buildings and industry.
Explanation: This solution is important for the integration of renewable energy sources into existing DC power systems, allowing for increased efficiency and cost savings.

2. Wind Turbine Generator: A system that generates electricity directly from wind turbines and feeds it into DC grids.
Explanation: This solution is essential for harnessing the renewable energy potential of wind power, which can complement solar PV systems in providing clean energy to buildings and industry.

3. Battery Energy Storage System: A system that stores excess energy from renewable sources such as solar panels and wind turbines, then releases it during periods of low output.
Explanation: This solution is important for managing the variability of renewable energy sources and smoothing out power supply fluctuations in DC grids.

4. DC Lighting System: A lighting system that uses direct current instead of AC, offering improved energy efficiency and reduced maintenance requirements.
Explanation: This solution is important for reducing energy consumption and costs in buildings, which can contribute to a more sustainable and cost-effective operation.

5. DC Motors and Drives: A system that uses direct current to power motors and other mechanical devices, offering increased efficiency and control over power output. 
Explanation: This solution is important for optimizing the use of mechanical systems in buildings and industry, reducing energy consumption and improving operational performance.
