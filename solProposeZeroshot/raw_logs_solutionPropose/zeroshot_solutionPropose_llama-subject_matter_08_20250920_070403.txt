Explanation: Implementing DC microgrids in ports can optimize energy usage and reduce the need for AC-DC conversion losses. This solution improves power management efficiency, reduces operational costs, and ensures reliable power supply in port areas with high demand.

Explanation: Incorporating DC motors and drives in manufacturing plants can enhance energy efficiency, reduce operational costs, and increase productivity by optimizing motor speed control. Explanation: Implementing precision cooling systems using DC components can reduce energy consumption, improve heat dissipation, and minimize the need for AC-DC conversion losses. This solution ensures stable temperature control while reducing power requirements in data centers.
