1. High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Transmission: A technology that allows long-distance transmission of electricity through DC lines with lower losses compared to AC transmission. It can be used in ports to efficiently transport power from renewable energy sources located far away.
2. Microgrid Control System: A control system that optimizes the operation of microgrids by managing energy storage, grid stability, and demand response for efficient energy usage in buildings, data centers, and industry sectors.
3. DC-DC Converter Efficiency Improvement: An innovation that improves the efficiency of DC-DC converters used in various sectors such as ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. It reduces power losses during energy conversion and minimizes energy consumption.
4. Energy Management System (EMS): A software solution that integrates renewable energy systems, energy storage, and DC grids to optimize energy usage and balance demand-supply dynamics in buildings, data centers, industry, and ports.
5. Modular Grid: An approach that enables the integration of multiple DC microgrids, enabling interoperability between different sectors such as ports, industry, data centers, and buildings. It allows for better management of energy resources, demand response, and grid stability.
