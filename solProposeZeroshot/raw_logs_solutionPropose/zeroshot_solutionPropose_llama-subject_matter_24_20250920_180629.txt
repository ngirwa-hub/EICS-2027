4. DC Lighting Systems for Buildings: An LED-based lighting system that operates on low-voltage DC power, offering improved energy efficiency, longer lifespan, and easier maintenance compared to traditional AC lighting systems.
5. Smart Grid Integration for DC Solutions: A comprehensive solution that integrates DC microgrids with smart grid infrastructure, enabling seamless interaction between renewable energy sources and load management systems in buildings and industrial applications.
