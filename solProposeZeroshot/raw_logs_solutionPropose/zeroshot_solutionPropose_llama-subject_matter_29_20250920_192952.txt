1. Solar PV Hybrid Power System: A hybrid system that combines solar photovoltaic (PV) technology with a battery storage and diesel generator set to ensure continuous power supply in ports.
Explanation: This solution addresses the need for reliable power supply in ports where grid connectivity is limited or unreliable. The hybrid system can provide backup power during periods of low solar radiation, thus ensuring uninterrupted operations at port facilities.

2. Efficient Cooling Systems: A DC-based cooling system that reduces energy consumption and maintenance costs in data centers.
Explanation: Data centers typically consume a large amount of energy for cooling purposes. This solution proposes the use of DC-based pumps, fans, and chillers to reduce energy consumption and lower operational costs, while maintaining optimal cooling performance.

3. Energy Efficient Lighting System: An LED lighting system that utilizes DC technology to reduce energy consumption and maintenance costs in buildings.
Explanation: Traditional AC-based lighting systems can consume up to 50% more energy compared to LED lighting systems powered by DC technology. This solution addresses the need for sustainable lighting solutions in buildings, reducing energy bills and promoting eco-friendliness.

4. Efficient Power Conversion: A DC-DC converter that optimizes energy conversion efficiency in industrial processes.
Explanation: Industrial processes often involve multiple stages of power conversion with significant energy losses. This solution proposes the use of efficient DC-DC converters to minimize energy losses and improve overall process efficiency.

5. Smart Grid Integration: A system that enables seamless integration of renewable energy sources with existing grid infrastructure in buildings.
Explanation: Buildings can benefit from renewable energy sources such as solar PV or wind turbines, but require a smart grid integration system to ensure efficient and reliable power supply. This solution addresses the need for an intelligent grid management system that optimizes energy consumption and storage, promoting sustainability in buildings.
