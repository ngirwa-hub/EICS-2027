1. DC Microgrids for Ports: A system that integrates distributed energy resources and loads in port areas into a localized DC grid, optimizing energy efficiency and reducing dependence on centralized power systems.
2. High-Efficiency DC Power Conversion for Industry: Advanced power electronic converters that enable efficient DC power conversion for industrial processes, reducing energy losses and increasing overall system efficiency.
3. DC Energy Storage for Data Centers: Battery storage solutions integrated with DC microgrids in data centers to provide backup power, reduce dependence on traditional AC grids, and improve resiliency against grid disruptions.
4. DC Lighting Systems for Buildings: An LED-based lighting system that utilizes DC technology to reduce energy consumption and maintenance costs, while providing improved illumination quality and flexibility.
5. DC HVAC System for Buildings: A highly efficient heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) system that employs DC technology to optimize energy performance and minimize environmental impact.
