1. Solar-Powered DC Microgrids: A system that integrates solar photovoltaic panels directly into low-voltage DC microgrids for efficient energy conversion and storage in port areas. This solution addresses the need for renewable energy integration in ports.
2. Energy Storage Backup Batteries: A system that uses backup batteries to store excess energy generated by DC solar panels or wind turbines in industrial applications, ensuring reliable power supply during peak demands and outages.
3. Data Center DC Cooling System: A high-efficiency cooling system designed specifically for data centers operating on DC power, reducing energy consumption and minimizing heat dissipation to improve overall efficiency.
4. Building-Scale DC Energy Management Systems: An integrated solution that optimizes energy management in buildings by integrating DC lighting systems, HVAC, and renewable energy sources through a centralized DC control system.
5. Low-Voltage DC Grid Expansion: A scalable solution for expanding low-voltage DC grids in buildings to integrate more devices and appliances with high power density, enabling efficient use of available resources and reducing energy waste.
