Smart Grid Cable Monitoring: Real-time monitoring and diagnostics for DC cables in the port, industrial, data center, and building sectors to ensure safe, reliable, and efficient operation of the DC grid.
DC Microgrid Control System: A system that enables dynamic control and management of microgrids in ports, industries, data centers, and buildings, enhancing energy efficiency, reliability, and resilience.
DC Fault Current Limiter: A device that limits fault currents in DC systems, protecting against overcurrents and minimizing damage or downtime.
DC Fast Charging System: An efficient and rapid charging system for electric vehicles (EVs) in the port, industrial, data center, and building sectors to support growing EV adoption.
DC Energy Management System: A comprehensive energy management system that optimizes energy consumption and efficiency in DC grids across ports, industries, data centers, and buildings.
