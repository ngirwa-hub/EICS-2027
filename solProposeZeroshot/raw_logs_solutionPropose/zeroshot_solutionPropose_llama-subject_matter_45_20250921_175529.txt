1. Solar PV Tracking System: A system that uses advanced sensors and algorithms to automatically adjust the orientation and tilt of solar photovoltaic panels based on weather and seasonal changes, increasing energy yield.

2. Energy Storage Backup Power Supply: A solution that integrates battery storage systems with backup power generators in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings to ensure uninterrupted operations during grid outages or emergencies.

3. DC Microgrids for Islanded Operations: A system that enables port facilities, industrial plants, data centers, and buildings to operate independently of the main power grid in remote locations, reducing dependence on centralized power distribution networks.

4. DC Smart Grid Integration: A solution that integrates DC smart grids with existing AC grids, facilitating the exchange of energy and ancillary services between different regions and sectors, and optimizing the overall energy system.

5. Hybrid Power Systems for Ports: A combination of renewable energy sources (e.g., solar, wind) with energy storage systems and backup power generators to provide reliable and sustainable power supply for port operations.
