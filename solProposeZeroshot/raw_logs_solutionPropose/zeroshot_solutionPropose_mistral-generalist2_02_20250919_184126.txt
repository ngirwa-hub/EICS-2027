Solution 1: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A system that enables ports to operate on direct current grids, improving efficiency and reducing conversion losses during the handling and storage of electrical power from renewable sources like wind and solar.

Solution 2: DC Power Supply Systems for Industrial Applications: Direct current power distribution systems designed for industrial environments, offering higher voltage levels, improved energy efficiency, and reduced electrical losses compared to traditional AC systems.

Solution 3: Modular DC Energy Storage Units for Data Centers: Compact, scalable energy storage solutions that can be easily integrated into data center infrastructure, allowing for efficient power management and enhanced grid stability during peak load periods.

Solution 4: DC Distribution Networks for Buildings: A network of low-voltage direct current (LVDC) wires within buildings to facilitate energy distribution, reducing voltage losses and enabling the integration of multiple renewable energy sources.

Solution 5: Smart DC Load Management Systems: An advanced control system that optimizes power consumption in buildings by dynamically managing and distributing electrical loads among different appliances and devices using direct current.
