Solution 1: DC Grid Tie Inverters for Ports: These devices convert excess DC power from renewable sources into AC power that can be fed into the main grid or used by the port's operations.

Solution 2: Energy Management Systems for Industry: Intelligent systems that optimize the use of DC energy in industrial settings, reducing energy waste and improving overall efficiency.

Solution 3: Power Electronics for Data Centers: Advanced power electronics to manage and distribute DC power more effectively within data centers, reducing energy losses and improving cooling efficiency.

Solution 4: DC Microgrids for Buildings: Self-contained DC power systems that can provide electricity to buildings, reducing reliance on the main grid and improving resilience during power outages.

Solution 5: Building Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV) for All Sectors: Solar panels integrated into building materials, such as roofing or windows, generating DC power directly at the point of use, reducing installation costs and improving aesthetics.
