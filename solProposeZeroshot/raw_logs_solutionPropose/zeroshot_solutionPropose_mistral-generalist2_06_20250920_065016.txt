Solution 1: DC Grid for Ports: A centralized high-voltage DC network that can manage and distribute electricity from various renewable sources (e.g., solar panels, wind turbines) within port facilities, improving efficiency and reducing emissions.

Solution 2: Dynamic Load Management for Industry: An intelligent system that optimizes the distribution of power among different industrial loads in real-time, minimizing energy waste and enhancing overall efficiency.

Solution 3: DC Microgrid for Data Centers: A self-contained grid within a data center that uses direct current, allowing for improved power quality, reduced losses, and increased reliability during power outages or peak loads.

Solution 4: Building Energy Management System (BEMS) with DC Capabilities: An integrated control system designed to manage energy usage in buildings, including lighting, heating, cooling, and appliances, by utilizing direct current technology for better energy efficiency and reduced costs.

Solution 5: Grid-tied Photovoltaic Inverters for Buildings: Advanced inverters that convert solar power generated at the building level into DC before feeding it back into the grid, enabling more efficient and cost-effective distribution of renewable energy.
