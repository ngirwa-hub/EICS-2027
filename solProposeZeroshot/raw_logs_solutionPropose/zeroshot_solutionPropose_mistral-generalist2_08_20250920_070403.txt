1. Solution Title: DC Charging Stations for Ports and Vehicles
   Brief Explanation: Dedicated charging stations that provide direct current power for electric vehicles (EV) in ports, facilitating easy and efficient EV charging during ship loading and unloading operations.

2. Solution Title: High-Voltage DC Power Distribution for Industry
   Brief Explanation: A system that delivers high voltage DC power directly to industrial equipment, reducing energy losses from AC-DC conversion and improving overall efficiency in large-scale manufacturing processes.

3. Solution Title: Modular Energy Storage Systems for Buildings
   Brief Explanation: Scalable, modular battery storage systems integrated with DC power systems in buildings, enabling flexible, on-site energy management and reducing dependence on external power grids.

4. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Data Centers
   Brief Explanation: Self-contained DC power networks within data centers that provide a reliable, efficient source of power while reducing the need for AC conversion equipment and minimizing energy losses.

5. Solution Title: Smart Grid Interface for Building Energy Management
   Brief Explanation: An intelligent interface between building energy management systems (BEMS) and the larger electrical grid, enabling real-time monitoring and optimization of DC power flow in buildings to maximize efficiency and minimize costs.
