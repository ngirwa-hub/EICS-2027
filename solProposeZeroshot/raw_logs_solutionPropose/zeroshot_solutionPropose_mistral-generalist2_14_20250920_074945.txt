Solution 1: Direct Current Grid Connectors for Ports: These are specialized connectors designed to facilitate the seamless integration of DC power sources such as solar and wind energy systems into port infrastructure, improving efficiency and reducing emissions.

Solution 2: DC Energy Management Systems for Buildings: An intelligent system that optimizes the distribution and consumption of DC energy within a building, maximizing energy savings and minimizing waste through smart load management and demand response features.

Solution 3: DC Microgrid Controllers for Industry: A control system designed to manage multiple DC power sources and loads in industrial settings, providing grid stability, resilience, and reducing reliance on traditional power sources.

Solution 4: Smart Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles (EVs) at Data Centers: An integrated charging solution that enables efficient and intelligent charging of EVs within data center facilities, leveraging DC power to reduce conversion losses and support grid stability during peak demand periods.

Solution 5: Power Conditioning and Distribution Systems for Ports: A comprehensive system designed to condition and distribute DC power from various renewable sources (e.g., solar panels, wind turbines) within port infrastructure, ensuring a stable, reliable power supply with minimal losses.
