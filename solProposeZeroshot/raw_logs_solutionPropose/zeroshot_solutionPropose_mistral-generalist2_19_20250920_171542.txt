Solution 2: DC-Based Energy Management System (DEMS) for Buildings: An intelligent system that optimizes energy consumption within buildings by managing electricity loads using direct current, improving efficiency and reducing costs.

Solution 3: Direct Current Transmission Network (DCTN) for Data Centers: A high-capacity, efficient transmission network designed specifically for data centers, which can deliver power directly as DC to improve the performance and reduce energy losses.

Solution 4: DC Microgrid Control System for Ports: A centralized control system that manages the operation of DC microgrids in ports, ensuring stable power supply, optimizing energy flow, and facilitating integration with various renewable energy sources.

Solution 5: Smart Load Management System (SLMS) for Industry: An advanced load management system that uses artificial intelligence to dynamically adjust power consumption across industrial facilities, maximizing efficiency and reducing costs.
