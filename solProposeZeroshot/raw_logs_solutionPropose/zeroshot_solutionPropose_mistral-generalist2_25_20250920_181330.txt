Solution 1: DC Grid Tie Inverter for Ports: A device that converts excess renewable energy generated by port-based systems (e.g., solar panels or wind turbines) from DC to AC, enabling it to be fed back into the power grid.

Solution 2: DC Power Distribution System for Industry: An efficient and reliable system designed for heavy industrial loads, delivering power directly in DC form, reducing energy losses during conversion and improving overall operational efficiency.

Solution 3: Energy Recovery Converters for Data Centers: Devices that capture wasted DC energy during load changes in data centers and convert it into usable power, increasing overall energy efficiency and reducing waste.

Solution 4: Building Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV) for Buildings: A system that integrates solar panels into building materials, such as roofing or facade elements, generating DC electricity on-site and contributing to the building's power needs.

Solution 5: DC Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles in Buildings: An infrastructure designed to charge electric vehicles directly with DC electricity within buildings, reducing charging times and increasing overall efficiency compared to AC systems.
