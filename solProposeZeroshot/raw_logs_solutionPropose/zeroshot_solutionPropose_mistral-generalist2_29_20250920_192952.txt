   Brief Explanation: This solution enables the use of a centralized DC grid in ports, connecting various electrical consumers like cranes, vehicles, and equipment, to improve energy management and reduce losses from converting AC to DC repeatedly.

2. Solution Title: DC Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles (EVs) at Industry Sites: Rapid charging stations that use DC power to charge EVs, improving efficiency and reducing charging times.
   Brief Explanation: This solution provides fast-charging infrastructure for electric vehicles at industrial sites, reducing the need for AC to DC conversion and enhancing productivity by minimizing vehicle downtime during charging.

3. Solution Title: DC Power Distribution in Data Centers: Directly distributing power from the power source to servers using DC power, increasing efficiency and reducing energy losses.
   Brief Explanation: By eliminating AC-DC conversion at each server rack in data centers, this solution improves overall energy efficiency, reduces costs, and enhances reliability.

4. Solution Title: Smart Building Energy Management System (BEMS) for DC: A system that optimizes energy consumption across a building's DC loads, improving efficiency and reducing costs.
   Brief Explanation: This solution allows for the control and management of various DC devices within buildings, ensuring optimal energy usage while minimizing costs and enhancing comfort levels.

5. Solution Title: Grid-Connected DC Microgrids for Buildings: A local power network that operates on DC voltage, providing reliable backup power during grid outages.
   Brief Explanation: By integrating renewable energy sources like solar panels and battery storage systems into grid-connected DC microgrids, buildings can benefit from improved energy security and resilience during power disruptions.
