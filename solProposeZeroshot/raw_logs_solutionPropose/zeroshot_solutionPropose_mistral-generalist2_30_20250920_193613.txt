Solution 1: DC Charging Stations for Ports:
Infrastructure for electric vessels to charge their batteries directly with DC power upon arrival at ports, reducing charging time and improving efficiency.

Solution 2: DC Energy Optimization in Industry:
System for monitoring and controlling energy usage in industrial facilities, optimizing energy consumption by switching between AC and DC loads depending on cost and availability.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers:
A self-contained group of electricity sources, power electronics, and loads that can operate independently from the main grid, improving resilience and reducing energy losses.

Solution 4: DC Energy Distribution in Buildings:
Replacing AC circuits with DC circuits in buildings to reduce energy losses during conversion, improve efficiency, and allow for easier integration of renewable energy sources.

Solution 5: DC Load Management Systems for Buildings:
Intelligent system that manages the distribution of power to different loads within a building based on their energy requirements and availability, improving overall energy efficiency.
