Solution 1: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A system that allows ports to connect directly to a DC grid, reducing energy losses and enabling efficient charging of electric vessels.

Solution 2: DC Power Distribution in Industry: Implementing DC power distribution within industrial facilities for improved efficiency, reduced transformer losses, and easier integration of renewable energy sources.

Solution 3: DC Energy Management Systems for Data Centers: A system to manage and optimize energy consumption in data centers using direct current, improving overall efficiency and reducing costs.

Solution 4: Building-Integrated Photovoltaic (BIPV) Systems: Utilizing solar panels integrated into building structures to generate DC electricity, contributing to the self-sufficiency of buildings and reducing reliance on the grid.

Solution 5: Energy Storage Solutions for Buildings: Integrating battery storage systems within buildings that operate at DC voltage levels, improving energy efficiency and enabling better integration with renewable sources.
