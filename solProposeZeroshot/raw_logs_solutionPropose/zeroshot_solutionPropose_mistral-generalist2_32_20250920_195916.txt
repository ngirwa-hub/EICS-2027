Solution 1: Modular DC Grid for Ports: A flexible and scalable DC grid system designed specifically for ports, allowing efficient management of multiple power sources such as renewable energy systems and electric vehicles.

Solution 2: High-Voltage DC Transmission for Industry: Implementing high-voltage DC transmission lines in industrial areas to reduce energy losses and enable the integration of various renewable energy sources into large-scale industrial operations.

Solution 3: Smart Load Management for Data Centers: A system that optimizes energy consumption within data centers by intelligently managing power loads, reducing energy waste, and ensuring smooth operation during peak demand periods.

Solution 4: DC Microgrid Control System for Buildings: Centralized control of DC microgrids in buildings, enabling efficient distribution of renewable energy sources, demand response management, and resilient backup power systems during outages.

Solution 5: Energy Harvesting DC Systems for Ports: Capturing wasted energy from various port operations, such as vessel movements and cargo handling equipment, to generate clean electricity for use within the port infrastructure.
