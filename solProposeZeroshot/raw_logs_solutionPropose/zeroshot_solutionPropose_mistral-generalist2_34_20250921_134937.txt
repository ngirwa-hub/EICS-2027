Solution 1: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A system that enables the integration of multiple DC sources such as solar panels and electric vehicles in ports, improving overall efficiency and reducing emissions.

Solution 2: Direct Current Arc Fault Protection (DC-AFP) for Industry: An advanced protection mechanism designed to prevent hazardous electrical arcs during DC operations in industrial settings, ensuring safety and minimizing downtime.

Solution 3: Modular DC Power Distribution Units for Data Centers: A flexible and scalable solution that provides efficient power distribution in data centers using DC voltage, reducing energy losses and facilitating the integration of renewables.

Solution 4: Intelligent Energy Management System (IEMS) for Buildings: An intelligent control system that optimizes the use of DC energy sources within buildings by managing load distribution and demand response, improving overall efficiency.

Solution 5: DC Microgrid Islanding Control for Emergency Power Supply: A mechanism enabling DC microgrids to isolate from the main grid during power outages or emergencies while providing essential power to critical loads, ensuring continuity of operations.
