Solution 1: DC Grid Tie Inverter for Ports: A device that converts excess DC power generated by renewable sources (e.g., solar or wind) at ports into AC power for grid connection, improving grid stability and facilitating the integration of more renewable energy.

Solution 2: DC Power Distribution System for Industry: An efficient system for distributing high-voltage DC power within industrial facilities, reducing transmission losses and enabling better control over energy consumption.

Solution 3: Modular Energy Storage Solutions for Data Centers: A series of scalable, modular battery systems that store excess energy generated by on-site renewables in DC form, improving efficiency and reducing costs for data centers.

Solution 4: DC Microgrids for Buildings: Small-scale power distribution networks that operate independently from the main grid using DC power, providing increased energy security, reduced transmission losses, and improved integration of renewable energy sources.

Solution 5: Smart Energy Management System for Buildings: An intelligent system that optimizes energy consumption in buildings by managing DC loads, balancing supply and demand, and incorporating real-time data to ensure efficient use of renewable energy.
