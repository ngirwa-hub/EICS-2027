Solution 1: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A comprehensive system designed to manage and distribute DC power within ports, improving the efficiency and reducing the costs associated with converting AC to DC multiple times.

Solution 2: High-Voltage DC Transmission for Industry: Utilizing high-voltage DC transmission lines to transport electricity from renewable energy sources to industrial facilities, minimizing transmission losses and increasing grid reliability.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: Decentralized DC power systems that generate, distribute, and consume electricity within data centers, reducing energy waste and improving overall efficiency.

Solution 4: Building-Integrated Photovoltaic Systems (BIPV) for Buildings: Combining solar panels with building materials to create energy-generating structures, thereby maximizing renewable energy production in urban areas.

Solution 5: Energy Management Systems for DC Networks in Buildings: Intelligent systems that monitor, control, and optimize the distribution of DC power within buildings, ensuring efficient energy usage and minimizing peak demand.
