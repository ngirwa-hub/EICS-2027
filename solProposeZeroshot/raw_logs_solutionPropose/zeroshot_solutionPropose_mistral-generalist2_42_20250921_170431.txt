Solution Title: High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Transmission: This technology allows for efficient long-distance power transmission in high-voltage DC form, reducing energy losses and improving grid stability. It is particularly beneficial for ports and industry applications due to their distance from renewable energy sources.

2. Solution Title: DC Power Distribution Systems: A system designed specifically for buildings that uses DC power directly from solar panels or other local DC sources. This reduces the need for AC-DC conversion and improves efficiency in building energy systems.

3. Solution Title: DC Microgrid Controllers: Devices that manage the operation of DC microgrids, optimizing load distribution and energy storage usage in data centers and industrial settings.

4. Solution Title: DC Battery Charging Stations: Specialized charging stations for electric vehicles (EVs) that use DC power, offering faster charging times compared to AC chargers. These are ideal for ports and can contribute to reduced emissions.

5. Solution Title: Smart Grid Interface Devices: Intelligent devices that enable seamless integration of various renewable energy sources into the grid, ensuring efficient and stable operation in buildings and industrial applications.
