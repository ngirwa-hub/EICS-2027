1. Solution Title: DC Electric Vehicle Charging Stations for Ports
   Brief Explanation: Installing DC charging stations at ports would enable faster and more efficient charging of electric vehicles (EVs), reducing downtime and emissions from idling vehicles.

2. Solution Title: Energy Storage Systems for Industrial Applications
   Brief Explanation: Integrating energy storage systems in industrial settings can help store excess energy produced during peak times, which can then be used during off-peak hours, improving overall energy efficiency and reducing reliance on the grid.

3. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Data Centers
   Brief Explanation: Implementing DC microgrids in data centers can reduce energy losses associated with AC-DC conversion, improve grid stability, and enhance energy efficiency.

4. Solution Title: Smart Building Energy Management Systems (BEMS)
   Brief Explanation: Implementing smart BEMS in buildings can optimize energy consumption by controlling and monitoring various energy-consuming devices and systems, improving overall energy efficiency.

5. Solution Title: DC Distribution Networks for Buildings
   Brief Explanation: Replacing AC distribution networks with DC networks within buildings can reduce energy losses associated with AC-DC conversion, improve system efficiency, and enable integration of renewable energy sources at the point of consumption.
