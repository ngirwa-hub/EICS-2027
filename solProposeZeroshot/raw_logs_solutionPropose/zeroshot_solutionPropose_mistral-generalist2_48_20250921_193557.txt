Solution 1: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A system that enables ports to connect directly to a central DC grid, reducing energy conversion losses and facilitating the integration of renewable energy sources such as wind and solar power.

Solution 2: DC Power Distribution in Industry: Implementing DC power distribution within industrial facilities can help minimize power loss during transmission, increase operational efficiency, and support the integration of renewable energy sources.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: Developing DC microgrids specifically for data centers would allow for direct connection to renewable energy sources, improving energy efficiency and reducing operational costs.

Solution 4: Building Energy Management System (BEMS): A smart building energy management system that utilizes DC technology can optimize energy usage in buildings by monitoring and controlling various energy-consuming devices and systems, ultimately reducing energy costs and carbon emissions.

Solution 5: DC Charging Stations for Electric Vehicles (EVs): Installing DC charging stations at ports, industries, data centers, and buildings can expedite the charging process of electric vehicles and support the widespread adoption of EVs as a means to reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
