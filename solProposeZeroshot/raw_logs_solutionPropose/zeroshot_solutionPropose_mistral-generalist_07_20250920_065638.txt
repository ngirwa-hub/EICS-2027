Solution 1: DC Port Infrastructure: A system that converts AC power from the grid into DC power for easier and more efficient distribution within port facilities, reducing power loss and improving charging efficiency for electric vehicles (EVs).

Solution 2: DC Grid Tie Inverter for Industry: A device that converts DC power generated by industrial processes or renewable energy sources into AC power for integration with the grid, maximizing the utilization of self-generated electricity.

Solution 3: DC Microgrid Control System for Data Centers: A system that enables data centers to operate as independent DC microgrids, improving efficiency, reducing energy losses, and ensuring a stable power supply during outages or grid disruptions.

Solution 4: DC Building Management System: An integrated control system that manages the distribution and consumption of DC power within buildings, optimizing energy usage, reducing losses, and facilitating the integration of renewable energy sources such as solar panels.

Solution 5: DC Heat Pump for Buildings: A heating and cooling system that uses DC power to transfer heat instead of converting AC power to DC first, increasing efficiency and potentially lowering overall energy consumption in buildings.
