Solution 1: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A system that allows ports to directly connect and distribute renewable energy sources like solar or wind power to their operations using DC infrastructure, reducing losses associated with AC-DC conversion.

Solution 2: Energy Management System for Data Centers: An intelligent, DC-based energy management system that optimizes the distribution and consumption of electricity in data centers, minimizing energy waste and improving overall efficiency.

Solution 3: DC Power Distribution in Industry: A high-voltage DC power distribution solution for industrial applications, offering improved transmission efficiency compared to traditional AC systems, reducing costs and environmental impact.

Solution 4: Building Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV) for Buildings: Solar panels integrated into the building structure that generate electricity using DC power, eliminating the need for separate photovoltaic arrays and improving aesthetic appeal.

Solution 5: DC Microgrids for Energy Autonomy in Ports: A self-contained, localized grid that can operate independently of the main power network, providing energy autonomy to ports using renewable sources and efficient DC technology.
