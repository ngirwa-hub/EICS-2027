Solution 1: Modular DC Power Distribution: A flexible system that allows easy installation and expansion of DC power sources in buildings, providing a scalable solution for varying energy demands.

Solution 2: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A system designed to connect multiple DC sources within a port, enhancing efficiency and reducing power losses during the transfer and distribution of electricity.

Solution 3: DC Energy Management System (DCEMS) for Industry: An intelligent system that optimizes energy usage in industrial facilities by monitoring consumption patterns and adjusting power allocation accordingly.

Solution 4: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: A localized DC power generation, storage, and distribution network that improves efficiency, reliability, and resilience in data centers.

Solution 5: Smart DC Load Management in Buildings: A system that intelligently manages the distribution of electricity to various loads within a building, prioritizing essential functions during peak demand or outages.
