Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: A standalone power network within a data center that can operate independently of the main grid during power outages, ensuring uninterrupted operation and improved energy efficiency.

Solution 4: DC Heating and Cooling Systems for Buildings: A system that uses DC power to directly drive heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) equipment, reducing energy losses associated with AC-DC conversion and improving overall system efficiency.

Solution 5: DC Battery Charging Stations for Electric Vehicles in Buildings: A station that charges electric vehicles using direct current, reducing the need for costly and complex AC-DC converters, making it easier to implement EV charging infrastructure within buildings.
