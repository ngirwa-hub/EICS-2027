Solution 1: DC Port Charging Stations: These are charging stations specifically designed for electric vessels, providing direct current charging to reduce energy conversion losses and speed up the charging process.

Solution 2: DC Load Management Systems (for Industry): A system that efficiently manages and controls the flow of DC power in industrial settings, optimizing energy usage and reducing overall energy costs.

Solution 3: DC UPS for Data Centers: Uninterruptible Power Supply systems utilizing direct current to provide emergency power to data centers during outages or grid failures, ensuring continuous operation.

Solution 4: Smart DC Distribution Systems (for Buildings): A network of intelligent devices that manage the distribution of DC power within buildings, optimizing energy efficiency and reducing energy costs.

Solution 5: DC Microgrids for Off-Grid Buildings: Small-scale grid systems designed to operate independently from the main power grid using direct current, ideal for remote or off-grid locations.
