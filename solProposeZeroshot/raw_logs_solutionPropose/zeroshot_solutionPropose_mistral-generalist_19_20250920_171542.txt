Solution 1: DC Power Distribution System for Ports: A centralized power management system that uses DC voltage for efficient distribution and control of electrical power in ports, reducing energy losses during transmission.

Solution 2: DC Grid-Connected Wind Turbines for Industry: Integrating wind turbines directly with DC grids to provide clean, on-site power for industrial facilities, improving grid stability and reducing dependence on fossil fuels.

Solution 3: DC Energy Management System for Data Centers: An intelligent system that optimizes energy consumption in data centers by managing the flow of DC power, maximizing efficiency and minimizing energy waste.

Solution 4: DC Microgrid Integration for Buildings: A microgrid solution that allows buildings to generate, store, and distribute their own DC power, reducing reliance on the main grid and improving resilience during power outages.

Solution 5: DC-DC Charging Stations for Electric Vehicles (EVs) in Buildings: Fast-charging stations for EVs that utilize DC power to charge vehicles quickly and efficiently, supporting the widespread adoption of electric transportation in buildings.
