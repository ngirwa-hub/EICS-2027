1. Solution Title: Modular DC Energy Storage: Pre-fabricated energy storage units that can be easily integrated into buildings, data centers, and industrial facilities for onsite renewable energy storage and backup power.
   Brief explanation: These modular systems reduce installation time and costs while providing a scalable solution for storing excess renewable energy production.

2. Solution Title: DC Microgrid Control System: An intelligent control system that optimizes the operation of DC microgrids in ports, data centers, and buildings by coordinating multiple DC sources and loads for improved efficiency and reliability.
   Brief explanation: This system can manage renewable energy generation, energy storage, and consumption to maintain a stable grid and minimize power losses.

3. Solution Title: High-Power DC Charging Stations: Fast charging stations for electric vehicles (EVs) using direct current, which charge EV batteries more efficiently than their alternating current counterparts.
   Brief explanation: These stations are essential for ports, where the high volume of vehicle traffic requires rapid charging to minimize downtime and improve efficiency.

4. Solution Title: DC Lighting Systems: Energy-efficient LED lighting systems that operate directly on direct current, reducing energy losses associated with AC conversion.
   Brief explanation: These systems can be implemented in ports, buildings, and data centers for cost savings and improved energy efficiency.

5. Solution Title: DC Distribution Networks: Specialized networks designed to distribute power within a building or industrial facility using direct current, improving energy efficiency and reducing costs associated with AC distribution.
   Brief explanation: These networks can be particularly beneficial in industries where high power consumption is common, such as manufacturing facilities or large data centers.
