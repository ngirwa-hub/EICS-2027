Solution 1: DC Grid-Tied Energy Storage: A battery storage system that can be connected directly to the DC grid, reducing energy loss and improving efficiency for data centers and industrial applications.

Solution 2: Port DC Charging Stations: Fast charging stations designed specifically for electric vehicles (EVs) in ports, using DC power for quicker and more efficient charging compared to AC alternatives.

Solution 3: Building-Integrated Photovoltaic Systems (BIPV): Solar panels integrated into building structures, such as roofing or facades, that directly produce DC power suitable for onsite consumption in buildings.

Solution 4: DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs) for Industry: A system that provides a centralized, efficient, and safe method of distributing DC power to various equipment in industrial settings, reducing energy losses and increasing productivity.

Solution 5: DC Microgrids for Off-Grid Buildings: Self-sufficient electricity systems that use DC microgrids to provide power to off-grid buildings, such as remote homes or isolated communities, improving accessibility and sustainability.
