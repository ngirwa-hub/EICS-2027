Solution 1: DC Grid Tie Inverter for Ports: A device that converts the DC power generated by renewable sources like solar panels or wind turbines at ports into AC power for grid connection, enhancing grid stability and reducing conversion losses.

Solution 2: DC Power Distribution System for Industry: An efficient, low-loss system that delivers DC power directly to industrial equipment from renewable energy sources, improving overall energy efficiency and reducing costs.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: A self-contained group of electricity production, distribution, and consumption resources within a data center, using DC technology to improve reliability, reduce conversion losses, and increase efficiency.

Solution 4: Building Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV) for Buildings: Solar panels integrated into the building structure itself, generating DC power that can be used directly or stored for later use, reducing energy costs and improving sustainability.

Solution 5: DC Battery Energy Storage Systems for Buildings: A system that integrates battery storage directly with renewable energy sources in buildings, storing excess energy produced during off-peak hours for use during peak demand, improving grid stability and efficiency.
