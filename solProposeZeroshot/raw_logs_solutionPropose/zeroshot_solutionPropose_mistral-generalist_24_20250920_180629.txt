Solution 1: "DC Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles at Ports": A system that provides efficient and high-powered charging stations for electric vehicles (EVs) using DC power at ports, reducing charging times and supporting the electrification of transportation in maritime operations.

Solution 2: "Port-Based Energy Storage Systems": Large-scale battery storage solutions integrated within port infrastructure to store excess renewable energy generated by onshore or offshore wind farms, solar panels, or tidal energy systems, providing a stable and reliable power source for port operations.

Solution 3: "Industrial DC Motor Drives": Replacing AC motor drives with DC motor drives in industrial applications, offering higher efficiency, better speed control, and reduced electromagnetic interference, leading to cost savings and improved performance.

Solution 4: "DC Power Distribution in Data Centers": Direct current power distribution within data centers can eliminate the need for AC-DC conversion multiple times, reducing energy losses and improving overall efficiency. This solution includes DC-powered servers, uninterruptible power supplies (UPS), and power distribution units (PDUs).

Solution 5: "Building Integrated Photovoltaic Systems (BIPV)": The integration of solar panels directly into building materials such as roof tiles or glass facades, allowing for the generation of electricity on-site while reducing construction costs and improving aesthetics. This solution can help buildings become energy producers rather than consumers.
