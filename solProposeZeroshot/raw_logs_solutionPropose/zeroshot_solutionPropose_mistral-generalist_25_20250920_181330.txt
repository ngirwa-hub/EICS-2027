1. Solution Title: DC Fuel Cell Systems for Ports
   Brief Explanation: These systems convert hydrogen into electricity using a fuel cell, providing a clean and efficient power source for port operations, reducing emissions and improving air quality.

2. Solution Title: Direct Current Distribution in Industry
   Brief Explanation: Implementing DC distribution networks in industrial facilities can reduce energy losses and improve efficiency compared to AC systems, making operations more cost-effective and sustainable.

3. Solution Title: DC Microgrid for Data Centers
   Brief Explanation: A microgrid that operates solely on DC power within data centers, allowing for improved energy efficiency and reduced conversion losses, which can lead to lower operating costs and higher reliability.

4. Solution Title: Solar Tracking DC Systems for Buildings
   Brief Explanation: Utilizing solar tracking systems in building-integrated photovoltaic (BIPV) systems to maximize solar panel efficiency by optimizing the angle of panels throughout the day, thus increasing energy production and reducing costs.

5. Solution Title: Smart Grid Management for DC Buildings
   Brief Explanation: A system that intelligently manages energy usage and distribution within DC buildings, optimizing power flow to reduce losses, increase efficiency, and ensure reliable operation during peak demand periods.
