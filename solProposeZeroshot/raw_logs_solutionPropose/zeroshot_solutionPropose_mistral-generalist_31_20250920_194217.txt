Solution 1: Direct Current (DC) Grid Integration for Ports: A system that allows ports to connect directly to a DC grid, reducing conversion losses and facilitating the integration of various renewable energy sources.

Solution 2: DC Docking Stations for Electric Vehicles in Industry: A charging solution that uses DC power for faster charging of electric vehicles in industrial settings, reducing downtime and improving efficiency.

Solution 3: DC Power Distribution Systems for Data Centers: An energy-efficient method of distributing power within data centers using DC voltage, which can result in reduced power losses and improved performance.

Solution 4: DC Heating and Cooling Systems for Buildings: A system that utilizes DC power to provide heating and cooling for buildings, offering increased efficiency compared to traditional AC systems.

Solution 5: Smart Energy Management System for DC Grids in Ports: An intelligent system that optimizes the distribution and consumption of electricity within a DC grid at ports, enhancing overall efficiency and reliability.

These solutions aim to address specific needs in each sector by leveraging the benefits of direct current technologies, such as improved efficiency and reduced energy losses. The integration of these solutions can contribute to a more sustainable energy system as promoted by the Shift2DC project.
