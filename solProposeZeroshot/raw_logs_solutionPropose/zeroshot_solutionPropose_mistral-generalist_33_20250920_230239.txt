Solution 1: DC Power Distribution System for Ports: A network of interconnected DC power sources and distribution equipment, designed to efficiently manage electricity for port operations such as cranes, vehicles, and lighting systems.

Solution 2: Direct Current Arc Furnace for Industry: An industrial heating process using DC current instead of AC, offering higher efficiency, better controllability, and reduced energy losses in electric arc furnaces used for steelmaking and other metal processing industries.

Solution 3: DC Microgrid for Data Centers: A localized grid that generates, stores, and distributes power using DC, reducing energy conversion losses and improving overall system efficiency in data centers with high electricity demand.

Solution 4: Building Integrated Photovoltaic (BIPV) System: Combining solar panels with building materials to generate DC power while also providing structural support, insulation, or aesthetic benefits for buildings.

Solution 5: DC Electric Vehicle Charging Stations: Fast-charging stations that deliver electricity directly to electric vehicles using DC current, eliminating the need for AC-DC conversion and reducing charging times.
