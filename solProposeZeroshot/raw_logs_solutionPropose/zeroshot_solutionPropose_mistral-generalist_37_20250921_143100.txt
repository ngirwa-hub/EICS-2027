   Brief Explanation: A modular, flexible grid architecture for industrial applications that allows for seamless integration of various DC sources and loads, improving overall efficiency and reducing downtime.

3. Solution Title: DC Microgrid Energy Sharing in Data Centers
   Brief Explanation: A system that enables data centers to share excess energy with neighboring microgrids, enhancing energy efficiency and grid stability while reducing overall energy costs.

   Brief Explanation: An intelligent building management system that controls lighting, heating, and appliances using direct current, reducing energy losses and improving overall efficiency.

5. Solution Title: DC Grid-Tied Energy Storage for Buildings
   Brief Explanation: A battery storage system that is directly connected to the grid, allowing buildings to store excess renewable energy generated on-site and sell back to the grid during peak demand periods.
