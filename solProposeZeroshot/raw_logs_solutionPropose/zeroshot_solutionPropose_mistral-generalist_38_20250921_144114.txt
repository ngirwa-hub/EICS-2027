Solution 1: Modular DC Power Distribution: A flexible and scalable power distribution system designed for buildings, which can handle varying loads from multiple sources. This system can improve energy efficiency and reduce costs by minimizing energy losses during distribution.

Solution 2: DC Grid-tied Solar Inverters: For ports, solar panels connected to the DC grid instead of AC, reducing conversion losses and improving overall efficiency. This solution can help reduce reliance on fossil fuels for power generation.

Solution 3: DC Microgrid Controller: A centralized system that manages and optimizes the operation of a DC microgrid in an industrial setting, improving stability, reliability, and efficiency.

Solution 4: DC-AC Power Converters (Uninterruptible Power Supply): For data centers, DC-AC power converters can be used with uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) to provide reliable, efficient backup power in case of grid outages.

Solution 5: DC Energy Harvesting Systems: For buildings and ports, energy harvesting systems can capture waste energy from various sources (e.g., wind, vibration, or solar radiation) and convert it into usable DC power, contributing to a more sustainable energy system.
