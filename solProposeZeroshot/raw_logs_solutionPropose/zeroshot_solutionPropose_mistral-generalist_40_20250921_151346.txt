3. Solution Title: DC UPS for Data Centers: An uninterruptible power supply (UPS) that uses DC instead of AC, providing faster, more efficient backup power during outages or emergencies.
   Brief explanation: This system ensures data center continuity while reducing energy losses and improving response times during emergency situations.

4. Solution Title: Smart Building Energy Management System with DC Backbone: A centralized control system that manages a building's energy consumption using a DC backbone, optimizing energy usage and efficiency.
   Brief explanation: This system simplifies the management of energy consumption across a building, improving its overall energy efficiency and sustainability.

5. Solution Title: Modular DC Energy Storage for Buildings: A scalable, modular energy storage solution that uses DC instead of AC, allowing for easy integration with renewable energy sources and efficient energy storage.
