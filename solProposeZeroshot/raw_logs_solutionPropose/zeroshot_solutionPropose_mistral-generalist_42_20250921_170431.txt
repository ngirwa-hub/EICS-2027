2. Solution Title: DC Power Distribution in Industry: A centralized DC power distribution system for industrial facilities, which can optimize the use of electricity, reduce energy losses, and facilitate the integration of renewable energy sources.

3. Solution Title: DC UPS Systems for Data Centers: Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) systems using DC power to provide backup power in case of an outage, improving efficiency and reducing costs associated with AC-DC conversion.

4. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Buildings: A small-scale DC grid within a building that can be used for local power distribution, reducing energy losses and enabling the integration of renewable energy sources such as solar panels or wind turbines.
