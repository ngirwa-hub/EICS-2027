4. Solution Title: Smart Building Energy Management System (DC focus): Integrating a smart building management system that primarily focuses on DC voltage levels within buildings, optimizing energy consumption, and facilitating the use of renewable energy sources.

5. Solution Title: On-site Solar Generation & DC Distribution: Installing solar panels on rooftops or in designated areas for on-site electricity generation and direct current distribution within buildings, reducing reliance on traditional power grids and promoting self-sustainability.
