Solution 1: DC Grid Tie Inverters for Ports: These devices convert excess DC power from renewable sources into AC for feeding into the main grid, facilitating the integration of sustainable energy in port operations.

Solution 2: Modular DC Microgrids for Industry: A modular system that allows industrial facilities to easily scale and manage their DC energy needs, optimizing energy efficiency and reducing reliance on fossil fuels.

Solution 3: DC Energy Management Systems for Data Centers: An intelligent energy management system that monitors and controls the flow of DC power in data centers, improving efficiency, reducing costs, and lowering carbon emissions.

Solution 4: Building Integrated Photovoltaic (BIPV) Systems for Buildings: Combining solar panels with building materials to generate electricity on-site, providing a visually appealing, efficient solution for both residential and commercial buildings.

Solution 5: Smart DC Load Management for Buildings: A system that optimizes the distribution of DC power within buildings, ensuring that energy is used efficiently and effectively while minimizing losses and outages.
