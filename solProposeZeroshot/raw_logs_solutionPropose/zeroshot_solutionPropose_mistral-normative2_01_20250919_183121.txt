Solution 1: DC Grid Tie Inverters for Ports:
These inverters convert DC power from renewable sources like wind or solar into AC power for grid connection, improving port's energy independence and reducing greenhouse gas emissions.

Solution 2: DC Power Distribution Systems for Industry:
This system efficiently distributes DC power within industrial facilities, reducing energy losses and increasing operational efficiency.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers:
DC microgrids provide a more efficient and reliable power supply to data centers, minimizing energy consumption and enhancing their resilience.

Solution 4: Building-Integrated Photovoltaic Systems for Buildings:
These systems integrate solar panels into the building structure, producing DC electricity on site that can be used directly or stored for later use.

Solution 5: Energy Storage Modules for DC-based Homes and Buildings:
Energy storage modules store excess renewable energy generated by building-integrated photovoltaic systems, providing a sustainable and cost-effective energy solution for buildings.
