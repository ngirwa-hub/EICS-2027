Solution 2: DC Renewable Energy Integration Hub: A device that facilitates the connection and management of various renewable energy sources (such as solar or wind) to DC networks within ports, industry, and buildings.

Solution 3: DC Load Management System: An intelligent system that dynamically allocates electricity to various loads in data centers and buildings based on their energy demands, promoting energy efficiency and reducing overall consumption.

Solution 4: DC Fault Detection and Isolation System: A device designed to quickly identify and isolate faults within DC networks in ports, industry, and buildings, improving safety and system reliability.

Solution 5: DC Energy Storage Management System: A control system for battery storage solutions in ports, industry, and buildings that optimizes energy usage, longevity, and efficiency of the stored energy.
