Solution 1: Direct Current Transmission for Ports: Utilizing high voltage DC transmission lines to transfer power efficiently from offshore renewable energy sources to onshore ports, reducing energy loss and promoting the integration of renewables in port operations.

Solution 2: DC Power Distribution for Industrial Buildings: Implementing centralized DC power distribution systems in industrial buildings, allowing for more efficient and flexible power management, reduced energy losses, and enabling easy integration of renewable energy sources.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: Establishing on-site DC microgrids in data centers to support the high power demands of these facilities while minimizing conversion losses and ensuring greater grid stability through local energy management.

Solution 4: Building Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV) for Buildings: Installing photovoltaic panels directly into building materials such as roofs, facades, or windows to generate electricity while reducing the use of traditional building materials and increasing overall energy efficiency.

Solution 5: Energy Storage Systems for Port Operations: Integrating DC-based energy storage systems in port operations to store excess renewable energy generated by onshore or offshore sources, ensuring a steady supply of power during peak demand periods and improving grid resilience.
