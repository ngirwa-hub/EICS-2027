Solution 1: Modular DC Microgrids for Ports: A scalable and adaptable DC power distribution system that can accommodate various port operations and facilitate easy integration of renewable energy sources.

Solution 2: DC High-Voltage Transmission for Industry: Utilizing high-voltage direct current (HVDC) transmission lines to transport electricity over long distances, reducing transmission losses and improving grid reliability in industrial applications.

Solution 3: Energy Efficient DC Lighting for Buildings: Replacing traditional AC lighting with LED lights that operate directly on DC power, increasing energy efficiency and reducing conversion losses within buildings.

Solution 4: Integrated Energy Management Systems (IEMS) for Data Centers: A centralized control system to manage DC distribution within data centers, optimizing energy usage and ensuring efficient operation of the various components in a DC-based infrastructure.

Solution 5: Smart Grid Interface for Building Integration: Enabling seamless integration between buildings and the wider DC power grid, facilitating bidirectional energy flow and optimizing overall grid performance.
