Solution 1: DC Grid Interconnection System for Ports: A system designed for seamless integration of multiple DC sources in port areas, facilitating efficient power exchange and management between ships, onshore facilities, and the grid.

Solution 2: DC Microgrids for Offshore Wind Farms (Industry): Decentralized DC microgrids to optimize energy distribution and reduce losses in offshore wind farms, enhancing their overall efficiency and reliability.

Solution 3: Modular DC Power Distribution for Data Centers: A flexible DC power distribution system that allows for easy scaling and maintenance within data centers, improving energy efficiency and reducing downtime during upgrades.

Solution 4: Building Integrated Photovoltaic (BIPV) Systems with DC Output (Buildings): Combining solar panels directly into building materials to generate DC power, reducing installation costs, and increasing the use of renewable energy in buildings.

Solution 5: Energy Storage Solutions for DC Grids (All sectors): Energy storage systems specifically designed to work with direct current grids, providing improved grid stability, reducing energy loss, and facilitating faster integration of intermittent renewable energy sources.
