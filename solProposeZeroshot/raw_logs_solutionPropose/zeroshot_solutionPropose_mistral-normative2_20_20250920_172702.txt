1. Solution Title: Modular Direct Current Module (MDCM) for Buildings
   Brief Explanation: Pre-fabricated MDCM units can be easily integrated into building structures, providing a flexible and scalable solution for energy distribution within buildings.

2. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Ports
   Brief Explanation: Integrating on-site renewable energy sources and energy storage into a DC microgrid can improve efficiency and reduce emissions at port facilities.

3. Solution Title: Direct Drive Motor Systems for Industry
   Brief Explanation: Using DC motors directly connected to power sources eliminates the need for AC to DC conversion, improving efficiency in industrial applications.

4. Solution Title: DC-DC Optimization Controller for Data Centers
   Brief Explanation: An intelligent controller can optimize the use of multiple DC power sources in data centers, ensuring efficient and stable energy distribution.

5. Solution Title: Smart DC Street Lighting Network for Buildings and Public Spaces
   Brief Explanation: A network of smart, centrally managed LED street lights operated on a DC system can reduce energy losses and improve lighting control.
