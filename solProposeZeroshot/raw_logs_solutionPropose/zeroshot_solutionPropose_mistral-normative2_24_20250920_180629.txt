1. Solution Title: Modular DC Power Distribution System for Buildings
   Brief Explanation: A system that offers flexible and efficient power distribution in buildings, reducing energy losses and facilitating integration of renewable energy sources.

2. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Ports and Industry
   Brief Explanation: Implementing localized DC microgrids at ports and industrial facilities to improve energy efficiency, reduce transmission losses, and enable on-site renewable energy generation.

3. Solution Title: Smart Grid Controllers for Data Centers (DC)
   Brief Explanation: Intelligent control systems designed to optimize the operation of DC grids in data centers, ensuring high reliability, power quality, and energy savings.

4. Solution Title: Integrated DC Bus for Building Energy Storage
   Brief Explanation: A centralized energy storage system that uses a DC bus to distribute stored energy efficiently within buildings, reducing conversion losses and improving grid stability.

5. Solution Title: DC Power Optimization System for Industry
   Brief Explanation: An energy management system that optimizes the use of direct current power in industrial facilities, minimizing energy losses during transmission and distribution.
