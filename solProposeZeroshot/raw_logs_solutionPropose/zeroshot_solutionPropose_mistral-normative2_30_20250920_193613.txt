Solution 1: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A system that enables the direct connection of various port operations (e.g., cranes, electric vehicles, and supply infrastructure) to a centralized DC grid, improving efficiency and reducing conversion losses.

Solution 2: Industrial DC Microgrids: The establishment of self-contained DC networks within industrial facilities to power equipment and reduce energy costs, with the ability for these microgrids to connect to the main power grid during peak demand or outages.

Solution 3: High-Power DC Transmission for Data Centers: Implementing high-voltage direct current (HVDC) transmission systems in data centers, reducing energy losses and allowing for the transfer of large amounts of electricity over long distances more efficiently.

Solution 4: DC Power Distribution in Buildings: Utilizing DC power distribution within buildings to minimize conversion losses from AC to DC and enable the integration of distributed renewable energy sources (e.g., solar panels) into building power systems.

Solution 5: Energy Storage Buffer for Buildings: A system that uses batteries to store excess energy generated by renewables during off-peak hours, releasing it back into the DC distribution system during peak demand, ensuring a stable and reliable energy supply for buildings.
