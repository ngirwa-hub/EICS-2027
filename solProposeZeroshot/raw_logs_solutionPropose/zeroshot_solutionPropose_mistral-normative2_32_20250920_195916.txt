Solution 1: Modular Direct Current Power Distribution Systems for Buildings
This solution involves a flexible and expandable DC power distribution system designed specifically for buildings, which can efficiently manage and distribute power from multiple sources while reducing energy losses associated with AC-DC conversions.

Solution 2: DC Microgrids for Ports
A DC microgrid system that optimizes the use of renewable energy sources such as solar panels and wind turbines for port operations. It aims to reduce reliance on fossil fuels, improve grid stability, and decrease operating costs.

Solution 3: Direct Current Arc Fault Protection for Industry
An arc fault protection system designed to minimize safety hazards and power interruptions in industrial settings by detecting and extinguishing direct current arcs quickly, enhancing worker safety and overall system reliability.

Solution 4: DC Energy Efficient Lighting Solutions for Buildings
Implementing energy-efficient lighting solutions that operate directly on DC power, reducing energy loss due to AC-DC conversions and lowering electricity consumption in buildings.

Solution 5: DC Power Management Systems for Data Centers
A power management system optimized for DC voltages, providing more efficient and reliable power distribution within data centers, thereby reducing cooling costs and energy waste associated with AC-DC conversions.
