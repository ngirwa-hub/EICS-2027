Solution 1: Direct Current Transmission System for Ports: A high voltage direct current (HVDC) transmission system designed specifically for ports to efficiently transfer and distribute power from renewable energy sources, reducing transmission losses and improving grid stability.

Solution 2: DC Power Distribution in Buildings: Implementing a DC power distribution network within buildings, using DC-DC converters at the point of load to convert the primary DC supply into suitable AC or lower voltage DC for appliances and devices, reducing energy losses and promoting the use of renewable energy sources.

Solution 3: Modular DC Power Solutions for Industry: Pre-fabricated, modular DC power solutions that can be easily integrated into existing industrial processes, allowing for scalability and flexibility in adopting renewable energy sources while minimizing conversion losses and downtime during implementation.

Solution 4: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: Developing DC microgrids to supply power directly to data centers, reducing the need for AC-DC conversions, improving energy efficiency, and enhancing grid resiliency in case of main grid outages or peak demands.

Solution 5: Smart Charging Stations for Electric Vehicles (EVs): Implementing intelligent charging stations that can handle both AC and DC charging for electric vehicles within ports, data centers, buildings, and industrial facilities, promoting the widespread adoption of EVs and reducing the overall carbon footprint.
