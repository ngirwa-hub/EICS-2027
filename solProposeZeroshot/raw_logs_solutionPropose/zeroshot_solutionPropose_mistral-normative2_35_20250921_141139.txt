1. Solution Title: DC Grid-Tied Energy Storage System for Ports
   Brief Explanation: A system that stores excess energy from renewable sources during off-peak hours and discharges it during peak demand, improving port operations' sustainability and reducing grid strain.

2. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Industrial Applications
   Brief Explanation: A self-contained network of distributed energy resources supplying power to industrial facilities, reducing reliance on the main grid and enhancing efficiency.

3. Solution Title: DC Power Distribution in Data Centers
   Brief Explanation: Direct current distribution within data centers can eliminate conversion losses, leading to increased energy efficiency and reduced cooling requirements.

4. Solution Title: Building-Integrated Photovoltaics with DC Output for Buildings
   Brief Explanation: Integrating solar panels directly into building structures that generate direct current electricity for immediate use, reducing energy loss during conversion.

5. Solution Title: DC Heating and Cooling Systems for Buildings
   Brief Explanation: Utilizing DC-powered heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems can increase energy efficiency by eliminating the need for AC conversion and reducing heat losses.
