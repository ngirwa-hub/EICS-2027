1. Solution Title: DC Grid for Ports: A high voltage DC network at ports to facilitate efficient charging and distribution of electric ships and vehicles.
   Brief Explanation: Reducing AC-DC conversion losses in port operations, enhancing grid stability, and accommodating large power demands of electric vessels.

2. Solution Title: Energy Storage Integration for Buildings: Direct integration of energy storage systems with building DC loads to eliminate conversion losses and improve resilience.
   Brief Explanation: Improving efficiency in buildings by integrating renewable energy sources directly into the DC power system, minimizing conversion losses.

3. Solution Title: Modular DC Power Architecture for Industry: A modular system designed for easy integration of various DC power loads in industrial applications.
   Brief Explanation: Facilitating the deployment of efficient and flexible DC power systems in industries, improving operational efficiency and reducing costs.

4. Solution Title: Smart Building Energy Management System (DC-SBES): A smart management system for buildings' DC energy systems to optimize consumption, production, and storage.
   Brief Explanation: Enhancing the control of DC energy systems in buildings by monitoring, analyzing, and managing energy consumption, production, and storage more effectively.

5. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: Distributed DC microgrids within data centers to enhance energy efficiency and reduce electrical losses.
   Brief Explanation: Decentralizing power distribution in data centers using DC microgrids, minimizing conversion losses, and improving overall energy efficiency.
