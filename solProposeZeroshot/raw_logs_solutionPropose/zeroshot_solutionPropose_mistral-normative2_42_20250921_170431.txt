1. Solution Title: Modular DC Power Distribution System for Ports
   Brief Explanation: A scalable and flexible system that enables efficient distribution of power from multiple sources (e.g., solar, wind) in port areas, reducing reliance on fossil fuels and improving energy security.

2. Solution Title: DC Microgrid Control and Management System for Industry
   Brief Explanation: An intelligent management system that optimizes the operation of a DC microgrid in industrial settings, ensuring stable power supply, minimizing energy losses, and facilitating the integration of renewable energy sources.

3. Solution Title: Energy Storage Integration for Data Centers (DCSS)
   Brief Explanation: A cost-effective solution that integrates battery storage directly with DC power systems in data centers, improving grid stability, reducing carbon footprint, and lowering operational costs.

4. Solution Title: Smart Building Energy Management System using DC Grids
   Brief Explanation: An advanced system that optimizes energy consumption and demand response in buildings connected to a DC grid, promoting efficient use of renewable energy and enhancing occupant comfort.

5. Solution Title: DC Microgrid Integration with Grid for Buildings (DCMIGB)
   Brief Explanation: A bi-directional interface that enables buildings to seamlessly interact with the AC power grid as well as a DC microgrid, improving grid stability and facilitating renewable energy integration.
