Solution 2: DC Microgrids in Industrial Facilities: Implementing DC microgrids in industrial facilities to provide clean and efficient power supply, reduce energy losses, and improve overall system reliability.

Solution 3: Energy Storage Management Systems for Data Centers: A smart management system that optimizes the use of battery storage within DC-based data centers, reducing energy costs and improving operational efficiency.

Solution 4: DC Building Automation Systems: Integrating building automation systems with DC power supply to reduce energy losses in transmitting data and control signals throughout buildings, enhancing energy savings and system flexibility.

Solution 5: Grid-tied Photovoltaic Inverters for Buildings: Deploying grid-tied photovoltaic (PV) inverters that directly generate DC power in buildings, bypassing AC-DC conversion steps and improving renewable energy integration.
