Solution 1: DC Port Charging Infrastructure: A network of high-power charging stations at ports for electric vessels, reducing charging times and energy losses during conversion from AC to DC.

Solution 2: Industrial Energy Storage (IES): Battery systems integrated with DC power distribution in industries for storing excess energy produced by renewable sources, ensuring continuous power supply and grid stability.

Solution 3: Data Center Power Distribution Units (PDUs): Advanced PDUs that utilize DC voltage for powering server racks within data centers, improving efficiency and reducing heat generated during AC-DC conversion.

Solution 4: Building Integrated Photovoltaic (BIPV) Systems: Combining solar panels with building materials to generate electricity directly as DC power in buildings, eliminating the need for separate solar farms and reducing energy losses during AC conversion.

Solution 5: Smart Grid Interface Device (SGID): An intelligent device that converts excess renewable energy generated by buildings into DC power and feeds it back into the grid or stores it locally for later use, optimizing self-consumption and minimizing grid congestion.
