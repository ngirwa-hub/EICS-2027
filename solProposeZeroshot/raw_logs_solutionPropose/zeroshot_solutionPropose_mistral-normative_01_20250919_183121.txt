Solution 1: Direct Current Grid Integration: A system that enables the integration of DC-powered equipment into existing AC grid systems at ports, industry sites, data centers, and buildings, facilitating a seamless transition towards DC energy distribution.

Solution 2: DC Microgrid Control System: An advanced control system for DC microgrids within port facilities, industrial parks, data centers, and buildings, optimizing energy consumption and ensuring efficient management of local renewable energy resources.

Solution 3: DC Distribution Transformer (DCT): A transformer designed specifically for direct current distribution, reducing losses in the grid and facilitating higher voltage levels for ports, industries, data centers, and buildings using DC power.

Solution 4: On-Site Renewable Energy DC Inverter: A device that converts renewable energy (e.g., solar or wind) into DC power on-site for use in ports, industries, data centers, and buildings, minimizing conversion losses and increasing system efficiency.

Solution 5: DC Load Management System: An intelligent system that optimizes the distribution of DC power to various loads within port facilities, industrial sites, data centers, and buildings, ensuring efficient energy utilization and reducing peak demand.
