Solution 1: Direct Current Distribution System for Ports: A decentralized and efficient distribution system that uses DC power from multiple sources like solar panels or wind turbines to power port operations, reducing energy losses and increasing sustainability.

Solution 2: Energy Storage Solutions for Data Centers (DC-based UPS): Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) systems in data centers that use direct current storage instead of traditional lead-acid batteries, improving efficiency and reducing maintenance costs.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Industrial Applications: Small-scale, locally controlled power grids that use DC electricity generated by renewable energy sources to provide a reliable and sustainable energy supply for industrial processes.

Solution 4: Intelligent Load Management for Buildings (DC Load Controllers): Smart devices that manage the distribution of DC power in buildings, optimizing energy usage and reducing energy losses while maintaining a comfortable environment for occupants.

Solution 5: Modular DC Power Architecture for Industry: A flexible and scalable power architecture that allows for easy integration of renewable energy sources into industrial processes, promoting sustainability and adaptability to changing energy demands.
