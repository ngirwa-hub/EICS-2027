Solution 1: DC Microgrid Controller: A device that manages and optimizes the operation of a DC microgrid in buildings, ports, and data centers, ensuring efficient energy distribution and load balancing.

Solution 2: DC Grid Tie Inverter for Industry: An inverter designed to convert excess industrial DC power into AC grid power, allowing for easier integration with existing power grids and facilitating the sale of surplus renewable energy back to the grid.

Solution 3: Portside DC Charging Stations: A network of charging stations at ports that offer efficient direct current charging solutions for electric vehicles (EVs), reducing charging times and improving overall port operations.

Solution 4: Modular DC Energy Storage System for Data Centers: A scalable, high-density energy storage system using batteries or other storage technologies specifically designed to store excess energy from data center DC systems for later use.

Solution 5: DC-AC Power Conversion for Building Applications: A power conversion solution that allows for efficient conversion of DC power generated by on-site renewable sources (such as solar panels) into AC power suitable for common building loads, reducing losses and improving grid stability in buildings.
