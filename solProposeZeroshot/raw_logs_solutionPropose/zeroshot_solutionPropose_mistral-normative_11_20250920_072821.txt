Solution 1: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A system that allows ports to connect directly to a DC grid, reducing losses from AC-DC conversion and enabling efficient handling of high power loads from ships and port equipment.

Solution 2: Modular DC Power Distribution for Industry: A flexible, scalable solution for DC power distribution in industrial settings, improving energy efficiency and reducing downtime due to power outages or maintenance.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: Implementing DC microgrids within data centers can reduce energy losses associated with AC-DC conversion and provide improved power quality and reliability.

Solution 4: Building Energy Management System (BEMS) with DC Support: An advanced building management system that supports DC energy flows, enabling optimal energy usage in buildings and supporting the integration of renewable energy sources.

Solution 5: Residential DC Distribution: A residential distribution network designed to handle DC power directly from renewable energy sources, reducing energy losses and increasing the adoption of renewable technologies at homes.
