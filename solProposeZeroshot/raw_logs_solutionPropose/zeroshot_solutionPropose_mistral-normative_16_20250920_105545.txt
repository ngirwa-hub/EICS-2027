Solution 1: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A comprehensive system that facilitates direct connection of multiple DC power sources in ports, allowing for efficient energy exchange and distribution among various port operations.

Solution 2: High Voltage DC Transmission for Industry: Utilizing high voltage DC transmission lines to transport electricity over long distances from remote renewable energy sources to industrial facilities, reducing losses and increasing efficiency.

Solution 3: Building Energy Management System (BEMS) for Data Centers: An intelligent system that optimizes energy consumption in data centers by managing DC power distribution, load balancing, and monitoring for improved efficiency and reduced costs.

Solution 4: Photovoltaic-Integrated Façade Panels for Buildings: Energy-generating solar panels integrated into building facades to produce DC electricity on-site, reducing energy consumption and carbon emissions.

Solution 5: DC Microgrids for Remote Building Applications: Off-grid DC microgrids designed for remote buildings, providing a reliable, sustainable, and cost-effective power solution in areas not connected to the main grid.
