Solution 1: High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Grid Interconnection: Enabling the connection of multiple distributed energy sources in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings to a centralized HVDC grid for efficient transmission and distribution.

Solution 2: DC Microgrid Controller: Intelligent management system for DC microgrids within buildings, data centers, and industrial facilities, ensuring optimal operation and seamless integration with AC grids when necessary.

Solution 3: Energy-Efficient DC-DC Converters (for ports): Reducing energy losses in converting various DC sources to a common voltage level for use in port equipment and electrical vehicles (EV) charging infrastructure.

Solution 4: Power Electronics Heat Management Systems (for data centers): Enhancing the thermal management of power electronics used in DC technologies within data centers, ensuring high efficiency and reliability.

Solution 5: DC Building Automation System: Integrating DC-powered sensors, actuators, and controls for energy-efficient building automation, reducing costs associated with AC-DC conversion and improving overall system performance.
