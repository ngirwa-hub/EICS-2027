Solution 1: DC Grid Tie Inverters for Ports: These devices convert excess DC power generated by renewable sources at ports into AC power that can be fed back into the grid, enhancing its resilience and sustainability.

Solution 2: DC Distribution Network for Industry: A network that delivers DC power directly to various industrial loads within a facility, reducing energy losses and improving operational efficiency.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: Self-contained electrical grids that can operate independently from the main grid and provide uninterrupted power supply using renewable sources, ensuring data center continuity during grid outages.

Solution 4: DC Energy Efficiency Lighting Systems for Buildings: Energy-efficient LED lighting systems designed to operate directly on DC power, reducing conversion losses and energy consumption in buildings.

Solution 5: Bidirectional DC Charging Stations for Electric Vehicles (EVs) in Buildings: Allows charging and discharging of EV batteries within buildings using DC power, providing a more efficient and flexible energy storage solution for both vehicles and buildings.
