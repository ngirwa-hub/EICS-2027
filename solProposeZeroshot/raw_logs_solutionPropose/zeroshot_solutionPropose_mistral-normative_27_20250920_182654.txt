Solution 1: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A system that allows for the direct connection of multiple DC sources in ports, improving efficiency and reducing conversion losses during energy exchange between ships and shore-side infrastructure.

Solution 2: DC Power Distribution for Industry: A modular, high-voltage DC power distribution system designed for industrial applications, reducing energy losses and enabling more efficient operation of equipment.

Solution 3: DC Microgrid Control for Data Centers: An intelligent control system that optimizes the performance of DC microgrids in data centers, ensuring grid stability, minimizing energy waste, and facilitating the integration of renewable energy sources.

Solution 4: Building Energy Management System (BEMS) with DC Capabilities: A BEMS designed to manage and monitor building energy consumption using DC power, improving efficiency, reducing losses, and facilitating the integration of renewable energy sources within buildings.

Solution 5: DC Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles (EVs): A network of high-power DC charging stations that can quickly charge EV batteries, reducing charging times and supporting the widespread adoption of electric vehicles in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.
