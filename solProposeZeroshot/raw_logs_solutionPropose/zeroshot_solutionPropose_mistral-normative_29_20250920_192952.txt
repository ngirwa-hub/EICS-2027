Solution 1: DC Distribution Networks for Ports: Implementing dedicated DC distribution networks in ports can improve efficiency and reduce energy losses during the transfer of electricity from onshore power grids to electric vessels. This would require specialized infrastructure and adapters compatible with both AC and DC connections.

Solution 2: Energy-Efficient DC Lighting for Buildings: Replacing traditional AC lighting systems in buildings with energy-efficient DC lighting solutions can result in reduced energy consumption and lower maintenance costs due to the compatibility of LED lights with DC power supply.

Solution 3: Modular DC Power Systems for Data Centers: Deploying modular DC power systems in data centers allows for easy scaling, improved efficiency, and increased reliability by eliminating the need for AC-DC conversion at each server rack. This could be achieved through prefabricated, containerized power units.

Solution 4: DC Microgrids for Industrial Applications: Establishing DC microgrids within industrial facilities can provide localized power distribution and reduce energy losses associated with long transmission lines, while also integrating renewable energy sources more efficiently.

Solution 5: DC Transmission Lines for Industry: High-voltage direct current (HVDC) transmission lines can be used to transport electricity over long distances with minimal energy loss and reduced impact on existing power grids. This could be particularly beneficial for industrial sites that require large amounts of electricity or are located far from renewable energy sources.
