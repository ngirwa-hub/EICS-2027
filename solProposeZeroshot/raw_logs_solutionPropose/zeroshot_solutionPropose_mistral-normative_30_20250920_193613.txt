1. Solution Title: DC Grid Interconnection for Ports:
   Brief Explanation: Enables seamless integration of various DC sources at ports, allowing for efficient energy management and distribution among multiple vessels and facilities.

2. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Energy Independence in Industry:
   Brief Explanation: Provides localized, on-site power generation and consumption for industrial operations, reducing reliance on the main grid and improving sustainability.

3. Solution Title: DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs) for Data Centers:
   Brief Explanation: Simplifies power distribution within data centers by using direct current, leading to reduced energy losses and improved efficiency.

4. Solution Title: DC Building Energy Management Systems (BEMS):
   Brief Explanation: Optimizes energy usage in buildings through centralized control of lighting, heating, ventilation, and other electrical systems, utilizing direct current for increased efficiency.

5. Solution Title: On-site Solar Inverters for DC Buildings:
   Brief Explanation: Converts solar power directly to DC, eliminating the need for AC-DC conversion in buildings and improving overall energy efficiency.
