Solution 1: DC Distribution Grid: A high voltage DC (HVDC) grid for ports and industry that reduces transmission losses and increases the efficiency of power distribution.

Solution 2: Modular DC Power Supply: Pre-fabricated, plug-and-play modules for easy integration in data centers and buildings, providing a scalable and flexible power solution.

Solution 3: Energy Harvesting DC System: A system that captures wasted energy from various sources (e.g., wind, solar, vibrations) within ports, buildings, or industries, converting it into usable DC power.

Solution 4: DC Microgrid Controllers: Intelligent control systems for buildings and data centers that optimize energy distribution and management across multiple DC microgrids.

Solution 5: DC Electric Vehicle Charging Stations: Fast-charging stations for electric vehicles in ports, reducing the need for time-consuming AC charging and improving efficiency.
