Solution 2: Modular DC Power Distribution Units for Industry: Pre-fabricated, modular units providing DC power distribution within industrial facilities, simplifying installation and maintenance processes.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: Decentralized energy systems supplying DC power to data centers, reducing energy losses associated with AC-DC conversion and improving overall efficiency.

Solution 4: DC Energy Recovery Systems for Buildings: Systems designed to capture waste energy from DC appliances and utilize it for other purposes within the building, promoting energy conservation.

Solution 5: Smart Load Management System for Building Energy Storage: A system that intelligently manages energy consumption in buildings based on storage capacity, reducing peak demand and optimizing power usage.
