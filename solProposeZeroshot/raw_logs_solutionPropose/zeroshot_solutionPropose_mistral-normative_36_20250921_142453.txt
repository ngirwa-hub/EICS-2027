1. Solution Title: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A system that enables ports to operate on a unified DC grid, reducing conversion losses and improving the efficiency of electrical equipment.

Brief Explanation: By integrating ports into a DC grid, the energy consumption can be optimized, leading to reduced costs and environmental impacts.

2. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Industry: On-site DC generation and distribution systems that improve energy independence and reduce grid reliance in industrial facilities.

Brief Explanation: DC microgrids can provide a more efficient power supply for industries, reducing transmission losses and enhancing operational resilience.

3. Solution Title: Energy Storage for Data Centers (DCES): A large-scale energy storage solution tailored to data centers, ensuring steady power supply and minimizing the carbon footprint.

Brief Explanation: DCES can store excess energy during off-peak hours for use during peak demand, resulting in improved energy efficiency and reduced costs.

4. Solution Title: DC Lighting for Buildings: Energy-efficient LED lighting operated directly from a DC power source, reducing conversion losses and lowering energy bills.

Brief Explanation: By adopting DC lighting, buildings can significantly decrease their electricity consumption and contribute to overall energy savings.

5. Solution Title: Smart Building Controls (SBC): A building automation system that utilizes DC power for enhanced control, efficiency, and compatibility with renewable energy sources.

Brief Explanation: SBC can optimize the energy usage in buildings by coordinating lighting, HVAC, and other systems on a unified DC platform, ultimately reducing energy costs and environmental impact.
