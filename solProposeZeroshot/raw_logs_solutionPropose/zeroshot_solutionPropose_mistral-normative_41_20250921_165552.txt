Solution 1: Direct Current Distribution Networks for Ports:
A system that enables efficient and safe distribution of DC power in port terminals, reducing energy losses and promoting the integration of electric vehicles and renewable energy sources.

Solution 2: High-Power DC Transmission Systems for Industry:
High-voltage DC transmission lines designed to transport electricity from remote renewable energy sources to industrial facilities, minimizing transmission losses and enhancing grid stability.

Solution 3: On-Site Solar Energy Conversion for Data Centers:
A system that converts solar power generated on-site into DC electricity for direct use in data centers, reducing dependence on the main grid and improving energy efficiency.

Solution 4: Building Integrated Photovoltaic Systems (BIPV) for Buildings:
Photovoltaic modules integrated within building materials such as roofs, walls, or windows, generating DC power that can be directly utilized in the building or fed back into the grid.

Solution 5: DC Microgrids for Energy Independence in Buildings:
Small-scale decentralized energy systems that generate, store, and distribute DC electricity within buildings, ensuring energy independence during power outages and reducing reliance on the main grid.
