Solution 1: High Voltage DC Transmission System for Ports
- A modern high voltage direct current transmission system designed for efficient power transfer and distribution in port areas, reducing energy loss and enabling large-scale renewable integration.

Solution 2: Smart Grid Management for Industry
- An intelligent grid management system that optimizes the use of DC energy in industrial applications, ensuring efficiency, reliability, and minimizing environmental impact.

Solution 3: Onsite Solar Power Generation and DC Microgrids for Buildings
- A combination of onsite solar power generation and DC microgrids that allows buildings to generate, store, and utilize their own clean energy, reducing dependence on the main grid and promoting energy independence.

Solution 4: Energy Storage Solutions for Data Centers (Battery-Based V2G)
- Two-way energy storage systems in data centers that allow for efficient utilization of excess power generation during peak hours and provide vehicle-to-grid capabilities, promoting renewable integration and grid stability.

Solution 5: DC Distribution Network for Building Renovations
- A retrofittable DC distribution network designed for building renovation projects, providing a cost-effective, efficient, and sustainable solution for integrating renewable energy sources into existing structures.
