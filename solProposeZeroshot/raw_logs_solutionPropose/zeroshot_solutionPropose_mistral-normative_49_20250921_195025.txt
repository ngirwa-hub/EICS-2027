1. Solution Title: Direct Current Distribution for Ports: Efficient Shore-side Power Supply
   Brief Explanation: Implementing DC grid infrastructure at ports allows for efficient and lossless power supply from shore to vessels during berthing, reducing emissions and improving energy efficiency.

2. Solution Title: Energy Storage Systems for Data Centers (DCSS)
   Brief Explanation: Integrating battery energy storage directly into the DC system of data centers can provide quick response backup power and optimize overall system efficiency.

3. Solution Title: On-site Solar Generation and DC Microgrids in Buildings
   Brief Explanation: Installing photovoltaic panels on building rooftops to generate DC power, which is then distributed within the building or fed into a local DC grid, reducing energy costs and carbon footprint.

4. Solution Title: DC-DC Optimization Controllers for Industry
   Brief Explanation: Utilizing advanced control systems to manage multiple DC loads and sources in industrial settings, improving power quality, efficiency, and reducing the need for AC conversion.

5. Solution Title: DC-DC Charging Stations for Electric Vehicles (EVs) in Ports and Buildings
   Brief Explanation: Installing DC fast-charging stations at ports and buildings for electric vehicles can reduce charging times, improve overall efficiency of the EV infrastructure, and support the integration of renewable energy sources.
