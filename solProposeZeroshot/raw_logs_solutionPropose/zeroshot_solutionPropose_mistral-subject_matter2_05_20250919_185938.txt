Solution 1: Direct Current Grid Integration for Ports: A system that enables ports to operate on a DC grid, reducing conversion losses and facilitating the integration of electric vessels and onshore renewable energy sources.

Solution 2: High-Voltage DC Transmission for Industry: Implementing high-voltage DC power transmission lines to transport electricity from renewable energy sources to industrial facilities, improving efficiency and reducing transmission losses.

Solution 3: DC Energy Management System for Data Centers: An intelligent management system that optimizes the use of direct current power within data centers, reducing energy consumption and improving overall efficiency.

Solution 4: DC Microgrid for Buildings: A distributed generation and storage system that allows buildings to operate on a local DC microgrid, improving grid stability, resiliency, and reducing peak demand.

Solution 5: DC Distribution Network for Industrial Facilities: Replacing traditional AC distribution networks with DC distribution networks within industrial facilities, reducing energy losses and enabling easier integration of renewable energy sources.
