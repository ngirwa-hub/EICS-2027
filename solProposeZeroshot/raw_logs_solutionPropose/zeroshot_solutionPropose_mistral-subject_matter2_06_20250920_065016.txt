Solution 1: DC Grid Integration for Ports: Implementing a high-voltage DC grid in ports can facilitate efficient energy distribution from shore-side power supply systems and renewable sources like wind farms or solar arrays, reducing conversion losses and increasing overall efficiency.

Solution 2: DC Microgrids for Industry: Utilizing on-site DC microgrids within industrial facilities allows for the direct connection of various distributed energy resources (DERs), improving grid stability and resilience while minimizing energy transmission losses.

Solution 3: DC Power Distribution in Data Centers: Employing DC power distribution systems in data centers can reduce conversion losses, improve energy efficiency, and enable the integration of renewable energy sources directly into the data center's power supply.

Solution 4: DC Home Energy Management Systems (HEMS) for Buildings: Implementing smart home energy management systems that operate on DC voltage can optimize energy consumption within buildings by efficiently managing distributed renewable energy sources and reducing conversion losses.

Solution 5: DC Bus Bar Systems in Buildings: High-voltage DC bus bar systems can provide a cost-effective solution for power distribution in large buildings, enabling the integration of multiple DERs and improving overall system efficiency while minimizing energy transmission losses.
