Solution 1: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A system that allows ports to operate on a direct current grid, reducing energy losses and enabling efficient handling of large amounts of power from renewable sources such as offshore wind farms.

Solution 2: DC Power Distribution in Industry: A high-voltage DC power distribution system designed for industrial applications, offering increased efficiency, lower transmission losses, and faster response times compared to traditional AC systems.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: A scalable microgrid solution that operates on direct current, allowing data centers to benefit from the higher efficiency and reduced energy costs associated with DC power distribution.

Solution 4: On-Site Solar DC Generation for Buildings: A rooftop solar system designed to generate direct current electricity directly, eliminating the need for AC-DC conversion and reducing energy losses in buildings.

Solution 5: DC Electric Vehicle Charging Stations: A charging infrastructure that utilizes direct current for faster and more efficient charging of electric vehicles, minimizing energy losses during the charging process.
