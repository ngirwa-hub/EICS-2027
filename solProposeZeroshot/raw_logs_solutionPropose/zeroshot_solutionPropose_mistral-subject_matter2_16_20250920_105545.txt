Solution 2: Modular DC Battery Storage for Industry: Pre-engineered, scalable battery storage systems designed to provide DC energy storage solutions for industrial applications, improving power quality and reducing energy costs.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: Implementing localized DC microgrids within data centers to reduce AC-DC conversion losses, increase power reliability, and facilitate the integration of renewable energy sources for improved energy efficiency.

Solution 4: DC Load Management for Buildings: A system that enables efficient management of DC loads in buildings, reducing energy losses associated with converting DC power from various devices (such as solar panels and electric vehicles) to AC power for distribution.

Solution 5: Smart DC Distribution Boards for Buildings: Intelligent distribution boards designed to manage and monitor DC circuits within buildings, improving safety, efficiency, and flexibility in the use of DC power systems.
