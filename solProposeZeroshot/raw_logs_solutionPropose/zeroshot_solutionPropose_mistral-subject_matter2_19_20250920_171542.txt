Solution 1: High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Transmission for Offshore Wind Farms:
A system that transmits electricity generated from offshore wind farms using HVDC technology, reducing transmission losses and enabling efficient power transfer over long distances to the grid.

Solution 2: DC Microgrids for Ports:
Integration of distributed energy resources into a localized DC power network within ports, providing reliable and efficient power supply to various port facilities while minimizing electromagnetic interference with navigation systems.

Solution 3: DC Distribution System for Industrial Facilities:
A high-efficiency distribution system that uses DC voltage for power transmission within industrial facilities, reducing energy losses and improving operational safety due to the elimination of AC-DC conversion steps.

Solution 4: DC Energy Storage for Data Centers:
Implementing on-site energy storage using DC batteries in data centers to store excess energy during low demand periods and provide backup power during peak demand or outages, while reducing conversion losses.

Solution 5: Smart Building Energy Management System (BEMS):
A control system that optimizes the distribution and consumption of electricity within buildings using DC voltage, providing real-time monitoring, load balancing, and energy management capabilities to improve efficiency and reduce costs.
