Solution 1: Modular DC Grid Architecture for Buildings: A scalable and flexible DC grid design that allows for easy integration of various renewable energy sources and loads within buildings. This can improve efficiency and reduce installation costs.

Solution 2: Portside High Voltage DC Shore Connection (HVDC): A system designed to connect offshore wind farms or other renewable energy sources directly to the port's high voltage DC grid, reducing power losses during transmission.

Solution 3: DC Microgrid Control and Optimization System for Industry: An intelligent system that controls and optimizes the operation of DC microgrids within industrial facilities, ensuring efficient power distribution and minimizing downtime.

Solution 4: Smart DC Distribution in Data Centers: A system that uses advanced control technologies to manage the distribution of DC power within data centers, improving energy efficiency and reducing cooling requirements.

Solution 5: DC Battery Swap Stations for Electric Vehicles (EVs) in Ports: A fast-charging solution for electric vehicles at ports, using swappable DC batteries to significantly reduce charging times and increase vehicle uptime.
