Solution 1: DC Grid Tie Inverter for Ports: A device that converts excess DC power from renewable sources (e.g., solar or wind) into AC power, allowing for seamless integration with existing port electrical infrastructure and grid connection.

Solution 2: DC Microgrid Controller for Industry: An advanced control system that manages the distribution and consumption of DC power in industrial settings, optimizing energy efficiency and ensuring reliable operation during peak loads and outages.

Solution 3: DC Battery Buffering for Data Centers: A system that stores excess DC power from renewable sources in batteries, providing a steady supply of power to data centers during periods of high demand or grid instability.

Solution 4: DC Power Distribution Units for Buildings: Modular units designed to distribute and manage DC power within buildings, reducing energy losses associated with AC-DC conversion and promoting the use of renewable energy sources.

Solution 5: DC Electric Vehicle Charging Stations for Ports, Industry, Data Centers, and Buildings: High-power charging stations that deliver DC electricity directly to electric vehicles, improving charging efficiency and reducing infrastructure costs compared to AC chargers.
