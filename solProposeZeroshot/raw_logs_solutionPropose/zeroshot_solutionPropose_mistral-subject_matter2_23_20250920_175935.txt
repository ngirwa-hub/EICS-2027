Solution 3: Modular DC Power Distribution Units for Data Centers: A scalable, easy-to-install solution for distributing DC power in data centers, improving efficiency, reducing cabling complexity, and facilitating the integration of renewable energy sources.

Solution 4: DC Microgrid Control and Optimization for Buildings: An intelligent system that manages the distribution and consumption of DC power within buildings, optimizing energy use, ensuring grid stability, and allowing for seamless integration of renewable energy sources.
