Solution 1: Direct Current (DC) Grid Integration for Ports: A comprehensive system designed to integrate DC power systems within port infrastructures, facilitating efficient charging of electric vessels and reducing energy losses associated with AC-DC conversion.

Solution 2: DC Distribution System for Industry: A high-voltage DC distribution network that supplies energy directly to industrial loads, improving efficiency and reducing the need for multiple conversion stages, particularly beneficial in heavy manufacturing environments.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Buildings: Small-scale, locally operated grids that generate, store, and distribute power within buildings using DC sources, enhancing energy efficiency and resilience while minimizing conversion losses.

Solution 4: DC Power Optimization for Data Centers: An advanced control system that optimizes the use of DC power in data centers by adjusting voltage levels to match varying loads, reducing energy waste and improving overall operational efficiency.

Solution 5: DC Renewable Energy Interconnection for Industry: A solution that enables direct connection of renewable energy sources to industrial loads using DC transmission lines, facilitating the integration of renewables into industry while minimizing energy losses and promoting grid stability.
