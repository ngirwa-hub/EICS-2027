Solution 1: High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Transmission for Ports: A power transmission system that uses high voltage direct current to transmit electricity over long distances, reducing energy losses and enabling efficient integration of renewable energy sources in ports.

Solution 2: DC Microgrids for Industry: Decentralized electric grids that operate independently from the main grid, using DC power for local industrial loads, improving efficiency and reliability while reducing energy losses during transmission.

Solution 3: Power Electronics-based Energy Management Systems (PE-EMS) for Data Centers: Intelligent systems that use power electronics to control and manage the distribution of electricity within data centers, optimizing energy usage and reducing overall energy consumption.

Solution 4: DC Distribution in Buildings: Replacing AC distribution networks with DC networks in buildings, offering increased efficiency, reduced power losses, and enabling seamless integration of renewable energy sources at the building level.

Solution 5: Flexible DC Power Architecture for Ports, Industry, Data Centers, and Buildings: A modular and adaptive power system that allows for easy integration of various DC components, promoting interoperability and facilitating the deployment of new technologies in diverse applications.
