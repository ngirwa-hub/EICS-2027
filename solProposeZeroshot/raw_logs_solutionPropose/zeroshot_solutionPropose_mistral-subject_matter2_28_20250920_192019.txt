1. Solution Title: Modular DC Power Distribution System for Ports
   Brief Explanation: A modular system designed for easy installation and maintenance in port environments, distributing power efficiently from high-voltage DC sources to various loads.

2. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Industrial Energy Management
   Brief Explanation: Decentralized energy production systems using renewable sources within industrial facilities, providing stable and reliable power supply while reducing carbon emissions.

3. Solution Title: DC Bus-tie Inverters for Data Centers
   Brief Explanation: A system that ties multiple DC power buses in data centers together, improving overall efficiency by allowing excess power to be shared among servers without AC conversion losses.

4. Solution Title: DC Building Integration and Management Systems (BIMSys)
   Brief Explanation: An intelligent control system for buildings integrating various energy sources (solar panels, fuel cells, batteries) and loads, optimizing the use of renewable energy while maintaining grid stability.

5. Solution Title: Smart DC Grid Islanding Solutions for Buildings
   Brief Explanation: A system that allows buildings to operate independently from the main grid during outages or emergencies, using stored energy in a safe and efficient manner.
