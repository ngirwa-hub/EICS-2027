Solution 2: DC Microgrids for Industry: A self-contained network of local energy sources and loads operated as a separate system within an industrial facility, providing reliable on-site power and reducing reliance on the main grid.

Solution 3: Energy Storage Systems for Buildings: Battery systems that store excess energy generated by renewable sources (e.g., solar panels) during off-peak hours for use during peak demand periods, improving overall energy efficiency in buildings.

Solution 4: DC Distribution Boards for Data Centers: High-efficiency distribution boards designed specifically for DC power, reducing transmission losses and improving the reliability of power supply in data centers.

Solution 5: DC Load Management Systems for Ports, Industry, and Buildings: Intelligent systems that optimize the distribution of DC power to various loads based on demand and availability, maximizing efficiency and minimizing energy waste.
