Solution 1: DC Grid Integration for Ports: A system that allows direct connection of multiple renewable energy sources (e.g., solar and wind) within a port, improving efficiency and reducing losses during energy conversion.

Solution 2: DC Distribution in Industry: Implementing DC power distribution networks in industrial facilities to reduce energy losses and improve operational efficiency, particularly for high-power applications such as electric arc furnaces or electrolysis processes.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: A decentralized, modular DC microgrid solution that can provide reliable and efficient power supply for data centers, reducing energy costs and improving resilience during grid outages.

Solution 4: DC Home Energy Management Systems (HEMS) for Buildings: An intelligent system that allows households to manage their energy consumption and production at the DC level, optimizing the use of renewable energy sources and improving overall energy efficiency.

Solution 5: DC-DC Optimization Controller for Buildings: A controller that optimizes power flow between distributed energy resources (e.g., solar panels, batteries) within a building's DC network, ensuring maximum efficiency and minimizing energy losses during conversion.
