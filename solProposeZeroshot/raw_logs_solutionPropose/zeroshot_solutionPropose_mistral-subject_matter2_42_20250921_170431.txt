Solution 1: Direct Current Microgrids for Ports: Decentralized power distribution systems that use DC as the primary power source within port facilities, reducing energy losses and increasing efficiency in handling various electrical loads.

Solution 2: DC Grid-tied Energy Storage Systems for Industry: Battery storage solutions designed to store excess energy generated by renewable sources during off-peak hours and discharge it during peak demand periods, improving grid stability and reducing energy costs.

Solution 3: DC High Voltage Distribution in Buildings: Employing DC distribution at higher voltages within buildings to reduce energy losses, enable more efficient power conversion, and facilitate the integration of distributed renewable energy sources like solar panels.

Solution 4: DC Power-over-Ethernet for Data Centers: A method that transmits both data and electrical power over a single Ethernet cable, reducing cabling complexity and improving energy efficiency in data center operations.

Solution 5: DC Fast Charging Stations for Electric Vehicles (EVs) at Ports: High-power charging stations using DC to rapidly charge EVs, providing quicker turnaround times for port vehicles and reducing overall energy consumption.
