Solution 1: DC Grid Tie Inverter for Ports: A device that converts excess DC power from renewable sources (e.g., solar or wind) into AC for grid injection at ports, promoting self-sufficiency and reducing reliance on fossil fuel generation.

Solution 2: DC Distribution System for Industry: An efficient high-voltage DC distribution network within industrial facilities to facilitate power transfer between various equipment, improving overall energy utilization and reducing conversion losses.

Solution 3: Modular DC Power Bus for Data Centers: A prefabricated, scalable, and modular DC power distribution system designed specifically for data centers, offering improved power density, reduced energy losses, and easier maintenance compared to traditional AC systems.

Solution 4: Smart DC Load Management for Buildings: An intelligent control system that optimizes the distribution of DC power within buildings, balancing loads across different circuits to ensure optimal efficiency and minimize power loss.

Solution 5: DC Microgrid Controller for Off-Grid Buildings: A control system designed to manage a standalone DC microgrid in off-grid buildings, allowing for local energy production, storage, and distribution, thereby enhancing resilience and reducing reliance on the main grid.
