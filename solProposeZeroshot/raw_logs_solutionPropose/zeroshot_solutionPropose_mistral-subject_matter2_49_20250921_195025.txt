Solution 1: Direct Current Grid Integration for Ports: Implementing a dedicated DC grid within port areas to streamline the handling and transfer of electricity from shore-side power supply systems, electric vehicles, and renewable energy sources like solar panels or wind turbines.

Solution 2: High Voltage DC Power Distribution in Industry: A modernized power distribution system for industrial facilities using high voltage DC (HVDC) to minimize losses during transmission, increase efficiency, and enable seamless integration of distributed renewable energy resources.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers: Creating on-site DC microgrids in data centers to reduce the need for AC-DC conversion, improve power quality, and enhance overall energy efficiency while ensuring uninterrupted operation during grid outages.

Solution 4: Building Automation Systems with DC Buses: Utilizing DC buses in building automation systems (BAS) to integrate various electrical loads such as lighting, HVAC, and appliances, streamlining power distribution, reducing losses, and facilitating renewable energy integration within buildings.

Solution 5: Smart Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles in Buildings: Deploying intelligent charging systems that utilize DC power for electric vehicles within buildings, optimizing charging schedules to minimize peak demand, improving overall efficiency, and reducing the strain on grid infrastructure.
