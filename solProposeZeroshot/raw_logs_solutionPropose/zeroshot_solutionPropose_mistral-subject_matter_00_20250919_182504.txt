1. Solution Title: DC Grid Tie Inverter for Ports
   Brief Explanation: A device that converts DC power from renewable energy sources (e.g., solar or wind) into AC power suitable for grid connection in ports, facilitating efficient energy storage and distribution.

2. Solution Title: Modular DC Power Distribution System for Industry
   Brief Explanation: A scalable system designed to distribute DC power efficiently within industrial facilities, reducing energy losses and promoting the integration of renewable energy sources into existing industrial infrastructure.

3. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Data Centers
   Brief Explanation: A self-contained group of electricity production, storage, and consumption devices that operate connected to or independently from the traditional grid, providing a more efficient and reliable power source for data centers.

4. Solution Title: On-site DC Generation and Storage for Buildings
   Brief Explanation: Installation of solar panels or other renewable energy sources on building rooftops, with integrated battery storage to provide DC power directly to the building's electrical system, reducing reliance on the grid and improving energy efficiency.

5. Solution Title: DC-DC Power Optimizers for Building-Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV)
   Brief Explanation: Maximizing the energy output of solar panels integrated into building materials by optimizing the DC voltage and current at the panel level, improving overall system efficiency and reducing energy losses.
