1. Solution Title: Modular DC Power Distribution for Buildings
   Brief Explanation: A system that uses modular components to provide flexible and scalable DC power distribution within buildings, enabling efficient integration of renewable energy sources and reducing energy loss during conversion.

2. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Ports
   Brief Explanation: Implementing DC microgrids in ports can help manage the high power demands of electrical equipment, reduce energy losses from AC-DC conversions, and facilitate the integration of renewable energy sources such as solar and wind.

3. Solution Title: High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Transmission for Industry
   Brief Explanation: Utilizing HVDC transmission lines to transport power over long distances from renewable energy sources to industrial facilities, minimizing transmission losses and increasing the efficiency of energy supply.

4. Solution Title: DC Power Bus Systems in Data Centers
   Brief Explanation: Replacing AC power distribution systems with DC power buses in data centers can improve energy efficiency, reduce conversion losses, and enable higher power density for increased computing capabilities.

5. Solution Title: Smart Energy Management System (SEMS) for Buildings
   Brief Explanation: A system that integrates various energy sources, including renewables, and manages the distribution of energy throughout buildings in real-time to optimize efficiency and reduce energy waste.
