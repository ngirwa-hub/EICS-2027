Solution 1: DC Microgrid Control System for Ports: A centralized control system that optimizes the distribution and management of DC power within port facilities, ensuring efficient energy usage and reducing operational costs.

Solution 2: DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs) for Data Centers: High-efficiency DC PDUs that replace AC power systems in data centers, improving energy efficiency, reducing losses, and enabling scalable, modular infrastructure.

Solution 3: DC Grid Tie Inverters for Industry: Devices that convert excess industrial DC power to AC for grid integration, maximizing the utilization of renewable energy sources and promoting self-sufficiency.

Solution 4: DC Building Automation System (BAS): A system that integrates heating, ventilation, air conditioning, lighting, and other building services with a centralized DC control network, reducing energy consumption and improving comfort.

Solution 5: DC Energy Storage Solutions for Buildings: Battery storage systems designed to store excess power generated by renewable sources within buildings, enabling self-consumption and grid support during peak demand periods.
