Solution 1: Modular DC Grid Architecture for Buildings: A scalable, flexible, and easily expandable DC grid system designed specifically for buildings, facilitating the integration of multiple renewable energy sources and improving overall efficiency.

Solution 2: DC Microgrid Control System for Ports: An advanced control system that manages the distribution and flow of power within a DC microgrid in ports, ensuring optimal operation of various port equipment and enhancing grid stability.

Solution 3: High-Power DC-DC Converters for Industry: Efficient high-power converters designed to handle the demanding power requirements of industrial applications, enabling seamless integration of renewable energy sources into existing industrial systems.

Solution 4: DC-AC Power Conditioning System for Data Centers: A system that converts DC power from renewable energy sources to AC power for data center usage, offering improved efficiency and reduced conversion losses compared to traditional AC power systems.

Solution 5: Smart Grid Interface for Building Energy Management Systems (BEMS): An intelligent interface that enables communication between building energy management systems and the wider smart grid, facilitating optimal energy consumption, demand response, and grid integration in buildings.
