Solution 1: High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Cable Systems for Underwater Power Transmission: These systems are designed for long-distance power transmission underwater, enabling efficient transfer of energy from offshore renewable sources like wind farms to onshore grids.

Solution 2: DC Microgrids for Ports: Implementing DC microgrids within ports can provide a more efficient and flexible distribution of electricity, accommodating various power needs such as electric vessels, cranes, and other port equipment.

Solution 3: Energy Storage Solutions for Data Centers: Utilizing batteries or supercapacitors to store excess energy generated by on-site renewable sources in data centers can ensure continuous operation during peak demand or grid outages.

Solution 4: DC Building Automation Systems: Integrating building automation systems with DC power can improve energy efficiency, as low-voltage DC circuits have lower losses than AC counterparts, and can enable the direct use of solar energy in buildings.

Solution 5: Modular DC Distribution for Industrial Applications: A modular, scalable DC distribution system can simplify the integration of renewables in industrial settings, allowing for easy addition or removal of power sources as needed, while also reducing conversion losses.
