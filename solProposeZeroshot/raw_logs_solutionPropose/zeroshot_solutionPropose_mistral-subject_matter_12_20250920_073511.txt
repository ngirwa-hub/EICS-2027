Solution 1: High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Transmission Systems for Ports:
A technology that allows for efficient and long-distance transmission of large amounts of power from renewable energy sources to ports, reducing the need for local fossil fuel generation.

Solution 2: DC Power Distribution in Industrial Facilities:
A system designed to distribute power directly within industrial facilities using DC voltage, improving efficiency and reducing losses compared to traditional AC systems.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Data Centers:
A self-contained group of electricity sources, energy storage, and loads that can operate autonomously from the main grid, providing stable power for data centers with minimal losses.

Solution 4: Building Integrated Photovoltaic (BIPV) Systems for Buildings:
A design approach that combines solar panels with building materials to generate electricity on-site while reducing construction costs and improving aesthetics.

Solution 5: DC Load Management in Residential Buildings:
A smart system that optimizes the distribution of power within buildings, prioritizing essential loads during outages or peak demand periods to maintain critical services and reduce overall energy consumption.
