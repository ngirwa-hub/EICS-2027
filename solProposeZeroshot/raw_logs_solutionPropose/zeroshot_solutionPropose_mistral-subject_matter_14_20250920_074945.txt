1. Solution Title: DC Port Load Management System
   Brief Explanation: A system that optimizes the distribution and management of electric power from multiple DC sources at a port, ensuring efficient use of resources and minimizing downtime for vessels.

2. Solution Title: DC Grid Energy Storage in Industrial Facilities
   Brief Explanation: A large-scale energy storage solution that stores excess energy generated by renewable sources during off-peak hours and releases it during peak industrial demand, enhancing grid stability and reducing energy costs.

3. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Data Centers
   Brief Explanation: A self-contained group of electricity sources and loads within a data center, providing power locally and reducing reliance on the main grid, improving efficiency and resilience.

4. Solution Title: Building Energy Management System (BEMS) with DC Capability
   Brief Explanation: An intelligent system that controls and monitors energy use in buildings, including DC power distribution, to optimize energy consumption and reduce costs while maintaining comfort levels.

5. Solution Title: DC Mini-Grid for Off-Grid Buildings
   Brief Explanation: A small-scale, self-sustaining electric grid that provides electricity to off-grid buildings using renewable energy sources and DC power distribution, ensuring continuous power supply without relying on the main grid.
