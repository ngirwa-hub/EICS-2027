1. Solution Title: High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Transmission for Ports:
   Brief explanation: Utilizing HVDC technology for long-distance power transmission from renewable energy sources to ports, improving grid stability and reducing transmission losses.

2. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Data Centers:
   Brief explanation: Implementing DC microgrids within data centers to reduce conversion losses, increase efficiency, and enable the integration of on-site renewable energy sources.

3. Solution Title: Direct Current Arc Fault Protection for Industry:
   Brief explanation: Incorporating advanced arc fault protection systems in DC industrial applications to minimize hazards, improve safety, and prevent equipment damage.

4. Solution Title: DC Building Energy Management Systems (BEMS):
   Brief explanation: Implementing DC-based building energy management systems to optimize energy consumption, facilitate renewable energy integration, and enhance overall efficiency.

5. Solution Title: DC Integrated Solar Power for Buildings:
   Brief explanation: Installing solar panels with built-in DC power electronics that directly produce DC power, eliminating the need for additional AC-DC conversion and reducing system complexity.
