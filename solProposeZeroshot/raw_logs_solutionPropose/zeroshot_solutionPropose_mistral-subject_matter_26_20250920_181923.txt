Solution 1: Direct Current Arc Fault Detection: A system designed for ports and industrial settings that can quickly and accurately detect and isolate direct current arc faults in high-power equipment to ensure safety and prevent equipment damage.

Solution 2: DC Grid Tie Inverter (GTIs) for Buildings: GTIs enable renewable energy generation, such as solar or wind, to feed directly into a building's DC power system, bypassing the need for AC-DC conversion and reducing energy losses.

Solution 3: Modular Power Distribution Units (MPDUs) for Data Centers: MPDUs are prefabricated, self-contained DC power distribution systems designed to improve efficiency, reduce installation time, and increase scalability in data center applications.

Solution 4: Smart Charging Stations for Electric Vehicles (EVs) in Ports and Buildings: Intelligent charging stations that can manage and optimize the charging of electric vehicles based on factors such as demand, battery state-of-charge, and available renewable energy generation.

Solution 5: DC Microgrid Energy Management Systems for Industry: A system to effectively control, monitor, and coordinate the distribution and consumption of electricity in industrial microgrids, ensuring optimal efficiency and resilience.
