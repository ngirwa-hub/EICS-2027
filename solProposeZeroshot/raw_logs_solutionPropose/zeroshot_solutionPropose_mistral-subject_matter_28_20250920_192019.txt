Solution 1: DC Grid Management System for Ports: A control system that optimizes the distribution and usage of DC power within port facilities, improving efficiency and reducing energy losses.

Solution 2: High-Voltage DC Transmission for Industry: Implementing high-voltage DC transmission lines to transport power from renewable sources directly to industrial sites, reducing power conversion losses and increasing grid capacity.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Buildings: Integrating DC microgrids into building infrastructure to support local renewable energy generation, improve energy efficiency, and enable decentralized power management.

Solution 4: DC Distribution Boards in Data Centers: Replacing AC distribution boards with DC counterparts in data centers to reduce energy conversion losses, increase power density, and enhance overall efficiency.

Solution 5: Smart Charging Stations for Electric Vehicles (EVs) at Ports and Buildings: Intelligent charging stations that support direct current charging of electric vehicles, ensuring fast and efficient charging while reducing the demand on AC power infrastructure.
