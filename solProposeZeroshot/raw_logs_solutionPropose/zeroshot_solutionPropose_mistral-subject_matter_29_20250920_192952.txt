1. Solution Title: Modular DC Power Distribution Units for Buildings
   Brief Explanation: Pre-fabricated units that distribute power from a central DC source to individual loads within buildings, promoting energy efficiency and reducing installation costs.

2. Solution Title: DC Microgrids for Ports
   Brief Explanation: Decentralized power systems for ports using renewable sources like solar panels and wind turbines, providing reliable and sustainable electricity supply with minimal grid interaction.

3. Solution Title: High-Power DC-DC Converters for Industry
   Brief Explanation: Efficient converters that step up or down the voltage of power supplies in industrial applications, enabling seamless integration of various renewable energy sources into existing systems.

4. Solution Title: Smart DC Load Management Systems for Data Centers
   Brief Explanation: Intelligent systems that optimize the distribution and consumption of DC power within data centers by monitoring load requirements and adjusting power flow accordingly to minimize waste and improve efficiency.

5. Solution Title: DC Battery Swap Stations for Electric Vehicles in Ports
   Brief Explanation: Fast-charging stations that use DC power for electric vehicles, allowing them to quickly swap depleted batteries for fully charged ones, improving turnaround times at ports and reducing infrastructure costs.
