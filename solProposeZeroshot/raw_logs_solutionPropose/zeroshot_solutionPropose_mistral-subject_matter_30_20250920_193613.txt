Solution 1: High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Grid Tie Inverters for Ports: These devices convert the high voltage DC power generated by renewable sources into AC power that can be fed into the main grid, making it easier to integrate renewables in port operations.

Solution 2: DC Microgrids for Buildings: A system that integrates multiple distributed energy resources (DERs) within a building, providing on-site electricity generation, storage, and distribution, enhancing resilience and reducing energy costs.

Solution 3: DC Power Distribution for Industry: An efficient method of power distribution within industrial facilities using low-voltage DC systems instead of traditional AC systems, improving safety, reducing energy losses, and enabling easier integration of renewable sources.

Solution 4: DC Battery Buffering Systems for Data Centers: A solution that uses batteries to store excess power during off-peak hours and discharge it during peak demand, ensuring a stable power supply and minimizing reliance on the grid.

Solution 5: DC Arc Fault Detection for Ports and Buildings: A system that monitors for dangerous electrical arcs in high-risk areas like switchboards, transformer rooms, and machinery, reducing fire hazards and improving safety in ports and buildings.
