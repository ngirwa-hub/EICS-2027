These inverters enable ports to connect to the HVDC grid, facilitating efficient power exchange and reducing losses during transmission.

Solution 2: DC-based Energy Storage Systems for Data Centers:
Integrating DC energy storage systems within data centers can improve energy efficiency by minimizing conversion losses associated with AC energy storage.

Solution 3: DC Microgrids for Industrial Applications:
DC microgrids offer a flexible and efficient power solution for industrial facilities, allowing for direct connection of various equipment and reducing conversion losses.

Solution 4: Smart DC Distribution Systems for Buildings:
Intelligent distribution systems that utilize DC power can optimize energy consumption, improve safety, and enable seamless integration of renewable energy sources within buildings.

Solution 5: DC Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles in Ports:
Installing DC charging stations at ports can support the rapid charging of electric vehicles, reducing charging times and facilitating the transition to sustainable transportation solutions.
