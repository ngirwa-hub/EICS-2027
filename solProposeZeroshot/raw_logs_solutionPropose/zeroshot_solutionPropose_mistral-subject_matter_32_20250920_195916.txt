Solution 1: DC Grid Tie Inverter for Ports: A system that converts excess renewable energy generated at ports into DC power for grid integration or storage, enhancing the efficiency and stability of port-based power systems.

Solution 2: DC Microgrid Controller for Industry: An intelligent controller to manage multiple energy sources in industrial DC microgrids, optimizing load management, energy distribution, and energy storage.

Solution 3: Direct Current Arc Fault Protection for Buildings: A system that detects and suppresses dangerous electrical arcs in building DC circuits, ensuring safety and reducing the risk of fires or equipment damage.

Solution 4: DC Energy Management System for Data Centers: An integrated energy management platform that optimizes power usage, cooling, and energy storage within data centers, improving efficiency and reducing costs.

Solution 5: DC-AC Inverter for Onshore Wind Turbines in Industry: A device that converts the direct current generated by onshore wind turbines into alternating current suitable for integration into existing AC power grids, facilitating renewable energy adoption in industrial settings.

These solutions aim to address specific challenges and opportunities related to ports, industry, data centers, and buildings while contributing to the wider goals of the Shift2DC project.
