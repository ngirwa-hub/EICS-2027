Solution 1: Direct Current Power Distribution Units (DC-PDUs) for Buildings:
A modular system that allows for efficient distribution of DC power within buildings, reducing energy losses and simplifying the wiring process.

Solution 2: DC Microgrids for Ports:
Decentralized electrical grids that can operate independently and connect to the main grid when necessary, providing resilient and flexible power supply for port operations.

Solution 3: High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Transmission for Industry:
Long-distance transmission of DC power from renewable energy sources to industrial sites, reducing line losses and improving grid stability.

Solution 4: DC Bus Systems for Data Centers:
A compact and efficient way to distribute large amounts of DC power within data centers, improving power density and reducing cooling requirements.

Solution 5: On-Site Renewable Energy Integration Inverters for Buildings:
Inverters that convert solar or wind energy directly to DC, eliminating the need for AC conversion and reducing system complexity in buildings.
