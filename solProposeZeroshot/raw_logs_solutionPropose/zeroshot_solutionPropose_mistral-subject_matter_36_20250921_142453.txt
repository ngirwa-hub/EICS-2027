Solution 2: DC Microgrid Control for Industry: An advanced control system for industrial microgrids operating on direct current, ensuring optimal load management, power quality, and efficient integration of distributed energy resources.

Solution 3: DC Energy Storage Solutions for Data Centers: Implementing high-capacity battery storage systems that operate directly with DC power in data centers, improving efficiency and reducing conversion losses.

Solution 4: DC Distribution Networks for Buildings: Designing buildings with direct current distribution networks, providing a more efficient and safer method of energy delivery within the structure while facilitating integration of renewable energy sources.

Solution 5: Smart Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles in Buildings: A smart charging infrastructure that supports electric vehicles operating on direct current, optimizing charging efficiency, reducing peak demand, and enabling vehicle-to-grid capabilities.
