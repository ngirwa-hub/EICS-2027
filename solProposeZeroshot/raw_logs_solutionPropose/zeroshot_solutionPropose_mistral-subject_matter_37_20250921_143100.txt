Solution 1: Modular DC Power Distribution Units for Buildings: Pre-fabricated, easy-to-install units that simplify the distribution and management of DC power within buildings, improving safety and efficiency.

Solution 2: DC Microgrids for Ports: Decentralized energy systems that generate, store, and distribute electricity using DC power at ports, reducing losses during transmission and increasing resilience.

Solution 3: High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Transmission for Industry: A more efficient method of transmitting large amounts of DC power over long distances for industrial applications, reducing energy loss and promoting the use of renewable sources.

Solution 4: DC Energy Management Systems for Data Centers: Real-time monitoring and control systems that optimize the distribution and utilization of DC power in data centers, improving efficiency and reducing costs.

Solution 5: Smart Grid Interface for Building Integration: A device that enables seamless integration between DC microgrids and the broader electricity grid in buildings, promoting renewable energy adoption and grid stability.
