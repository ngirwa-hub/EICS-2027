Solution 1: High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Grid Integration: A system that allows for seamless integration of renewable energy sources into existing or new high-voltage direct current grids, increasing grid stability and reducing losses during transmission.

Solution 2: DC Microgrids for Ports: Implementing DC microgrids in ports to power electric vehicles, cranes, and other port equipment, improving efficiency and reducing emissions from diesel-powered machinery.

Solution 3: DC Distribution within Industrial Facilities: Utilizing DC distribution networks within industrial facilities to reduce energy losses and improve the efficiency of power supply to various loads, such as electric motors and lighting systems.

Solution 4: Energy Storage Systems for Data Centers: Implementing high-capacity energy storage solutions, such as batteries or flywheels, to store excess renewable energy during off-peak hours and provide a stable power supply during peak demand.

Solution 5: DC Building Automation Systems: Integrating building automation systems (HVAC, lighting, security) with DC power for improved efficiency, reduced energy losses, and compatibility with renewable energy sources.
