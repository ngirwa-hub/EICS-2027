1. **DC Microgrid Control System**: A management tool that optimizes the operation and distribution of power in DC microgrids, improving energy efficiency and reliability across ports, industries, data centers, and buildings.

2. **DC Power Quality Analyzer**: An advanced monitoring device to assess voltage levels, harmonics, and other quality factors in DC systems, ensuring stable and efficient operation in varied applications like industrial processes or data center operations.

3. **DC-Powered Cooling Systems**: Innovative cooling solutions specifically designed for DC systems that reduce energy consumption by eliminating AC-DC conversions typically required in traditional setups, enhancing efficiency in data centers and buildings.

4. **Hybrid Energy Storage System (HESS)**: A system combining different types of storage technologies such as batteries and supercapacitors optimized for DC applications, increasing flexibility and capacity in industrial settings or ports where power demands can be variable.

5. **DC-AC Hybrid Inverters**: Devices that efficiently manage energy flow between AC and DC systems, enabling seamless integration of renewable sources like solar panels into existing AC infrastructure commonly found in buildings and industries.
