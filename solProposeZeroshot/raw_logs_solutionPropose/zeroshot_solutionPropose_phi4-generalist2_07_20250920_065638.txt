1. **DC Microgrid Management System: Smart control systems for optimizing energy flow and distribution within DC microgrids in ports and industrial settings.**  
   These systems ensure efficient energy management by dynamically balancing supply and demand, reducing energy waste, and enhancing reliability.

2. **DC-Powered Electric Vehicle Charging Stations: Infrastructure that utilizes direct current for fast-charging electric vehicles at ports and commercial buildings.**  
   By directly using DC, these stations reduce conversion losses compared to AC-based systems, offering quicker charging times and improving overall efficiency.

3. **Integrated Renewable Energy Systems (IRES): Combined solar, wind, or other renewable sources with DC technology to create self-sufficient energy solutions for industries and data centers.**  
   These integrated systems optimize the use of local renewable resources, minimizing dependency on external power grids and reducing carbon footprints.

4. **DC-Based Lighting Solutions: LED lighting designed specifically for DC power applications in industrial buildings and data centers, enhancing efficiency and reducing costs.**  
   Directly powered by DC, these solutions eliminate energy loss from AC to DC conversion, resulting in lower operational costs and increased lifespan of lighting systems.

5. **Energy Harvesting Devices for Data Centers: Systems that capture ambient energy (e.g., heat or kinetic energy) and convert it into usable DC power to support data center operations.**  
   These devices help reduce the overall energy consumption of data centers by utilizing otherwise wasted energy sources, contributing to sustainability goals.
