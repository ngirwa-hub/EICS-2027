1. **DC Power Distribution System for Ports:**
   A system that enhances port operations by directly distributing power through DC networks, reducing energy conversion losses and improving overall efficiency.

2. **Industrial Process Optimization with DC Integration:**
   This solution involves tailoring industrial processes to utilize direct current, minimizing the need for AC-DC conversions, which leads to lower operational costs and improved process reliability.

3. **Data Center Energy Efficiency Enhancement using DC Power:**
   A strategy that leverages DC power directly within data centers to reduce conversion losses from AC to DC, thereby increasing energy efficiency and reducing cooling requirements.

4. **Building-Level DC Microgrid Implementation:**
   This involves setting up microgrids at the building level powered by DC, enhancing energy management through direct use of renewable sources like solar panels, and improving resilience during power outages.

5. **DC Power Quality Monitoring System:**
   A tool designed to continuously monitor the quality of DC power in various applications such as ports and industrial settings, ensuring stable and efficient operation while identifying issues before they escalate.
