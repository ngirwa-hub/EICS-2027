1. **DC Power Distribution Units**: These units efficiently distribute direct current power across facilities such as ports or data centers, reducing conversion losses and improving energy efficiency.

2. **DC Cooling Systems for Data Centers**: Leveraging the integration of cooling systems with DC infrastructure to enhance efficiency and reduce thermal management costs in data centers by using DC directly from renewable sources.

3. **Industrial DC Motors**: These motors are designed to operate on direct current power, offering higher efficiency and longer lifespans compared to AC motors, which is particularly beneficial for industrial applications that require precise control.

4. **DC-Powered Lighting Solutions**: Utilizing LED lighting systems powered by direct current can reduce energy losses in buildings and offer more efficient operation compared to traditional AC-powered systems.

5. **Hybrid Renewable Energy Systems with DC Integration**: Combining solar panels or wind turbines with a DC network within ports or industrial sites allows for seamless integration of renewable power sources, minimizing the need for AC-DC conversions and enhancing overall system resilience.
