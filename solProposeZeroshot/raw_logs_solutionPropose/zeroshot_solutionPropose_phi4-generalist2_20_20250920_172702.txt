1. **DC Power Management System:** A centralized system designed to optimize the distribution and use of direct current within data centers or industrial facilities, enhancing energy efficiency by reducing conversion losses between AC and DC power.

2. **Modular DC Distribution Panels:** Customizable panels that allow flexible integration of various DC-powered devices in buildings, promoting efficient energy usage and simplifying maintenance operations.

3. **High-Efficiency DC Microinverters for Solar Arrays:** Specialized inverters that convert solar-generated DC to DC at high efficiency levels, suitable for both large-scale industrial applications and individual building rooftops.

4. **Integrated DC Charging Infrastructure for Ports:** Systems designed to facilitate the rapid charging of electric vehicles or ships using direct current, supporting a shift towards electrification in port operations and reducing reliance on fossil fuels.

5. **DC-based HVAC System for Buildings:** A heating, ventilation, and air conditioning system that operates directly from DC power, minimizing conversion losses and improving overall energy efficiency for residential and commercial buildings.
