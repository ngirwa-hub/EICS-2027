1. **DC Power Distribution Network:** A framework that efficiently distributes power in DC form within ports and industrial facilities, reducing energy loss compared to AC systems and enhancing overall efficiency.

2. **DC Load Balancing System:** An intelligent system designed to optimize the distribution of electrical loads across a network, ensuring stability and reliability in data centers and large buildings by minimizing peak demand stress on the grid.

3. **Renewable Energy Integration Module:** A technology that seamlessly integrates renewable energy sources like solar panels or wind turbines into DC grids used in ports and industries, maximizing the use of green energy while maintaining consistent power supply levels.

4. **DC Microgrid Control System:** An advanced control mechanism for managing DC microgrids in buildings, allowing for autonomous operation, improved fault tolerance, and enhanced resilience against outages by coordinating distributed generation and storage resources.

5. **DC Energy Efficiency Audit Tool:** A diagnostic tool that assesses energy usage within industries and data centers to identify areas where efficiency can be improved through the adoption of DC technologies, leading to reduced operational costs and lower carbon footprints.
