1. **DC Microgrid Management System:** A platform that optimizes the operation and integration of various DC energy sources and loads within a microgrid, enhancing efficiency and reliability across sectors like ports and data centers.

2. **Hybrid Power Conditioning Unit (HPCU):** A device designed to seamlessly integrate multiple renewable energy sources with existing AC and DC infrastructure in industries, ensuring stable and efficient power distribution.

3. **DC Energy Storage Integration:** A solution for integrating battery storage directly within DC systems in buildings, improving efficiency by avoiding AC-DC conversion losses and enhancing resilience.

4. **Dynamic Load Management System:** An intelligent system to optimize energy consumption patterns of industrial and data center equipment based on real-time demand and supply conditions, facilitating better use of DC power.

5. **IoT-based Monitoring for DC Systems:** A solution employing Internet of Things (IoT) technology to provide continuous monitoring and management of DC infrastructure across ports and buildings, improving maintenance efficiency and system reliability.
