1. **DC Microgrid Control System: Port and industry-specific energy management tools**  
   These systems optimize the distribution and usage of power within a direct current microgrid setup, enhancing efficiency in sectors like ports and industrial facilities.

2. **DC Renewable Integration Module: Seamless integration of renewable sources into DC networks**  
   This module facilitates the incorporation of solar panels or wind turbines directly into DC grids for buildings and data centers, maximizing renewable energy utilization without complex AC conversion processes.

3. **High-Efficiency Power Inverters for Industry:** Advanced inverters to convert high-voltage DC efficiently within industrial settings  
   These inverters provide a reliable method of converting DC power to meet various voltage and current requirements in industrial applications, improving efficiency and reducing losses.

4. **DC Cooling Solutions for Data Centers: Innovative cooling methods tailored for direct current systems**  
   Using DC-based cooling technologies that leverage the inherent characteristics of DC power can enhance energy efficiency and reduce operational costs in data center environments.

5. **Energy Management Software for Smart Buildings:** Tailored software solutions for optimizing DC power usage in buildings  
   This tool aids in managing and monitoring DC power consumption within smart buildings, enabling more effective energy use strategies and supporting sustainability goals.
