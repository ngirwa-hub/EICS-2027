1. **DC-Powered Automated Equipment: Specialized machinery for ports powered by direct current**  
   Ports often utilize heavy equipment like cranes and automated guided vehicles (AGVs). Using DC power directly can reduce conversion losses and improve energy efficiency in these applications.

2. **Industrial Process Optimization via DC Power**: Tailoring the power supply to match specific industrial process needs using direct current.  
   In industry, processes such as electrochemical manufacturing or high-frequency plasma cutting benefit from stable and efficient DC power sources, enhancing precision and reducing waste.

3. **Data Center Power Redundancy through DC Backups**: Implementing DC-based backup systems in data centers for improved reliability and reduced downtime.  
   Data centers can use DC-powered battery backups to ensure continuous operation during outages, bypassing AC conversion stages and increasing efficiency.

4. **DC-Integrated Building Management Systems: Smart building management systems that leverage DC power for enhanced efficiency**  
   Modern buildings with integrated lighting, HVAC, and security systems can benefit from DC integration, which minimizes energy loss by eliminating unnecessary AC conversions.

5. **DC Renewable Integration Platform**: A system to efficiently integrate various renewable sources like solar or wind into existing DC infrastructure.  
   This platform optimizes the use of renewable energy in sectors such as ports and buildings, reducing dependency on fossil fuels and enhancing sustainability efforts.
