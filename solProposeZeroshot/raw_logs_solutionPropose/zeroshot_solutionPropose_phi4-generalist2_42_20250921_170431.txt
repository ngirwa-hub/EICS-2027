1. **DC Microgrid Integration Platform**: A centralized system for managing multiple DC power sources and loads within a facility, optimizing energy usage, reducing losses, and enhancing reliability.

2. **Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs)**: Scalable units that distribute DC power efficiently in buildings or data centers, simplifying the integration of diverse renewable energy sources and improving load management.

3. **DC-Powered Smart Building Management Systems**: Integrated systems that leverage DC power for controlling building functions such as lighting, HVAC, and security, enhancing energy efficiency by reducing conversion losses.

4. **Industrial Process DC Power Solutions**: Tailored DC power supplies that directly power industrial machinery or processes, minimizing conversion stages and improving operational efficiency in manufacturing settings.

5. **DC Energy Storage Optimization Tool**: A software tool designed to optimize the performance of battery storage systems within a DC grid, ensuring efficient energy use and supporting grid stability during peak demand periods.
