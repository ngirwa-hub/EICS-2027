1. **DC Microgrid Management System:** A platform that optimizes the operation and management of DC microgrids in ports, industries, data centers, and buildings by efficiently integrating various DC sources and loads.

2. **Energy Demand Forecasting Tool:** An analytics tool designed to predict energy consumption patterns in sectors like industry and data centers, aiding in efficient load balancing and resource allocation for DC systems.

3. **DC Power Quality Monitoring System:** A device that continuously assesses the quality of DC power supplied within industrial setups or buildings, ensuring consistent performance and identifying issues early.

4. **Portable DC Charging Infrastructure:** An easily deployable solution that provides flexible charging capabilities for electric vehicles and equipment in ports or logistics hubs using DC technology.

5. **DC Load Balancing System:** A system designed to distribute electrical load efficiently across different areas within a building or data center, reducing strain on infrastructure and enhancing energy efficiency.
