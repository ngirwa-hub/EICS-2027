3. **DC-Coupled Energy Management Software**: Software tools designed for optimizing the distribution and use of DC power within data centers, focusing on minimizing energy waste and maximizing system reliability.

4. **Modular DC Power Distribution Units**: Flexible units that distribute direct current efficiently across different sections of buildings or industrial facilities, facilitating scalability and ease of maintenance.

5. **DC-Powered HVAC Systems**: Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems powered by direct current to improve energy efficiency in commercial buildings and data centers.
