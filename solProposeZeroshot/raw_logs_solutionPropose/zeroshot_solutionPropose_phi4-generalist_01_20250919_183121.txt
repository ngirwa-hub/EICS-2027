1. **DC Microgrid Systems: Integration for Port Facilities**  
   These systems enable direct connection and management of various DC-powered devices in ports, enhancing energy efficiency by reducing conversion losses.

2. **Industrial Process Optimization via DC Drives**  
   Using DC drives in industrial machinery improves control precision and reduces energy consumption compared to traditional AC motors, leading to more efficient operations.

3. **DC Power Distribution for Data Centers**  
   Direct current distribution within data centers minimizes power losses associated with frequent AC-DC conversions, enhancing overall energy efficiency and reducing cooling demands.

4. **Smart Building Management Systems (BMS) with DC Integration**  
   These systems manage energy use in buildings by optimizing the operation of DC-powered devices, improving energy savings, and facilitating seamless integration with renewable sources like solar panels.

5. **Resilient DC Microgrid for Remote Industrial Sites**  
   This solution provides reliable power to remote industrial locations through self-sufficient DC microgrids that integrate local renewable energy generation, reducing dependency on external AC grids.
