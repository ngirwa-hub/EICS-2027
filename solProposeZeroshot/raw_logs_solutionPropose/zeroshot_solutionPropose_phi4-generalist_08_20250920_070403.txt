1. **DC Energy Management System**: A platform that optimizes energy usage and distribution in real-time across various sectors like ports, industry, data centers, and buildings by leveraging direct current systems.

2. **High-Efficiency DC Power Conditioning Unit**: Equipment designed to enhance the efficiency of power conversion and conditioning processes, particularly beneficial for industrial applications where large amounts of electricity need precise management.

3. **DC Microgrid Controller**: A centralized system that manages the flow and distribution of DC power within microgrids, improving reliability and reducing energy losses in sectors such as data centers and ports.

4. **Integrated DC Charging Infrastructure**: For ports and buildings, this solution involves creating infrastructure to support electric vehicles using direct current, facilitating faster charging times and more efficient energy use.

5. **Thermal Management System for DC Applications**: A system designed to efficiently manage heat generated by DC equipment in industries and data centers, ensuring optimal performance and reducing cooling requirements.
