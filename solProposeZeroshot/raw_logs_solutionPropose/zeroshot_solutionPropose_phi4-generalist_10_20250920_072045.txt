1. **DC Power Quality Management System**: A system designed to monitor and control the quality of power in DC networks, ensuring consistent voltage levels and reducing disturbances for sensitive industrial processes or data center operations.

2. **Hybrid Renewable Energy Integration Platform**: A platform that seamlessly integrates multiple renewable energy sources (like solar and wind) with existing DC infrastructure, optimizing energy use and improving sustainability at ports and industrial sites.

3. **DC Microgrid Management Software**: Advanced software tools for managing DC microgrids, enabling efficient distribution of power among various users in buildings and industries while enhancing reliability and reducing downtime.

4. **DC Load Balancing Technology**: A technology that optimizes the distribution of electrical loads within a DC network, ensuring efficient energy use and minimizing strain on infrastructure, crucial for high-demand environments like data centers and industrial complexes.

5. **Energy Demand Forecasting Tool**: An AI-driven tool to predict future energy demands in real-time across different sectors (ports, industry, data centers, buildings), allowing better planning and integration of DC technologies to meet these needs efficiently.
