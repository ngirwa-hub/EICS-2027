1. **DC Microgrid Management System**: A platform that optimizes energy distribution and consumption within a DC microgrid setup at ports, industries, data centers, or buildings. This system enhances efficiency by managing loads dynamically.

2. **Integrated DC Solar Harvesting**: An approach to seamlessly integrate solar panels into existing infrastructure for direct DC power supply in sectors like industry and data centers, reducing the need for AC conversion and improving energy efficiency.

3. **DC Power Quality Monitoring**: A technology that ensures consistent quality of DC power supplied across different nodes within ports or industrial complexes, helping prevent disruptions due to voltage fluctuations or harmonics.

4. **Thermal Management Solutions for DC Systems**: Techniques or devices designed to manage the heat generated by DC components in densely packed environments like data centers and industrial settings, ensuring reliability and performance.

5. **DC Energy Storage Optimization Tool**: A software tool that enhances the charging and discharging efficiency of DC batteries used in buildings or data centers, maximizing their lifespan and performance while minimizing energy loss.
