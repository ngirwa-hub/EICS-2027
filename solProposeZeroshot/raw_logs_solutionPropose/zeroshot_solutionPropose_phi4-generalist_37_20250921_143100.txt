Certainly! Here are some additional direct current (DC) solutions that could be important for the sectors of ports, industry, data centers, and buildings:

1. **DC Microgrid Systems:** Integrated networks allowing localized generation, distribution, and consumption of electricity using DC power, enhancing energy efficiency and reliability.

2. **Hybrid Energy Management System:** A platform to optimize the use of multiple renewable sources (like solar and wind) with battery storage in a DC framework for enhanced performance and reduced costs.

3. **DC-Powered HVAC Systems:** Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems designed to operate directly on DC power, improving energy efficiency especially in buildings with substantial solar generation.

4. **Renewable Energy Integration Platform:** A tool that facilitates seamless integration of various renewable energy sources into existing infrastructure using DC technology, particularly beneficial for ports and industrial sites.

5. **DC-Powered Industrial Motors:** Advanced motors designed to operate on DC power, reducing conversion losses and increasing efficiency in industrial applications where high power demands are common.
