1. **DC Microgrid Control System:**
   A centralized or distributed control system that optimizes the operation and efficiency of a DC microgrid, ensuring reliable power delivery across ports, industries, data centers, and buildings.

2. **Energy Management Software for DC Systems:**
   Software tools designed to monitor, analyze, and optimize energy usage in real-time within DC networks, enhancing energy savings and operational efficiency.

3. **Modular DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs):**
   Scalable power distribution units that allow flexible integration and management of various DC-powered devices and systems, suitable for environments like data centers and industrial facilities.

4. **DC Charging Infrastructure:**
   Advanced charging stations specifically designed to efficiently charge electric vehicles or equipment using DC power, reducing conversion losses and improving energy efficiency in ports and buildings.

5. **Thermal Management Solutions for DC Systems:**
   Innovative cooling technologies that manage the heat generated by high-efficiency DC components, ensuring optimal performance and longevity of systems in industries and data centers.
