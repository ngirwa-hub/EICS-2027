1. **DC Microgrid Integration for Ports:** Systems that integrate various renewable energy sources and loads within ports using direct current, enhancing energy efficiency by reducing conversion losses.

2. **Industrial Process Optimization Using DC Power:** Implementing DC power directly into industrial machinery can improve process efficiency by eliminating the need for AC to DC conversion in many applications.

3. **DC Cooling Solutions for Data Centers:** Advanced cooling systems that use DC power to operate more efficiently, reducing energy consumption and improving the sustainability of data centers.

4. **Residential DC Power Units for Buildings:** Compact units installed in homes that allow direct connection of appliances to a DC grid, lowering conversion losses and increasing overall efficiency.

5. **Hybrid Renewable Energy Systems with DC Integration:** Combining solar panels, wind turbines, and other renewable sources directly into a DC network to optimize energy production and distribution without AC conversions.
