1. **DC Microgrid Controllers:** Devices that manage power distribution within a DC microgrid in ports and industrial settings, enhancing efficiency and reliability by optimizing energy flow between various sources and loads.

2. **DC-AC Hybrid Inverters:** Systems designed for data centers and buildings that can seamlessly convert DC to AC when needed while maximizing the use of available DC energy from renewable sources or storage systems.

3. **Energy Management Software for DC Systems:** A software platform that monitors, controls, and optimizes the performance of DC networks in industries and data centers, improving energy efficiency and reducing operational costs.

4. **DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Stations:** Charging stations designed to provide direct current power to electric vehicles at ports and buildings, increasing charging speed and compatibility with renewable energy sources.

5. **Thermal Energy Storage for DC Systems:** Solutions that integrate thermal storage into DC networks in industries or data centers to balance energy loads, improve efficiency, and utilize excess renewable energy effectively by storing it as heat.
