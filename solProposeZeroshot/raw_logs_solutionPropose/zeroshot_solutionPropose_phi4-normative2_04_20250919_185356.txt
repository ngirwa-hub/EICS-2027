1. **DC Microgrid Infrastructure for Ports**: A dedicated microgrid system utilizing DC power for operational efficiency and reduced emissions in port operations, enhancing sustainability.

2. **Industrial Process Integration via DC Power**: Direct integration of industrial machinery with DC power sources to minimize conversion losses and optimize energy efficiency in production processes.

3. **Data Center DC Optimization Module**: A module designed to manage and streamline DC power usage within data centers, reducing AC-DC conversions for improved energy efficiency.

4. **DC-Powered Smart Building Automation Systems**: Utilization of DC-powered sensors and controllers in buildings to enhance automation systems' energy efficiency and reduce reliance on conventional AC grids.

5. **DC Renewable Energy Source Aggregation Hub**: A centralized hub that aggregates various renewable DC sources (e.g., solar panels, wind turbines) for efficient distribution within an industrial or commercial setting, minimizing conversion losses.
