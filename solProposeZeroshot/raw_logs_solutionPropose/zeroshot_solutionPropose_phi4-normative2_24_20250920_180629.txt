1. **DC Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles:** Implementation of widespread DC charging stations specifically designed for electric vehicles in ports and industrial areas to facilitate faster charging times, reduce grid load during peak hours, and promote the use of clean transportation.

2. **Integrated Renewable Energy Management System (I-REMS):** A system that optimizes energy usage by directly integrating solar panels, wind turbines, and battery storage with DC microgrids in buildings and data centers, maximizing efficiency and sustainability.

3. **DC-based Microgrid Control Systems:** Advanced control systems for managing local energy generation and consumption within a DC microgrid framework, enhancing resilience and reliability especially in industrial zones with high energy demands.

4. **DC Power Distribution Optimization Algorithms:** Software tools that optimize the layout and operation of DC power distribution networks in buildings and data centers, ensuring minimal losses and maximum efficiency across all connected loads.

5. **Smart DC Load Balancing Devices:** Innovative devices designed to automatically balance power demand within a DC network in industrial settings, reducing strain on equipment and preventing potential downtime due to overloads.
