1. **DC Distribution Networks for Ports**: Develop dedicated DC distribution networks within ports to directly supply power to electric ships and port machinery, reducing reliance on AC systems and minimizing conversion losses.

2. **Industrial Process Integration with DC Power**: Implement systems that allow industrial processes to be powered directly by DC sources, optimizing energy efficiency in manufacturing operations by eliminating unnecessary conversions from AC to DC.

3. **Data Center Heat Recovery Systems**: Design systems that capture waste heat generated by data centers' DC power supplies and convert it into usable thermal energy for heating or cooling purposes within the facility, enhancing overall energy sustainability.

4. **DC Power Management Systems for Buildings**: Integrate advanced DC power management systems in buildings to optimize the distribution of solar-generated DC power across various uses like lighting, HVAC systems, and appliances, improving efficiency.

5. **Modular DC Microgrids for Urban Areas**: Establish modular DC microgrid solutions that connect multiple urban buildings or infrastructure components directly with renewable DC energy sources, fostering greater energy independence and resilience in urban environments.
