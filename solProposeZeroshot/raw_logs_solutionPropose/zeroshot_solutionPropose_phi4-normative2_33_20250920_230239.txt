1. **DC-Powered Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure**: Implement charging stations for electric vehicles that use direct current (DC) power at ports and industrial areas to enable faster and more efficient charging processes, thereby reducing downtime and emissions.

2. **Integrated DC Microgrid Management System**: Develop a centralized management system for DC microgrids within buildings and data centers to optimize energy distribution, enhance reliability, and seamlessly integrate renewable sources like solar panels directly into the DC network.

3. **DC Solar Panel Integration Kit**: Create solutions specifically designed for integrating solar panels with existing DC infrastructure in industries and buildings, allowing direct harnessing of solar power without conversion losses, thus increasing overall efficiency and sustainability.

4. **Thermal Management System for DC Systems**: Develop advanced thermal management solutions for data centers utilizing DC technologies, focusing on efficient cooling methods that handle the specific heat profiles generated by high-density DC equipment and reduce energy consumption related to air conditioning.

5. **DC-Powered IoT Sensor Networks**: Design sensor networks powered directly by DC in ports and industrial settings for monitoring environmental conditions, machinery performance, or logistics efficiency, reducing the need for AC-DC conversion and enhancing operational sustainability.
