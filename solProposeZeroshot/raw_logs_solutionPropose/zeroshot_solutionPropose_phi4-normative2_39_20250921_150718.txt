1. **DC Microgrid Management System**: A comprehensive control system designed for optimizing the operation and efficiency of DC microgrids within ports, industries, data centers, and buildings. It integrates renewable energy sources, storage systems, and loads, ensuring reliable and efficient power distribution.

2. **Industrial Process Heat Integration**: Utilizes direct current to directly drive industrial heating processes, improving energy efficiency by reducing conversion losses compared to AC-driven methods. This can significantly lower operational costs in industries reliant on process heat.

3. **Data Center DC Cooling System**: A cooling solution tailored for data centers that leverage the thermal properties of DC power distribution systems to enhance cooling efficiency and reduce energy consumption. This system is crucial for maintaining optimal operating temperatures with minimal environmental impact.

4. **Building-Integrated Photovoltaic (BIPV) DC Systems**: An innovative integration of photovoltaics within building structures, designed to generate and distribute direct current at the point of use. This reduces transmission losses and enhances the sustainability of buildings by maximizing on-site energy production.

5. **DC-Powered Smart Grid Interface**: A technology that enables seamless interaction between DC microgrids and traditional AC grids, facilitating efficient energy exchange and enhancing grid stability. It plays a crucial role in enabling a hybrid grid environment where both DC and AC systems coexist effectively.
