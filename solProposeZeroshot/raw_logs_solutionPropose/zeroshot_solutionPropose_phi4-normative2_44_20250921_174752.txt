1. **DC Microgrid Management System**: A centralized platform that efficiently manages power distribution and load balancing across various DC microgrids within ports, industries, data centers, and buildings.

2. **Hybrid AC-DC Power Inverters**: Devices that can seamlessly convert between alternating current (AC) and direct current (DC), optimizing energy use in environments where both types of systems are present, particularly useful in industrial settings.

3. **Portable DC Energy Stations**: Mobile units designed to provide temporary DC power solutions for emergency situations or rapid deployment needs within ports and data centers, ensuring minimal downtime during maintenance or unforeseen outages.

4. **DC Power Quality Analyzer**: A tool that monitors the quality of DC power supply, detecting anomalies and improving reliability in critical infrastructure like data centers where consistent power is crucial.

5. **Building Integrated Photovoltaic-DC Systems**: Solar panels integrated directly into building materials (e.g., windows or facades) designed to generate and supply DC power on-site, reducing dependence on external AC grids and enhancing sustainability.
