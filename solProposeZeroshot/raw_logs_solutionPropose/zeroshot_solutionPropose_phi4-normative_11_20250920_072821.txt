1. **DC Microgrid Management System**: An integrated platform for managing and optimizing the distribution and utilization of DC power within microgrids at ports and industrial sites, ensuring efficient energy use and stability.

2. **DC Power Quality Monitoring**: A solution that continuously monitors the quality of DC power in data centers and buildings to prevent disruptions and maintain optimal performance by detecting anomalies such as voltage fluctuations or harmonics.

3. **Energy-efficient DC Lighting System**: An advanced lighting system designed for industrial environments and large building complexes, utilizing DC power directly to reduce energy losses associated with AC-DC conversions typical in conventional lighting systems.

4. **DC Heating Solution for Industrial Processes**: A technology that provides efficient heating solutions by using direct current, reducing energy consumption and emissions in industries where thermal processes are critical.

5. **Integrated DC Renewable Energy Management System**: A system specifically designed to manage the integration of renewable energy sources like solar panels directly into DC grids in ports, data centers, and buildings, optimizing for energy efficiency and sustainability.
