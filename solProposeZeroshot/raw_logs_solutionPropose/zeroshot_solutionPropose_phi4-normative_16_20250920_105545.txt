4. **DC Lighting Retrofit Kits:** Kits enabling the transition of existing lighting infrastructure to direct current systems, particularly useful in industrial settings and buildings to reduce energy consumption and increase reliability.

5. **Modular DC Power Supply for Industry Applications:** A flexible and scalable power supply system designed for industrial use that allows easy adaptation to varying power needs and supports high-efficiency DC operations.
