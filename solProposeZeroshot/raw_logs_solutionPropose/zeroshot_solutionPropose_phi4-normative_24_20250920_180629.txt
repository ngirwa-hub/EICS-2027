1. **DC-Powered Automated Container Handling**: Implement automated systems for handling containers in ports using direct current power, reducing energy consumption and emissions compared to traditional AC-powered systems.

2. **Industrially Integrated DC Microgrids**: Develop microgrid solutions that enable industrial facilities to efficiently distribute DC power across various processes, minimizing conversion losses and enhancing energy management capabilities.

3. **DC-Powered Data Center Cooling Systems**: Introduce cooling technologies for data centers powered directly by DC electricity, improving efficiency and reducing the need for AC-DC conversions in thermal management systems.

4. **Residential DC Appliance Compatibility**: Promote the development of residential appliances compatible with DC power to enhance energy efficiency within buildings, aligning with renewable energy integration.

5. **DC Charging Infrastructure for Ports and Industries**: Establish comprehensive charging stations and infrastructure supporting DC fast charging for electric vehicles and machinery used in ports and industrial settings, facilitating a smoother transition to electrification.
