1. **DC-Powered Electric Vehicle Chargers:** Infrastructure for charging electric vehicles directly with DC power, reducing conversion losses and enhancing efficiency at ports and industrial sites.

2. **Integrated Energy Management System (IEMS):** A platform that optimizes the use of renewable energy sources by managing DC power flows across data centers and buildings, improving overall energy efficiency.

3. **DC Microgrid for Industrial Processes:** A localized grid system powered by DC to supply stable and efficient power directly to industrial machinery, minimizing energy conversion losses and enhancing process reliability.

4. **Energy Recovery Systems for Data Centers:** Technologies that capture waste heat from data center operations and convert it into usable DC power, thus improving sustainability and reducing cooling costs.

5. **DC-Powered Lighting Solutions for Buildings:** Advanced LED lighting systems designed for direct use with DC power, offering improved energy efficiency and reduced infrastructure complexity in residential and commercial buildings.
