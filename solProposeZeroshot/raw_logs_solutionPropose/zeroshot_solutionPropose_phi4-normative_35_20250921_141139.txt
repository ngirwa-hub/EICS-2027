1. **DC Charging Infrastructure for Ports**: A comprehensive network of charging stations specifically designed for maritime vessels and port vehicles that operate on direct current power, reducing reliance on fossil fuels and improving air quality at ports.

2. **Industrial Process Integration System**: An advanced system that integrates DC power directly into industrial processes to minimize energy conversion losses and optimize production efficiency in sectors like manufacturing and heavy industry.

3. **Data Center Microgrid with DC Power**: A localized microgrid solution for data centers utilizing DC power to enhance energy efficiency, reduce operational costs, and improve reliability by minimizing AC/DC conversions and leveraging renewable sources directly.

4. **Building-Level DC Integration Framework**: An architectural framework that facilitates the seamless integration of DC systems in buildings, promoting efficient lighting, HVAC systems, and appliances operating on DC to lower energy consumption and simplify grid interactions.

5. **Smart Grid Interface for DC Networks**: A technology platform enabling communication between existing AC grids and emerging DC networks, ensuring smooth operation, scalability, and optimization of mixed-voltage environments in urban infrastructure.
