1. **DC Microgrid Management System**: A comprehensive platform that manages and optimizes the distribution and consumption of DC power within ports, industries, data centers, and buildings to enhance energy efficiency and reliability.

2. **Integrated Renewable Energy Sources**: Systems that directly incorporate solar panels or wind turbines with DC infrastructure in industrial settings to maximize renewable energy use without AC conversion losses.

3. **DC-Powered HVAC Systems**: Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems designed to operate on direct current, reducing conversion losses and increasing efficiency in data centers and commercial buildings.

4. **DC Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles (EVs)**: Development of fast-charging stations that provide DC power directly to electric vehicles, minimizing energy loss during charging processes at ports and industrial sites.

5. **Demand Response Management Tool**: A system designed to adjust the load consumption of DC-powered sectors such as data centers in real-time based on grid demand, thereby enhancing grid stability and optimizing energy usage.
