1. **DC-Powered Electrification Stations**: Facilities equipped with direct current charging points for electric vehicles and machinery, optimizing energy efficiency by eliminating AC-DC conversion losses during the charging process.

2. **Industrial Process Integration via DC Bus**: A centralized DC bus system that connects various industrial machines and processes directly to a common DC supply, enhancing energy distribution efficiency and reducing complexity in power management systems.

3. **Data Center Thermal Management Using DC Systems**: Implementing cooling solutions powered by DC that interface seamlessly with data center infrastructure, reducing conversion losses and improving overall thermal efficiency.

4. **DC Microgrid for Building Energy Management**: A localized DC microgrid solution designed to integrate renewable energy sources directly into building operations, facilitating efficient energy use and storage while minimizing reliance on the AC grid.

5. **Modular DC Power Distribution Systems**: Scalable DC power distribution frameworks that can be easily integrated into existing infrastructure in ports, industries, data centers, and buildings, promoting flexibility and reducing installation costs.
