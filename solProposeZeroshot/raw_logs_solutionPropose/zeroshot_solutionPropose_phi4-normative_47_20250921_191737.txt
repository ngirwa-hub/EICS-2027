1. **DC-Powered Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs):** Utilizing direct current for powering AGVs in ports and industrial settings reduces reliance on AC conversion systems, enhancing efficiency and reducing maintenance needs.

2. **Integrated DC Microgrid Systems:** Implementing microgrids that operate entirely on DC power within data centers and buildings can minimize energy losses associated with multiple AC-DC conversions, leading to improved energy efficiency and reliability.

3. **DC Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles (EVs):** Establishing direct current charging stations in ports and industrial areas supports the growing fleet of electric vehicles by providing faster and more efficient charging solutions.

4. **DC-Powered Smart Building Systems:** Integrating DC systems into building management, including lighting, heating, and cooling, to optimize energy use and reduce conversion losses, enhancing overall sustainability.

5. **DC-Based Renewable Energy Integration:** Facilitating seamless integration of renewable energy sources like solar panels directly into DC networks within industries and data centers, bypassing AC conversion steps for improved efficiency and reduced carbon footprint.
