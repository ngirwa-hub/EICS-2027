1. **DC Microgrid Control System**: A centralized management system for coordinating DC power distribution and optimizing energy flows in microgrids, crucial for enhancing efficiency in data centers and industrial facilities.

2. **DC Power Quality Monitoring Unit**: A device that continuously monitors the quality of DC power supply to ensure stable operation of sensitive equipment in buildings and data centers, reducing downtime due to power disturbances.

3. **Hybrid AC/DC Distribution Network**: An integrated energy distribution system combining both AC and DC networks to leverage the strengths of each technology, particularly beneficial for ports with diverse energy demands from different sectors.

4. **Energy Management System (DC-focused)**: A platform designed specifically for managing and optimizing energy use in DC grids across industries, enhancing energy savings and reducing operational costs through intelligent control strategies.

5. **DC Load Forecasting Tool**: An analytical tool that predicts future electricity demand on a DC network within industrial settings or ports, allowing better planning of energy resources and minimizing peak load impacts.
