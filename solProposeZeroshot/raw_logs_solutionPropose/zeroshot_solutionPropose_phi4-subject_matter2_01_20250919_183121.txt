1. **DC Microgrid Integration Platform:**
   A centralized control and management system designed for integrating and optimizing the operation of multiple DC microgrids in ports, industry facilities, data centers, and buildings.

2. **Modular DC Power Distribution Unit (PDU):** 
   An adaptable power distribution unit that facilitates efficient DC power delivery within industrial sites, data centers, and modern building infrastructures, allowing for easy scalability and maintenance.

3. **DC-Powered Electric Vehicle Charging Stations:**
   Advanced charging solutions specifically designed to use direct current for rapid and efficient electric vehicle charging in ports and industrial complexes.

4. **Renewable Energy Source DC Interface Module:**
   A module that allows seamless integration of renewable energy sources like solar panels or wind turbines directly into the DC grid, minimizing conversion losses and improving overall efficiency at data centers and buildings.

5. **DC Power Quality Monitoring System:** 
   An advanced monitoring system designed to ensure stable and high-quality power delivery in critical infrastructure environments such as data centers and industrial plants by tracking performance metrics and identifying anomalies in real time.
