1. **DC Microgrid Controller**: A centralized or distributed controller for managing the operations and energy flow within a DC microgrid, ensuring efficient distribution and stability across ports, industrial sites, data centers, and buildings.

2. **Renewable Energy Integration Module (REIM)**: A platform facilitating seamless integration of renewable energy sources like solar panels and wind turbines into existing DC grids, optimizing power usage in industries and enhancing sustainability in buildings and data centers.

3. **DC Power Quality Enhancer**: An innovative solution designed to improve the quality of DC power by reducing voltage fluctuations and harmonics, crucial for sensitive equipment in data centers and industrial applications.

4. **Modular DC Energy Storage System (MDESS)**: A scalable energy storage system that provides flexible capacity options for various applications, supporting load balancing and peak shaving across ports, industries, and buildings with high energy demands.

5. **DC Infrastructure Monitoring and Diagnostics Tool**: A comprehensive tool that offers real-time monitoring and diagnostic capabilities for DC infrastructure health, vital for maintaining reliability in critical facilities like data centers and industrial environments.
