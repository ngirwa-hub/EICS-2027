1. **DC Microgrid Management System**: A centralized control platform for managing and optimizing the distribution of DC power across microgrids in ports, industries, data centers, and buildings, ensuring efficient energy use and grid stability.

2. **Hybrid AC-DC Power Distribution Network**: An integrated system that combines AC and DC networks to leverage the benefits of both, such as reduced conversion losses and improved compatibility with existing infrastructure, particularly useful in industrial settings.

3. **Renewable Energy Integration Interface**: A solution designed to facilitate seamless integration of renewable energy sources like solar panels directly into DC grids within buildings and data centers, maximizing efficiency by minimizing AC-DC conversions.

4. **DC Charging Infrastructure for Electric Vehicles (EVs)**: An infrastructure setup that provides fast and efficient DC charging stations at ports and industrial facilities, supporting the growing adoption of electric vehicles by reducing charging times through direct current supply.

5. **DC Power Quality Monitoring System**: A monitoring solution focused on maintaining high power quality in DC systems across various sectors, utilizing advanced sensors and analytics to detect and mitigate issues such as voltage sags or harmonics.
