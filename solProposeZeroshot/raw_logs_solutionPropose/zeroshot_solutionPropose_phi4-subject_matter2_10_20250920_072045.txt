1. **DC Microgrid Integration:** Implementation of localized DC microgrids within ports and industrial sites to optimize energy use from renewable sources without AC conversion losses.

2. **DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Stations:** Deployment of fast-charging stations for electric vehicles using high-voltage DC systems, enhancing efficiency and reducing charging times in commercial areas like ports and data centers.

3. **DC-Enabled Smart Lighting Systems:** Advanced lighting solutions that operate directly on low-voltage DC to improve energy efficiency in buildings and industrial facilities by eliminating AC/DC conversion stages.

4. **Industrial Process Equipment Direct Drive:** Integration of direct-drive motors and equipment in industry sectors powered directly by DC, reducing mechanical losses associated with traditional AC systems.

5. **Data Center Power Redundancy Systems:** Implementation of redundant power architectures using DC to ensure uninterrupted operation and increased resilience against power failures in data centers.
