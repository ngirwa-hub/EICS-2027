1. **DC Microgrid Control System**: A centralized or decentralized control system for managing energy flow within a DC microgrid, ensuring optimal performance and stability across various loads in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings.

2. **DC Power Optimizer Modules**: Devices that enhance the efficiency of renewable energy sources by optimizing power output to match the load requirements directly on a DC basis, reducing conversion losses in industrial settings and data centers.

3. **Thermal Management System for DC Circuits**: A solution focused on efficiently dissipating heat generated by high-power DC systems, crucial for maintaining system reliability and performance in densely packed environments like data centers and large industrial facilities.

4. **DC Distribution Network Standards Compliance Tool**: Software or toolset designed to ensure that DC distribution networks in buildings and ports adhere to emerging industry standards and regulations, facilitating seamless integration and interoperability of various DC technologies.

5. **Hybrid AC/DC Transformer**: A transformer designed for hybrid energy systems that can handle both AC and DC inputs, providing flexibility in transitioning existing infrastructures like those found in ports and industrial facilities towards more efficient DC-based operations.
