1. **DC Microgrid Integration for Ports**: This solution involves developing integrated DC microgrids within port areas to directly power cargo handling equipment and facilities, enhancing energy efficiency by reducing AC-DC conversion losses.

2. **Industry-Specific DC Power Modules**: Customized DC power modules designed specifically for industrial applications that require high reliability and low maintenance, such as heavy machinery or manufacturing plants, optimizing energy use and minimizing downtime.

3. **Data Center DC Redundancy Systems**: Advanced redundancy systems for data centers using dual DC supply paths to ensure continuous operation with enhanced fault tolerance and reduced reliance on traditional AC backup power sources.

4. **DC Load Balancing Solutions for Buildings**: Innovative load balancing solutions that optimize the distribution of DC power within buildings, facilitating efficient energy management and integration of renewable energy sources like solar panels directly into the building's electrical system.

5. **Hybrid Renewable Energy Storage Systems (HRESS)**: Hybrid systems combining multiple renewable energy storage technologies to provide stable and reliable DC power supply for critical infrastructure in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, improving resilience against grid fluctuations.
