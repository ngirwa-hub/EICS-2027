1. **DC Microgrid Management System: Advanced software platform**  
   This system optimizes and manages the distribution of power within a DC microgrid, ensuring efficient energy use across ports, industrial sites, data centers, and buildings.

2. **Integrated Renewable Energy Interface: Seamless integration module**  
   Facilitates direct connection and synchronization of renewable energy sources like solar panels and wind turbines with existing DC infrastructure in various sectors.

3. **DC Smart Inverters for PV Systems: Optimized conversion technology**  
   Enhances the efficiency of photovoltaic systems by converting generated power directly to DC, reducing losses associated with AC-DC conversions.

4. **DC Power Quality Monitoring System: Comprehensive analysis tool**  
   Monitors and maintains high-quality DC power delivery in critical sectors like data centers, helping prevent equipment damage and improve reliability.

5. **Modular DC Energy Storage Solutions: Scalable storage units**  
   Offers flexible, scalable energy storage options tailored for use in ports, industry, and buildings, ensuring reliable power supply during peak demands or outages.
