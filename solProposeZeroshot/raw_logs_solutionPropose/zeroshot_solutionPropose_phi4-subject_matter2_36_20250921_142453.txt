4. **Renewable Energy Integration Platform:** A platform facilitating the direct connection of renewable energy sources (solar panels, wind turbines) to DC grids, minimizing conversion losses and enhancing sustainability across sectors.

5. **DC Energy Management Software:** Advanced software tools for optimizing energy consumption and distribution in DC systems, particularly useful for managing complex energy needs in data centers and industrial settings.
