1. **DC Microgrid Control System**: A centralized or decentralized control system that manages the operation and integration of various DC energy sources, loads, and storage units within microgrids at ports, industries, data centers, and buildings.

2. **DC Solar Panel Interface Module**: An interface module designed to optimize the connection between solar panels and DC grids, allowing for efficient conversion and management of solar power without intermediate AC conversion.

3. **Hybrid Energy Management System (HEMS)**: A system that seamlessly manages multiple energy sources like solar, wind, and storage within DC networks in data centers and industrial settings, optimizing efficiency and reliability.

4. **DC-AC-DC Power Conversion Unit**: A unit designed to convert AC power from traditional grids directly into DC for integration with existing DC infrastructure, minimizing conversion losses and facilitating the transition to DC technologies in buildings.

5. **DC Resilience Monitoring System**: An advanced monitoring system that assesses and enhances the resilience of DC networks against faults and disruptions, particularly crucial for critical infrastructure like ports and data centers.
