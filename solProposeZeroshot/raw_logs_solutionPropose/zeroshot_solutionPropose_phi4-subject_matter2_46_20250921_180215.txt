1. **DC Microgrid Management System:**
   A control system designed for managing microgrids that operate on DC power, optimizing energy distribution and enhancing resilience in ports, industries, data centers, and buildings.

2. **Hybrid Renewable Energy Systems (HRES) for DC Grids:**
   Integration of multiple renewable sources like solar panels and wind turbines with a unified DC grid to maximize efficiency and reliability across various sectors.

3. **DC Power Quality Monitoring System:**
   A tool that continuously assesses the quality of DC power, ensuring stable voltage levels and minimizing disturbances in industrial settings and data centers.

4. **Integrated DC Energy Storage Solutions:**
   Systems combining batteries or other storage technologies with DC grids to provide backup power and improve energy management in buildings and data centers.

5. **DC Distribution Automation System:**
   A system that automates the distribution of DC power, enhancing efficiency by dynamically adjusting loads and sources across industrial sites and large commercial buildings.
