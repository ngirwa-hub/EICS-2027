1. **DC Microgrid Management System**: A centralized control system designed for managing and optimizing the performance of DC microgrids within ports, industries, data centers, and buildings, enhancing energy efficiency and reliability.

2. **DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Stations**: Infrastructure to charge electric vehicles using direct current at ports and industrial sites, reducing conversion losses and improving charging speed and efficiency.

3. **Integrated Renewable Energy Management Platform**: A software platform that seamlessly integrates solar panels, wind turbines, and other renewable sources with DC systems in buildings and data centers, optimizing energy utilization and storage.

4. **DC Heat Pump Systems**: Utilizing direct current to power heat pumps for efficient heating and cooling in industrial facilities and large-scale buildings, reducing energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions.

5. **DC Power Quality Monitoring System**: A real-time monitoring solution that ensures the quality of DC power across systems in ports, industries, data centers, and buildings by detecting fluctuations and anomalies, thus ensuring stable operations.
