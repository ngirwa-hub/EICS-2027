1. **DC Microgrid Management System**: An integrated platform for managing and optimizing the operation of DC microgrids in ports and industrial settings, enhancing energy efficiency and reliability.

2. **DC-Powered HVAC Systems**: Advanced heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems powered by DC to reduce conversion losses and improve energy efficiency in buildings and data centers.

3. **DC Renewable Energy Integration Platform**: A solution designed for seamless integration of renewable energy sources like solar panels directly into the DC grid, minimizing conversion steps and maximizing utilization efficiency.

4. **DC Power Quality Monitoring System**: A system that ensures the stability and quality of DC power supplied to critical infrastructure such as data centers, preventing disruptions due to voltage fluctuations or harmonics.

5. **Modular DC Energy Storage Units**: Scalable battery storage systems designed for easy integration with existing DC infrastructures in buildings and industries, providing backup power and enhancing grid flexibility.
