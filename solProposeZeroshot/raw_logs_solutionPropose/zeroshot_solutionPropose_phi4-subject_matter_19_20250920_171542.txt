1. **DC Port Power Distribution System**: An efficient power distribution network tailored for ports that can directly supply DC power from renewable sources like solar or wind to ships and port facilities, reducing conversion losses.

2. **Industrial Process Optimization with DC Drives**: Implementing variable frequency drives in industrial processes that operate on DC power, enhancing energy efficiency by optimizing motor speeds according to real-time load demands.

3. **Data Center Direct Cooling Using DC Systems**: Utilizing DC power for direct cooling systems within data centers that bypass AC conversion inefficiencies, improving overall energy efficiency and reducing thermal management costs.

4. **DC Smart Building Management System**: A comprehensive building management system designed to operate on DC power, integrating renewable sources and optimizing energy use through intelligent control of lighting, HVAC, and appliances.

5. **Hybrid Energy Storage Systems for DC Grids**: Integrating multiple types of energy storage technologies (such as batteries and supercapacitors) directly into DC grids to enhance reliability, support peak demand, and improve integration with intermittent renewable sources.
