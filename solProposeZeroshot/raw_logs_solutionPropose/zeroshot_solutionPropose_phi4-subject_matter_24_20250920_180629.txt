1. **DC Microgrid Architectural Framework**: A comprehensive guideline for designing and implementing DC microgrids tailored specifically for ports, industrial sites, data centers, and buildings. This framework addresses integration challenges, enhancing operational efficiency by optimizing power distribution and minimizing conversion losses.

2. **DC Power Management System**: An advanced control system that efficiently manages the flow of direct current within various sectors such as ports and data centers. It ensures optimal load balancing, reduces energy waste, and enhances reliability through real-time monitoring and adaptive response mechanisms.

3. **Thermal Management Solutions for DC Systems**: Techniques and technologies designed to manage heat generated by high-power DC systems in industrial settings and data centers. These solutions include advanced cooling strategies and materials that improve thermal efficiency and system longevity, reducing downtime and maintenance costs.

4. **DC-AC Hybrid Power Conversion Units**: Innovative units capable of seamlessly switching between direct current and alternating current outputs based on demand and infrastructure compatibility. This flexibility is crucial for industries and buildings transitioning to DC while maintaining interoperability with existing AC systems.

5. **Resilient DC Infrastructure Materials**: Development of advanced materials that enhance the durability, safety, and performance of DC power infrastructures in harsh environments like ports. These materials offer improved insulation, resistance to corrosion, and support high-voltage applications, facilitating more robust and reliable energy solutions.
