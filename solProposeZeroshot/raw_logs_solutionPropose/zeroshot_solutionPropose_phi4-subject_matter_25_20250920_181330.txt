2. **DC-Powered HVAC Systems**: Innovative heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems designed to operate on DC power directly from solar panels or other renewable sources, reducing conversion losses and improving energy use in buildings and data centers.

3. **Integrated DC Lighting Solutions**: Advanced LED lighting systems that utilize direct current, eliminating the need for AC-DC conversion, leading to improved efficiency and reduced operational costs in industrial facilities and office buildings.

4. **DC-Powered Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure**: Specialized infrastructure for charging electric vehicles using DC power directly from renewable sources or local grids, particularly useful for ports where high energy demand can be met sustainably.

5. **DC-Based Cooling Systems**: Advanced cooling technologies for data centers that operate on direct current to enhance energy efficiency and reduce dependency on traditional AC-powered systems, leveraging innovations in thermoelectric and absorption refrigeration.
