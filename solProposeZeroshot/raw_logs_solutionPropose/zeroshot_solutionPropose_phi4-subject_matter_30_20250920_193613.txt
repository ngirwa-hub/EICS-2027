1. **DC Microgrid Management System**: A centralized control platform for managing and optimizing DC microgrids in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, enhancing energy efficiency and reliability.

2. **Renewable Energy Integration Interface**: An interface designed to seamlessly connect renewable energy sources such as solar panels and wind turbines directly into DC grids, minimizing conversion losses.

3. **DC-to-AC Hybrid Inverter**: A versatile inverter that allows for efficient conversion between DC and AC power, enabling compatibility with existing AC infrastructure while maximizing the use of DC systems.

4. **DC Power Quality Monitoring System**: Advanced monitoring tools to ensure consistent power quality within DC grids, crucial for sensitive applications like data centers and industrial processes.

5. **Modular DC Battery Storage Units**: Scalable battery units designed for easy integration into existing DC infrastructures in ports, industry, data centers, and buildings, supporting energy storage and grid stability.
