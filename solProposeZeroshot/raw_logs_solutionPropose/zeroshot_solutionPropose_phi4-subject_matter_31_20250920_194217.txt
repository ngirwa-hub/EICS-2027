1. **DC Microgrid Management System**: A centralized control system for managing DC microgrids in ports and data centers, optimizing power distribution and enhancing reliability by integrating renewable energy sources and storage systems.

2. **DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Stations**: Fast-charging stations designed specifically for DC-powered EVs, reducing charging times and improving efficiency in industrial and port settings where electric vehicle use is prevalent.

3. **Inductive Power Transfer Systems**: Wireless power transfer technology using high-frequency AC converted to DC, ideal for industrial automation equipment and building infrastructure to reduce wiring complexity and increase flexibility.

4. **DC Renewable Energy Forecasting Tool**: An advanced analytics platform that predicts renewable energy output (solar, wind) in DC format, aiding industries and data centers in planning and optimizing their energy usage and storage needs.

5. **Energy Management Software for DC Systems**: Customized software solutions that monitor, control, and optimize the performance of DC power systems across buildings and industrial plants, enhancing efficiency and reducing operational costs through real-time data analysis.
