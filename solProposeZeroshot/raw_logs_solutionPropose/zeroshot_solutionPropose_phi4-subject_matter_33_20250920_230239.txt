1. **DC Microgrid Management System**: A centralized control system for managing and optimizing the distribution and utilization of DC power within microgrids in ports, industries, data centers, and buildings.

2. **DC Power Quality Monitoring Unit**: An advanced monitoring tool designed to ensure consistent voltage levels and current quality in DC networks, crucial for sensitive applications like those found in data centers.

3. **Hybrid AC/DC Distribution Network**: A network infrastructure that seamlessly integrates both AC and DC power distribution, allowing flexibility and efficiency improvements across various sectors such as ports and industrial facilities.

4. **DC Renewable Energy Integration Platform**: A platform facilitating the direct integration of renewable energy sources (like solar PV panels) into existing DC grids without intermediate conversion to AC, optimizing efficiency in data centers and buildings.

5. **Energy Storage Management System for DC Grids**: An intelligent management system designed to optimize the operation of energy storage systems within DC power networks, enhancing reliability and energy availability in industries and ports.
