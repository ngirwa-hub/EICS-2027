1. **DC Microgrid Management System**: A centralized platform for managing DC microgrids in ports and industrial settings, optimizing power distribution, enhancing fault detection, and improving overall system efficiency through real-time monitoring.

2. **DC-Powered Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Stations**: Infrastructure specifically designed to charge electric vehicles using DC power directly, reducing conversion losses compared to AC charging stations and accelerating the adoption of EVs in urban areas, including ports and industrial complexes.

3. **Integrated Renewable Energy Management System**: A solution for data centers and buildings that integrates solar panels or wind turbines with DC systems, optimizing energy use and storage while minimizing reliance on external power sources through intelligent management algorithms.

4. **DC-Powered HVAC Systems**: Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems designed to operate directly on DC power, reducing conversion losses and enhancing the efficiency of climate control solutions in data centers and large buildings.

5. **Wireless Power Transfer for DC Applications**: An innovative approach that enables wireless transmission of power at low-voltage levels suitable for portable devices or sensor networks within industrial environments or smart buildings, improving flexibility and reducing wiring complexity.
