1. **DC Microgrid Management System**: A centralized control system for coordinating and optimizing the operation of DC microgrids in ports and industries, enhancing energy efficiency and reliability.

2. **Thermal Energy Storage (TES) Integration with DC Systems**: Utilizes surplus thermal energy from industrial processes or solar heat to store energy as heat, which can be converted back into electricity via thermoelectric generators connected to a DC grid, reducing peak demand stress on the system.

3. **DC Power Quality Monitoring System**: A real-time monitoring solution for ensuring consistent voltage and current levels in DC grids within data centers, minimizing power quality issues that could affect sensitive electronic equipment.

4. **Renewable Energy Forecasting Tool for DC Systems**: An AI-driven tool to predict renewable energy generation (e.g., solar, wind) more accurately for DC networks in buildings, facilitating better integration and reducing reliance on backup AC systems.

5. **DC-based Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure**: A fast-charging station network specifically designed for direct current compatibility with electric vehicles, improving charging speed and efficiency at ports and urban areas.
