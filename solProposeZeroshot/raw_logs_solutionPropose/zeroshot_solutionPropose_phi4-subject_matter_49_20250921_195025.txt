1. **DC Microgrid Management System:**
   A centralized platform for monitoring and controlling DC microgrids in ports, industries, data centers, and buildings, optimizing energy distribution and enhancing system reliability through real-time analytics.

2. **DC Solar Inverter Optimization:**
   Advanced inverters designed specifically for DC integration that maximize the efficiency of solar panels by directly converting sunlight to DC power, reducing conversion losses and improving overall renewable energy utilization.

3. **Hybrid AC/DC Power Architecture:**
   A system architecture that seamlessly integrates both AC and DC components within industrial facilities and data centers, allowing flexible operation modes and optimizing energy usage based on real-time demands.

4. **DC Power Distribution Units (PDUs):**
   Specialized PDUs for managing DC power distribution in buildings and data centers, enhancing energy efficiency by minimizing conversion steps and enabling direct connection to DC-powered devices.

5. **Advanced Insulation Materials:**
   Development of high-performance insulation materials specifically designed for DC cables, addressing challenges like dielectric breakdown and extending the lifespan of infrastructure in harsh environments such as ports.
